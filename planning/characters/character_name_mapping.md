## 1. Primary Characters

### Romeo → Роман Владимирович Монтягин

**Full Name**: Роман Владимирович Монтягин (Roman Vladimirovich Montyagin)
**Pronunciation**: ro-MÁN vla-DÍ-mi-ro-vich mon-TYÁ-gin
**Stress Patterns**:
- Ромáн (ro-MÁN) - 2 syllables, stress on 2nd
- Владимирович (vla-DÍ-mi-ro-vich) - 5 syllables, stress on 2nd
- Монтягин (mon-TYÁ-gin) - 3 syllables, stress on 2nd

**Nickname/Informal**: Рóма (Róma), Ромáша (Romásha)
**Age**: 18-19 years old
**Family Position**: Only son (or eldest son)

**Name Rationale**:
- "Роман" is direct Russian equivalent of "Romeo/Roman"
- Владимирович (son of Vladimir) - strong, princely patronymic
- Монтягин invented surname based on Italian "Montague"
- Easy to use in verse (multiple stress patterns available)

**Character Essence**: Melancholic youth, poetic, passionate, ultimately tragic hero

---

### Juliet → Юлия Григорьевна Капулетова

**Full Name**: Юлия Григорьевна Капулетова (Yulia Grigoryevna Kapuletova)
**Pronunciation**: YÚ-li-ya gri-GÓR-yev-na ka-pu-LÉ-to-va
**Stress Patterns**:
- Юлия (YÚ-li-ya) - 3 syllables, stress on 1st
- Григорьевна (gri-GÓR-yev-na) - 4 syllables, stress on 2nd
- Капулетова (ka-pu-LÉ-to-va) - 5 syllables, stress on 3rd

**Nickname/Informal**: Юля (Yúlya), Юленька (Yúlenka)
**Age**: 13-14 years old (maintaining Shakespeare's age)
**Family Position**: Only daughter

**Name Rationale**:
- "Юлия" is standard Russian form of Julia/Juliet
- Григорьевна (daughter of Grigory) - traditional patronymic
- Капулетова feminine form of invented surname "Капулетов" (from Capulet)
- Юлия works beautifully in verse (can be 2 syllables: Юль-я or 3: Ю-ли-я)

**Character Essence**: Obedient daughter transforms into passionate, decisive heroine

---

### Mercutio → Меркурий Александрович Вольский

**Full Name**: Меркурий Александрович Вольский (Merkury Alexandrovich Volsky)
**Pronunciation**: mer-KÚ-ry a-lek-SÁN-dro-vich VÓL-sky
**Stress Patterns**:
- Меркурий (mer-KÚ-ry) - 3 syllables, stress on 2nd
- Александрович (a-lek-SÁN-dro-vich) - 5 syllables, stress on 3rd
- Вольский (VÓL-sky) - 2 syllables, stress on 1st

**Nickname/Informal**: Меркуша (Merkúsha), Меркуха (Merkúkha)
**Age**: 20-22 years old
**Family Position**: Not related to Монтягины, but close friend

**Name Rationale**:
- "Меркурий" is Russian form of Mercury/Mercutio
- Александрович (son of Alexander) - heroic, common patronymic
- Вольский invented surname suggesting "вóльный" (free, independent)
- Associated with Prince Escalus equivalent (cousin or friend)

**Character Essence**: Witty, bawdy, fantastic imagination, tragic comic figure

---

### Tybalt → Тихон Иванович Капулетов

**Full Name**: Тихон Иванович Капулетов (Tikhon Ivanovich Kapuletov)
**Pronunciation**: TÍ-khon i-VÁ-no-vich ka-pu-LÉ-tov
**Stress Patterns**:
- Тихон (TÍ-khon) - 2 syllables, stress on 1st
- Иванович (i-VÁ-no-vich) - 4 syllables, stress on 2nd
- Капулетов (ka-pu-LÉ-tov) - 4 syllables, stress on 3rd

**Nickname/Informal**: Тиша (Tísha)
**Age**: 25-27 years old
**Family Position**: Nephew or cousin of Yulia's father

**Name Rationale**:
- "Тихон" maintains T- initial, ironically means "peaceful, quiet"
- Иванович (son of Ivan) - extremely common, solid patronymic
- Капулетов masculine form of family name
- Ironic: "Tíkhon" (peaceful) for hot-tempered character

**Character Essence**: Hot-blooded, proud, defending family honor

### Benvolio → Бенедикт Сергеевич Монтягин

**Full Name**: Бенедикт Сергеевич Монтягин (Benedikt Sergeyevich Montyagin)
**Pronunciation**: be-ne-DÍKT ser-GÉ-ye-vich mon-TYÁ-gin
**Stress Patterns**:
- Бенедикт (be-ne-DÍKT) - 3 syllables, stress on 3rd
- Сергеевич (ser-GÉ-ye-vich) - 4 syllables, stress on 2nd
- Монтягин (mon-TYÁ-gin) - 3 syllables, stress on 2nd

**Nickname/Informal**: Бéня (Bénya), Бенедя (Benédya)
**Age**: 20-21 years old
**Family Position**: Cousin of Roman

**Name Rationale**:
- "Бенедикт" similar sound to Benvolio, means "blessed"
- Сергеевич (son of Sergey) - common, respectable patronymic
- Монтягин same family as Roman
- Name suggests peacemaking nature

**Character Essence**: Peacemaker, loyal friend, voice of reason

## 5. Address Forms and Social Hierarchy

### Formal Address (Public, Respectful)

**Full name with patronymic**:
- Роман Владимирович (to Roman)
- Юлия Григорьевна (to Yulia)

**Title + Surname**:
- Господин Монтягин (Lord/Mr. Montyagin)
- Госпожа Капулетова (Lady/Mrs. Kapuletova)

**Used by**:
- Social inferiors to superiors
- Strangers
- Formal occasions

---

### Informal Address (Private, Intimate)

**First name only**:
- Роман, Юлия, Меркурий

**Diminutive forms** (affection):
- Ромáша, Юленька, Меркуша

**Used by**:
- Family members
- Close friends
- Lovers (especially diminutives)

---

### Servants/Inferiors

**Address nobles as**:
- Барин/Барыня (Master/Mistress)
- Господин/Госпожа (Sir/Madam) + surname
- Full name with patronymic (respectful)

**Nobles address servants as**:
- First name only: "Матрёна!"
- Patronymic if showing respect: "Матрёна Ивановна"

## 6. Verse Usage Considerations

### Metrical Flexibility

**Names chosen for multiple stress patterns**:

**Роман** (2 syllables):
- ro-MÁN (iambic: u —)
- Can also be stretched: Ро-ма-на (genitive, 3 syllables)

**Юлия** (3 syllables):
- YÚ-li-ya (dactyl: — u u)
- Or compressed: Юль-я (2 syllables)
- Diminutive: Юля (YÚ-lya, 2 syllables, trochee: — u)

**Меркурий** (3 syllables):
- mer-KÚ-ry (amphibrach: u — u)
- Good for mid-line position

**Тихон** (2 syllables):
- TÍ-khon (trochee: — u)
- Or Ti-хó-на (genitive, 3 syllables)

---

### Rhyme Possibilities

**Names ending in -ин** (Монтягин, Капулетов-Капулетин):
- Can rhyme with: один (one), сын (son), господин (sir), причин (reasons-genitive)
- Masculine rhyme (stress final)

**Names ending in -ова/-ева** (Капулетова):
- Can rhyme with: слова (words), сурова (stern-feminine), основа (basis)
- Feminine rhyme (stress penultimate)

**First names**:
- Роман: туман (fog), обман (deception), карман (pocket)
- Юлия: more difficult, avoid forcing rhymes with names

## 7. Cultural Notes

### Patronymics

**Formation**:
- Father's name + -ович/-евич (male children)
- Father's name + -овна/-евна (female children)

**Examples**:
- Владимир → Владимирович / Владимировна
- Григорий → Григорьевич / Григорьевна

**Usage**: Standard in all formal contexts, sign of respect

---

### Diminutives

**Show affection, intimacy**:
- Роман → Рома, Ромаша, Ромочка
- Юлия → Юля, Юленька, Юлечка
- Меркурий → Меркуша, Меркуха

**Progression**: Novel can show relationships deepening through name usage
- Start: Formal names
- Develop: First names
- Intimate: Diminutives

---

### Noble Surnames

**Invented surnames** (Монтягин, Капулетов):
- Sound plausibly Russian
- -ин/-ов endings standard for Russian surnames
- Feminine forms: -ина/-ова (Монтягина, Капулетова)

**Real surnames** (Волконский, Щербатов):
- Used for Prince/Governor to add authenticity
- Well-known historical families
