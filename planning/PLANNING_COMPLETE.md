## Success Criteria Review

### Technical Excellence:
- [ ] Perfect Onegin stanza form (aBaBccDDeFFeGG)
- [ ] Iambic tetrameter with correct stress patterns
- [ ] NO forbidden rhymes (verbs, grammatical endings)
- [ ] Rich rhymes preferred (consonants before stress match)
- [ ] Natural-sounding Russian despite strict meter
- [ ] All stress patterns verified with dictionaries

### Narrative Quality:
- [ ] Compelling adaptation maintaining Shakespeare's power
- [ ] Well-differentiated character voices
- [ ] Consistent, engaging narrator persona
- [ ] Effective balance of narrative and digression (1:2 ratio)
- [ ] Successful cultural transposition (feels authentically Russian)
- [ ] Five-day timeline maintained with appropriate compression

### Pushkinian Style:
- [ ] Precision and economy of language
- [ ] Deceptive simplicity masking technical complexity
- [ ] Ironic distance with genuine emotion
- [ ] Metafictional self-awareness
- [ ] Synthesis of high and low registers
- [ ] "Effortless" verse from meticulous revision

### Emotional Impact:
- [ ] Tragedy feels inevitable yet shocking
- [ ] Young lovers sympathetically portrayed
- [ ] Social critique implicit in narrator's commentary
- [ ] Reader engaged both intellectually and emotionally
- [ ] Ending elegiac and thought-provoking
