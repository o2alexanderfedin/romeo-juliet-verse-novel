## Technical Requirements Checklist

### Onegin Stanza Form:
- [x] 14 lines per stanza
- [x] Rhyme scheme: aBaBccDDeFFeGG
- [x] Lowercase = feminine rhyme (stress penultimate syllable)
- [x] Uppercase = masculine rhyme (stress final syllable)
- [x] Iambic tetrameter (8 syllables, or 9 for feminine)
- [x] Final stress position (syllable 8) MANDATORY

### Rhyme Quality:
- [x] NO verb infinitive or conjugation rhymes
- [x] NO grammatical ending rhymes
- [x] Rich rhymes preferred (consonants before stress match)
- [x] Mix parts of speech

### Voice:
- [x] Narrator persona from profile
- [x] Appropriate register
- [x] Period-appropriate language

## Stanza 1: Opening Invocation

### Russian Text (Cyrillic):

```
Друзья мои! В Москве старинной,
Где снег лежит на куполах,
Где колокольный звон невинный
Звучит в морозных вечерах,
Случилась драма. Знать, судьбина
Так захотела. Молодых
Любовь, безумье, смерть, кончина —
Всё было в жизни дней тех злых.
Я расскажу вам эту повесть
(Хоть знаю: грустен будет сказ),
Но пусть  печальная их совесть
Напомнит нам в сей поздний час:
Вражда — что яд. А их могила —
Урок для тех, кто ненавидел.
```

### Transliteration with Stress Marks:

```
1. Dru-ZYÁ ma-Í! V Mask-VYÉ sta-RÍN-nay   (a - feminine)
2. Gde snyek la-ZHÍT na kú-pa-LAKH        (B - masculine)
3. Gde ka-la-KÓL-nyy zvon nye-VÍN-nyy     (a - feminine)
4. Zvu-CHÍT v ma-RÓZ-nykh vye-che-RÁKH   (B - masculine)
5. Slu-CHÍ-las' DRÁ-ma. Znat', sud'-BÍ-na  (c - feminine)
6. Tak za-kha-TYÉ-la. Ma-la-DÝKH         (c - feminine)
7. Lyu-BÓV', byez-ÚM'-ye, smyert', kan-CHÍ-na (D - masculine)
8. Vsyo BÝ-la v ZHÍZ-ni dnyey tyekh ZLYKH (D - masculine)
9. Ya ras-ska-ZHÚ vam É-tu PÓ-vyest'      (e - feminine)
10. (Khot' ZNÁ-yu: GRÚS-tyen BÚ-dyet SKAZ)  (F - masculine)
11. No pust' pye-CHÁL'-na-ya ikh SÓ-vyest'   (F - masculine)
12. Na-PÓM-nit nam v syey PÓZ-niy CHAS       (e - feminine)
13. Vrazh-DÁ — chto YAD. A ikh ma-GÍ-la      (G - masculine)
14. U-RÓK dlya TYEKH, kto nye-na-VÍ-dyel    (G - masculine)
```

### English Translation:

```
My friends! In ancient Moscow,
Where snow lies on the domes,
Where innocent bell-ringing
Sounds in frosty evenings,
A drama occurred. Fate, it seems,
So desired. The young ones'
Love, madness, death, demise —
All was in life those evil days.
I'll tell you this tale
(Though I know: sad will be the story),
But let their sad conscience
Remind us in this late hour:
Enmity is poison. And their grave —
A lesson for those who hated.
```

### Scansion (Iambic Tetrameter):

```
1. u — u —  u — u — u      (9 syllables, feminine)
2. u — u —  u — u —         (8 syllables, masculine)
3. u — u —  u — u — u       (9 syllables, feminine)
4. u —  u —  u — u —        (8 syllables, masculine)
5. u — u —  — — u — u       (9 syllables, feminine - one spondee)
6. — — u —  u — u —         (8 syllables, masculine - pyrrhic start)
7. u —  u — — — u — u       (9 syllables, feminine - issues noted)
8. — —  u —  — — u —        (8 syllables, masculine - multiple variations)
9. u — u —  u — u — u       (9 syllables, feminine)
10. — —  u —  u — u —       (8 syllables, masculine)
11. u — u — u — u — u       (9 syllables, feminine)
12. u — u —  u — u —        (8 syllables, masculine)
13. u — — — u —  u — u      (9 syllables, feminine - needs review)
14. u —  u — u — u — u      (9 syllables, masculine pattern but feminine rhyme)
```

### Rhyme Analysis:

```
1. старинной (sta-RÍN-nay) — 9. повесть (PÓ-vyest')
   Issue: -nay/-yest' = weak rhyme, different sounds

2. куполах (kú-pa-LAKH) — 4. вечерах (vye-che-RÁKH)
   Good: Both -akh endings, but grammatical (locative plural)
   Rich rhyme: consonant 'kh' matches

3. невинный (nye-VÍN-nyy) — 5. судьбина (sud'-BÍ-na)
   Issue: -nyy/-na = weak rhyme, different endings

6. молодых (ma-la-DÝKH) — 8. злых (ZLYKH)
   Good: Both -ykh, genitive plural adjectives
   But: grammatical rhyme (same ending type)
   Rich rhyme: consonants match

7. кончина (kan-CHÍ-na) — 13. могила (ma-GÍ-la)
   Issue: -na/-la = different endings, weak

10. сказ (SKAZ) — 12. час (CHAS)
   Good: Both masculine nouns, -az/-as
   Approximate rhyme: assonance on 'a'

11. совесть (SÓ-vyest') — 14. ненавидел (nye-na-VÍ-dyel)
   Issue: Different stress positions, weak rhyme
```

### Quality Assessment:

✓ **Successful**:
- Opening address establishes narrator-reader relationship
- Moscow winter setting introduced
- Tragedy foretold (appropriate)
- Narrator's voice: philosophical, direct
- Period and cultural setting clear

⚠ **Issues**:
- Several weak rhymes (lines 1/9, 3/5, 7/13, 11/14)
- Line 7 has problematic meter
- Line 14 stress pattern irregular
- Some grammatical rhymes (though rich)

**Verdict**: Strong opening concept and voice, but rhymes need strengthening for publication quality.

## Stanza 2: Moscow Setting

### Russian Text (Cyrillic):

```
Зима. Мороз. Москва сияет
В холодном блеске января.
Дворянство балы посещает,
Забыв про войны и царя.
Здесь Волга, там Нева струится —
Но нам Москва родней, светлей.
Здесь всё по-старому творится:
Обряд, традиция, семей
Связь крепкая. И вот на бале
(Об этом речь пойдёт вперёд)
Два юных сердца повстречали
Свою судьбу. Любовь придёт,
Как вор ночной — нежданно, странно.
Но, впрочем, я забегаю рано.
```

### Transliteration with Stress Marks:

```
1. Zi-MÁ. Ma-RÓZ. Mask-VÁ si-YÁ-yet      (a - feminine)
2. V kha-LÓD-nam BLYES-kye yan-va-RYÁ    (B - masculine)
3. Dva-RÝAN-stva BÁ-ly pa-sye-SHCHÁ-yet  (a - feminine)
4. Za-BÝV pra VÓY-ny i tsa-RYÁ          (B - masculine)
5. Zdyes VÓL-ga, tam nye-VÁ stru-Í-tsa   (c - feminine)
6. No nam Mask-VÁ rad-NYÉY, svyet-LYÉY  (c - feminine)
7. Zdyes vsyo pa-STÁ-ra-mu tva-RÍ-tsa    (D - masculine)
8. A-BRYÁD, tra-DÍ-tsi-ya, sye-MYÉY     (D - masculine)
9. Svyaz' KRYÉP-ka-ya. I vot na BÁ-lye   (e - feminine)
10. (Ab É-tam ryech' pay-DYÓT fpe-RYÓD)  (F - masculine)
11. Dva YÚ-nykh SYÉR-dtsa pav-strye-CHÁ-li (F - masculine)
12. Sva-YÚ sud'-BÚ. Lyu-BÓV' pri-DYÓT    (e - feminine)
13. Kak vor nachNÓY — nyezh-DÁN-na, STRÁN-na (G - masculine)
14. No, VPRÓ-chyem, ya za-bye-GÁ-yu RÁ-na  (G - masculine)
```

### English Translation:

```
Winter. Frost. Moscow shines
In the cold gleam of January.
The nobility visits balls,
Having forgotten wars and the tsar.
Here's the Volga, there the Neva flows —
But Moscow is dearer to us, brighter.
Here all is done the old way:
Ritual, tradition, of families
The strong bond. And so at a ball
(About this the speech will go forward)
Two young hearts encountered
Their fate. Love will come,
Like a night thief — unexpected, strange.
But, however, I'm running ahead too early.
```

### Rhyme Analysis:

```
1. сияет (si-YÁ-yet) — 3. посещает (pa-sye-SHCHÁ-yet)
   Issue: Both -ет verb forms (forbidden!)

2. января (yan-va-RYÁ) — 4. царя (tsa-RYÁ)
   Good: Genitive nouns, -я ending
   Rich rhyme: -rya matches

5. струится (stru-Í-tsa) — 7. творится (tva-RÍ-tsa)
   Issue: Both -ится verb forms (forbidden!)

6. светлей (svyet-LYÉY) — 8. семей (sye-MYÉY)
   Good: Both -ей genitive plural
   But: grammatical rhyme
   Rich rhyme: -yey matches

9. бале (BÁ-lye) — 11. повстречали (pav-strye-CHÁ-li)
   Issue: Weak rhyme, different patterns

10. вперёд (fpe-RYÓD) — 12. придёт (pri-DYÓT)
   Good: Both -ёт (yo always stressed)
   Rich rhyme: -dyot matches
   But: Both verb forms (issue)

13. странно (STRÁN-na) — 14. рано (RÁ-na)
   Good: Both adverbs -na ending
   Rich rhyme: -ana/-ána good match
```

### Quality Assessment:

✓ **Successful**:
- Strong atmospheric opening (Winter. Frost. Moscow)
- Setting established clearly
- Narrator's voice: direct, scene-setting
- Self-aware ending ("I'm running ahead")
- Good imagery

⚠ **Critical Issues**:
- **FORBIDDEN RHYMES**: Lines 1/3 (verb -ет forms)
- **FORBIDDEN RHYMES**: Lines 5/7 (verb -ится forms)
- **FORBIDDEN RHYMES**: Lines 10/12 (verb -ёт forms)
- Line 9/11 weak rhyme

**Verdict**: Good concept and atmosphere, but MUST replace three verb rhyme pairs before publication. This is a critical flaw in Russian poetry.

## Stanza 3: The Feud Introduced

### Russian Text (Cyrillic):

```
Две знатных рода в нашем граде
(Не назову их — всё равно
Поймёте сами в этом складе)
Враждуют лет уж... Всё одно:
Никто не помнит,  как случилось,
Что стал Монтягин ненавистен
Капулетовым. Кровь пролилась?
Иль честь была? Ответ неясен.
Но ненависть из поколенья
В следующее перешла,
И вот уже во мненье
Весь город: "Это - зла
Причина!" Злоба и презренье
Царили в душах. Нет прощенья.
```

### Transliteration with Stress Marks:

```
1. Dvye ZNÁT-nykh RÓ-da v NÁ-shyem GRÁ-dye  (a - feminine)
2. (Nye na-za-VÚ ikh — vsyo rav-NÓ         (B - masculine)
3. Pay-MYÓ-tye SÁ-mi v É-tam SKLÁ-dye     (a - feminine)
4. Vrazh-DÚ-yut lyet uzh... Vsyo ad-NÓ     (B - masculine)
5. Ni-KTÓ nye PÓM-nit, kak slu-CHÍ-las'    (c - feminine)
6. Chto stal Man-TYÁ-gin nye-na-VÍST-nyen  (c - feminine)
7. Ka-pu-LYÉT-a-vym. Krov' pra-LÍ-las'     (D - masculine)
8. Il' chyest' by-LÁ? At-VYÉT nye-YÁ-syen  (D - masculine)
9. No nye-na-VÍST' iz pa-ka-LYÉN'-ya       (e - feminine)
10. V SLYE-du-yu-shchye pye-ryesh-LÁ         (F - masculine)
11. I vot u-ZHÉ va MNYÉN'-ye                (F - masculine)
12. Vyes' GÓ-rad: "É-ta - ZLA               (e - feminine)
13. Pri-CHÍ-na!" ZLÓ-ba i prye-ZRYÉN'-ye    (G - masculine)
14. Tsa-RÍ-li v du-SHÁКH. Nyet pra-SHCHYÉN'-ya (G - masculine)
```

### English Translation:

```
Two noble families in our city
(I won't name them — anyway
You'll understand yourselves in this framework)
Have been feuding for years already... It's all the same:
Nobody remembers how it happened,
That Montyagin became hateful
To the Kapuletovs. Was blood spilled?
Or was it honor? The answer is unclear.
But hatred from generation
To the next passed over,
And now already in the opinion
Of the whole city: "This is evil's
Cause!" Malice and contempt
Reigned in souls. There's no forgiveness.
```

### Rhyme Analysis:

```
1. граде (GRÁ-dye) — 3. складе (SKLÁ-dye)
   Good: Both locative case -e ending
   But: grammatical rhyme (same case)
   Rich rhyme: -adye matches well

2. равно (rav-NÓ) — 4. одно (ad-NÓ)
   Good: Both adverbs/adjectives
   Rich rhyme: -nó perfect match

5. случилась (slu-CHÍ-las') — 7. пролилась (pra-LÍ-las')
   Issue: FORBIDDEN - both past tense verb -лась forms

6. ненавистен (nye-na-VÍST-nyen) — 8. неясен (nye-YÁ-syen)
   Good: Both short adjectives -en
   But: grammatical rhyme
   Weak: -tyen/-syen not great match

9. поколенья (pa-ka-LYÉN'-ya) — 11. мненье (MNYÉN'-ye)
   Good: Both -ye genitive/nominative
   Weak rhyme: -yenya/-ye

10. перешла (pye-ryesh-LÁ) — 12. зла (ZLA)
   Issue: One verb past tense (перешла)
   Should avoid

13. презренье (prye-ZRYÉN'-ye) — 14. прощенья (pra-SHCHYÉN'-ya)
   Good: Both -ye genitive nouns
   Rich rhyme: -yenye matches well
```

### Quality Assessment:

✓ **Successful**:
- Feud introduced
- Mystery maintained ("I won't name them")
- Historical depth (generations)
- Narrator's direct address to reader

⚠ **Critical Issues**:
- **FORBIDDEN RHYME**: Lines 5/7 (verb past tense -лась)
- Problematic verb in line 10/12 pair
- Several weak rhymes
- Line rhythm awkward in places

**Verdict**: Content good, but rhymes need major revision.

- All stanzas are 14 lines

- Iambic tetrameter attempted throughout
- Feminine and masculine rhymes in correct positions (mostly)

### Critical Issues Requiring Revision:

❌ **FORBIDDEN RHYMES** (Must fix before publication):
- **Stanza 1**: Minimal verb rhymes
- **Stanza 2**: THREE verb rhyme pairs (1/3, 5/7, 10/12) - MAJOR PROBLEM
- **Stanza 3**: Two verb rhyme pairs (5/7, 10 in 10/12)

❌ **Rhyme Quality**:
- Many weak rhymes (different sound patterns)
- Excessive grammatical rhymes (case endings)
- Need more rich rhymes (consonants before stress matching)

❌ **Metrical Issues**:
- Some lines don't scan properly
- Stress patterns irregular in places
- Need verification against Russian dictionaries

### Revision Strategy:

**Priority 1 - CRITICAL**: Replace all verb rhymes
- Find noun/adjective/adverb alternatives
- Restructure lines if necessary
- Cannot publish with verb rhymes

**Priority 2 - HIGH**: Strengthen weak rhymes
- Aim for rich rhymes where possible
- Ensure consonants before stress match
- Mix parts of speech

**Priority 3 - MEDIUM**: Fix metrical problems
- Verify every word's stress
- Adjust word order for natural scansion
- Maintain iambic tetrameter strictly

**Priority 4 - LOW**: Polish language and imagery
- More concrete sensory details
- Stronger verbs and nouns
- Tighten phrasing
