## Technical Requirements (All Stanzas)

### Onegin Stanza Form
- **14 lines** per stanza (inviolable)
- **Rhyme scheme**: aBaBccDDeFFeGG
  - Lowercase (a,c,e) = **feminine** rhyme (9 syllables, stress on penultimate)
  - Uppercase (B,D,F,G) = **masculine** rhyme (8 syllables, stress on final)
- **Meter**: Iambic tetrameter throughout
- **Line 8 Mandatory**: Final stress on syllable 8 (NEVER violate)

### Forbidden Rhymes (Critical - Zero Tolerance)
❌ **NEVER use**:
- Verb infinitives (-ть, -ти): любить/говорить, смотреть/гореть
- Verb conjugations (-ет, -ит, -ает): говорит/звонит, идёт/придёт
- Verb past tense (-л, -ла, -ло): сказал/узнал, встретил/заметил
- Excessive grammatical endings: дома/города, цветы/мечты
- Trite pairs: любовь/кровь, розы/морозы, природа/свобода

### Rhyme Quality Targets (ELEVATED for Meeting Scene)
- **Stanza 31**: 45-55% rich rhymes (meeting dialogue continuation)
- **Stanza 32**: 45-55% rich rhymes (conversation or dance invitation)
- **Stanza 33**: 50-60% rich rhymes (dance or deepening conversation)
- **Stanza 34**: 50-60% rich rhymes (culmination building)
- **Stanza 35**: 50-60% rich rhymes (hand-kiss or digression—maximum elevation)

### Dialogue Guidance (Shakespearean Adaptation)

**Shakespeare's Original** (Romeo & Juliet, Act 1, Scene 5, lines 93-110):
```
ROMEO: If I profane with my unworthiest hand
        This holy shrine, the gentle fine is this:
        My lips, two blushing pilgrims, ready stand
        To smooth that rough touch with a tender kiss.

JULIET: Good pilgrim, you do wrong your hand too much,
         Which mannerly devotion shows in this;
         For saints have hands that pilgrims' hands do touch,
         And palm to palm is holy palmers' kiss.

ROMEO: Have not saints lips, and holy palmers too?

JULIET: Ay, pilgrim, lips that they must use in prayer.

ROMEO: O, then, dear saint, let lips do what hands do;
        They pray, grant thou, lest faith turn to despair.

JULIET: Saints do not move, though grant for prayers' sake.

ROMEO: Then move not, while my prayer's effect I take.
        [He kisses her]
```

**Russian Adaptation Principles**:

1. **Religious Imagery Adaptation**:
   - **Shakespeare**: Catholic imagery (shrine, pilgrims, saints, prayer)
   - **Russian**: Orthodox Christian imagery (святыня/shrine, грешный/sinful, благословение/blessing)
   - **Note**: Pilgrimage less common in Orthodox tradition—adapt carefully
   - **Alternative**: Focus on sin/purity, blessing/grace, light/darkness (more Orthodox)

2. **Compression Required**:
   - **Shakespeare**: 14 lines of dialogue (too long for Onegin stanza)
   - **Russian**: Must compress to 4-6 lines maximum direct speech
   - **Solution**: Heavy narrator framing, reported speech, brief direct quotes

3. **Formal Address Mandatory**:
   - **вы** (you-formal), NOT ты (you-intimate)
   - **ваш** (your-formal), NOT твой (your-intimate)
   - They just met—formal address non-negotiable

4. **Hand Imagery Maintained**:
   - Hand touching acceptable in Russian culture (appropriate)
   - "Рука" (hand), "прикосновение" (touch), "ладонь" (palm)
   - Hand-kiss comes later (Stanza 35, if Option A chosen)

5. **Scanning Requirement**:
   - EVERY line must scan perfectly in iambic tetrameter
   - 8 syllables (9 for feminine ending)
   - Stress pattern must be natural-sounding
   - Test aloud multiple times

**Possible Russian Exchange** (Compressed, Heavily Narrator-Framed):

### Technical Notes

**Dialogue in Iambic Tetrameter - Critical Challenge**:
- Rendering natural-sounding speech in strict meter EXTREMELY difficult
- Russian speech patterns don't naturally fall into iambic rhythm
- Every syllable must be counted, every stress verified
- Formal address adds syllables: "вы" vs "ты", "ваш" vs "твой"

**Recommended Approaches**:

1. **Heavy Narrator Framing** (EASIEST, MOST CONTROL):
   - Narrator describes exchange: "Роман сказал..." (Roman said...)
   - Brief direct speech: only 1-2 words quoted
   - Narrator describes manner, tone, impact
   - Rhyme scheme carried by narrator's words, not dialogue

2. **Reported Speech** (MEDIUM DIFFICULTY):
   - Indirect speech: "Он попросил прощенья..." (He asked forgiveness...)
   - What was said summarized, not quoted
   - More natural to scan (narrator's words, not character's)
   - Less immediate impact, but more control

3. **Brief Direct Speech** (HARDEST, HIGHEST IMPACT):
   - One line maximum per character per stanza
   - Must scan perfectly in iambic tetrameter
   - Must use formal вы (you-formal)
   - Test aloud multiple times before finalizing

**Recommended for Stanza 31**: Combination of #1 and #2 (heavy narrator framing with brief reported speech), with possibility of ONE line of direct speech for maximum impact.

**Pushkin's Model**:

**When to Use Digression**:
- If chapter digression quota not met (Ch 3 target: 56% digressive)
- If want to delay discovery, build anticipation
- If want Pushkinian flavor emphasized
- If previous stanzas very action-heavy, want variety

**When NOT to Use**:
- If momentum toward climax (hand-kiss more satisfying)
- If digression quota already met or exceeded
- If reader anticipation too high (digression may frustrate)

**If Chosen**:
- Must be profound, not filler
- Must serve thematic purpose (love transcends language)
- Must connect to narrative (return to scene by end)
- Should be playful/light touch, not heavy-handed
