## Technical Requirements (All Stanzas)

### Onegin Stanza Form
- **14 lines** per stanza (inviolable)
- **Rhyme scheme**: aBaBccDDeFFeGG
  - Lowercase (a,b,c,e,f) = **feminine** rhyme (9 syllables, stress on penultimate)
  - Uppercase (B,D,F,G) = **masculine** rhyme (8 syllables, stress on final)
- **Meter**: Iambic tetrameter throughout
- **Line 8 Mandatory**: Final stress on syllable 8 (never violate)

### Forbidden Rhymes (Critical - Zero Tolerance)
❌ **NEVER use**:
- Verb infinitives (-ть, -ти): любить/говорить
- Verb conjugations (-ет, -ит, -ает): говорит/звонит
- Verb past tense (-л, -ла, -ло, -ли): сказал/узнал (minimize, use only narratively)
- Excessive grammatical endings
- Trite pairs: любовь/кровь, розы/морозы

### Rhyme Quality Targets
- **Stanzas 11-12**: 40-50% rich rhymes (digression, philosophical)
- **Stanza 13**: 45-55% rich rhymes (character introduction, elevated)
- **Stanzas 14-15**: 40-50% rich rhymes (tension building, action)
- **Overall minimum**: 40-50% rich rhymes across all five stanzas

## Technical Notes: Cultural & Historical Context

### Russian Noble Honor Codes

**Host's Obligation (критично для Stanza 15)**:
- **Guest Right** (гостеприимство): Sacred duty to protect guests under one's roof
- Even enemy, once admitted, cannot be harmed or expelled by host
- Violation brings dishonor worse than tolerating enemy's presence
- Grigory MUST restrain himself and Tikhon due to this code
- Explains why Roman not immediately expelled despite Kapuletov fury

**Family Honor**:
- Insult to family name requires response (blood or satisfaction)
- Each generation inherits feuds and obligations
- Male relatives are enforcers of family honor
- Tikhon's role: Younger male kinsman, family "sword"

**Feud Dynamics**:
- Napoleonic War betrayal backstory (one family accused other of collaboration with French)
- Neither side remembers full truth anymore, only hatred
- Passed to next generation as pure enmity
- Montyagin/Kapuletov equivalent to Montague/Capulet

### Russian Names in Verse

**Stress Patterns Critical for Meter**:
- **Тихон** (TÍ-khon): Trochee (— u), can begin line or follow unstressed syllable
- **Иванович** (i-VÁ-no-vich): 4 syllables, can span mid-line
- **Григорий** (gri-GÓ-ry): Amphibrach (u — u), good for mid-line
- **Монтягин** (mon-TYÁ-gin): Amphibrach, rhymes with -ин words

**Declension for Meter Flexibility**:
- Nominative: Тихон (2 syll.)
- Genitive: Тихона (3 syll.)
- Dative: Тихону (3 syll.)
- Accusative: Тихона (3 syll.)
- Instrumental: Тихоном (3 syll.)
- Prepositional: (о) Тихоне (3 syll.)

Use different cases to fit metrical requirements of each line.

### Ballroom Setting Details

**Physical Space**:
- Large hall (зал), high ceilings
- Chandeliers (люстры), candlelight (свечи)
- Musicians' gallery (хоры для музыкантов)
- Banquet tables along sides
- Dance floor center (танцевальная площадка)

**Social Dynamics**:
- Host presides, visible and accessible
- Guests mingle, dance, feast
- Servants circulate with refreshments
- Conversations in clusters
- Masked balls allow some anonymity (but not complete)

**Tikhon's Position**:
- As family member, moving freely through hall
- Can observe dancers, guests
- Close enough to host (Grigory) to reach quickly
- Recognizes voices/figures despite masks

**Roman's Position**:
- Among dancers or at edge of dance floor
- Transfixed by Yulia (probably dancing)
- Asked someone "Who is she?" (overheard by Tikhon)

- No awareness he's been recognized
