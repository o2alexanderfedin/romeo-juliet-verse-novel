## Technical Requirements (All Stanzas)

### Onegin Stanza Form
- **14 lines** per stanza (inviolable)
- **Rhyme scheme**: aBaBccDDeFFeGG
  - Lowercase (a,c,e) = **feminine** rhyme (9 syllables, stress on penultimate)
  - Uppercase (B,D,F,G) = **masculine** rhyme (8 syllables, stress on final)
- **Meter**: Iambic tetrameter throughout
- **Final stress**: Position 8 mandatory (NEVER violate)

### Forbidden Rhymes (Critical - Zero Tolerance)
❌ **NEVER use**:
- Verb infinitives (-ть, -ти): любить/говорить, смотреть/гореть
- Verb conjugations (-ет, -ит, -ает): говорит/звонит, идёт/придёт
- Verb past tense (-л, -ла, -ло): сказал/узнал, встретил/заметил
- Excessive grammatical endings: дома/города, цветы/мечты
- Trite pairs: любовь/кровь, розы/морозы, природа/свобода

### Rhyme Quality Targets (ELEVATED for Chapter Ending)
- **Stanza 41**: 40-45% rich rhymes (standard narrative)
- **Stanza 42**: 40-45% rich rhymes (atmospheric)
- **Stanza 43**: 40-45% rich rhymes (recognition scene)
- **Stanza 44**: 40-50% rich rhymes (first exchange - elevated)
- **Stanza 45**: 45-55% rich rhymes (CHAPTER ENDING - MAXIMUM ELEVATION)
- **Overall target**: 42-48% rich rhymes across five stanzas (elevated for chapter conclusion)

### Chapter Ending Techniques (Critical for Stanza 45)

Based on Eugene Onegin analysis:
1. **Philosophical Reflection**: Narrator comments on events, fate, love
2. **Open-Ended Commentary**: Not fully resolved, continues in next chapter
3. **Transition Signal**: Hints at what comes next without revealing all
4. **Elevated Language**: More polished, memorable final line
5. **Natural Break**: Completes narrative unit (feast + discovery + balcony setup done)
6. **Anticipatory**: Creates desire to read Chapter 4

**Pushkin's Chapter Ending Examples**:
- Often ends with narrator reflection or philosophical aside
- Sometimes addresses reader directly
- Creates natural pause while maintaining momentum
- Final couplet (GG) particularly strong and memorable

## Stanza 41: Feast Ends, Guests Departing

### Narrative Function
- **Primary**: Show feast winding down, guests leaving, separation of Roman and Yulia
- **Secondary**: Create sense of time passing, night deepening, moment ending
- **Transition**: From peak of discovery shock → toward private aftermath
- **Atmosphere**: Exhaustion, candlelight dimming, music fading, carriages called
- **Conflict**: Both want to speak, but swept into crowd, separated by propriety

### Narrator Voice
- **Tone**: Observant, slightly melancholy, noting passage of moment
- **Perspective**: Omniscient - sees both Roman and Yulia in separate parts of crowd
- **Involvement**: Medium - describing scene while hinting at lovers' emotional state
- **Approach**: Cinematic - wide shot of feast ending, then focus on two individuals
- **Balance**: 60% narration (scene description), 40% internal (characters' feelings)
