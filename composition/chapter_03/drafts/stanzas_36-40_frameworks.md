## Technical Requirements (All Stanzas)

### Onegin Stanza Form
- **14 lines** per stanza (inviolable)
- **Rhyme scheme**: aBaBccDDeFFeGG
  - Lowercase (a,c,e) = **feminine** rhyme (9 syllables, stress on penultimate)
  - Uppercase (B,D,F,G) = **masculine** rhyme (8 syllables, stress on final)
- **Meter**: Iambic tetrameter throughout
- **Line 8 Mandatory**: Final stress on syllable 8 (NEVER violate)

### Forbidden Rhymes (Critical - Zero Tolerance)
❌ **NEVER use**:
- Verb infinitives (-ть, -ти): любить/говорить, смотреть/гореть
- Verb conjugations (-ет, -ит, -ает): говорит/звонит, идёт/придёт
- Verb past tense (-л, -ла, -ло): сказал/узнал, встретил/заметил
- Excessive grammatical endings: дома/города, цветы/мечты
- Trite pairs: любовь/кровь, розы/морозы, природа/свобода

### Rhyme Quality Targets (VARIED for Sequence)
- **Stanza 36**: 45-55% rich rhymes (CRITICAL kiss/culmination moment - ELEVATED)
- **Stanza 37**:
  - Option A (Separation): 40-45% rich rhymes (standard-elevated)
  - Option B (Digression): 45-50% rich rhymes (philosophical elevation)
- **Stanza 38**: 40-45% rich rhymes (shock - visceral but not overly polished)
- **Stanza 39**: 40-45% rich rhymes (devastation - emotion over technique)
- **Stanza 40**: 40-45% rich rhymes (parallel to St.38-39)
- **Overall target**: 42-48% rich rhymes across five stanzas (elevated but varied by content)

## STANZA 36: Kiss OR Meeting Culmination

### Narrative Function
**PRIMARY - CRITICAL DECISION**: Kiss (transgressive but masked ball license) OR meeting culmination without lips-kiss
**Secondary**: Peak moment of joy and connection (highest point before fall)
**Tertiary**: Both characters transformed utterly, commitment sealed

**IMPORTANCE**: This stanza equals transformation stanzas (Ch3 St.8, Ch3 St.29) in critical importance. If kiss chosen: MAXIMUM elevation (45-55% rich rhymes). If culmination without kiss: still highly elevated (45-50% rich rhymes).

### KISS DECISION (Option A vs. Option B)

**Option A: LIPS-KISS** (bold, transgressive, Shakespearean model)
- **Shakespearean Precedent**: Romeo kisses Juliet in Act 1, Scene 5 (twice)
  - First kiss: "Thus from my lips, by yours, my sin is purged."
  - Second kiss: "You kiss by th' book"
- **Masked Ball License**: Carnival/masked ball provides social license for transgression
- **Era Considerations**:
  - Transgressive but not impossible
  - Masked identity provides cover
  - Passion overcomes protocol
- **Narrative Impact**:
  - Compounds horror of discovery (transgressed with enemy)
  - Point of no return (physical commitment)
  - Both "ruined" in social sense if discovered
- **Technical Requirements**: 45-55% rich rhymes, maximum elevation

**Option B: CULMINATION WITHOUT LIPS-KISS** (era-appropriate intensity)
- **Alternative Peak**:
  - Hand-holding, prolonged eye contact
  - Faces very close, breath mingling
  - OR hand-kiss (Roman kisses her hand reverently)
  - Commitment sealed without lips contact
- **Era Appropriateness**:
  - More socially acceptable
  - Still passionate and transformative
  - Leaves lips-kiss for later (balcony/secret meeting)
- **Narrative Impact**:
  - Still point of no return (emotional commitment)
  - Discovery still devastating (fell in love with enemy)
  - Maintains some propriety (less transgression)
- **Technical Requirements**: 45-50% rich rhymes, highly elevated

**RECOMMENDATION**: Consider overall narrative arc and cultural authenticity. Shakespeare used kiss; Russian 19th c. context might prefer Option B (hand-kiss or intense connection without lips). EITHER choice is valid and powerful. Choose based on:
1. Desired level of transgression
2. Cultural authenticity preference
3. Narrative pacing (save kiss for later OR use now)

**IMPORTANCE**: This is THE moment dramatic irony shifts. Readers have known since Chapter 1 that Roman and Yulia are from enemy families. NOW Yulia learns. Equal importance to kiss/culmination, but different quality: visceral shock vs. elevated transcendence.

### Shakespearean Model

**Shakespeare's Juliet** (Act 1, Scene 5, lines 134-141):
```
JULIET: Come hither, Nurse. What is yond gentleman?
NURSE: The son and heir of old Tiberio.
JULIET: What's he that now is going out of door?
NURSE: Marry, that, I think, be young Petruchio.
JULIET: What's he that follows there, that would not dance?
NURSE: I know not.
JULIET: Go ask his name.—If he be married,
        My grave is like to be my wedding bed.
NURSE: His name is Romeo, and a Montague,

JULIET: My only love sprung from my only hate!
        Too early seen unknown, and known too late!
```

**Russian Adaptation Principles**:
1. **Compress**: Cannot be 14+ lines of dialogue, must compress

- **Mode**: Close third-person with Yulia, narrator reports action and inner state
