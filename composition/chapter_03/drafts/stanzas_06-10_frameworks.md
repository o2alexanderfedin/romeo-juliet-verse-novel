## Technical Requirements (All Stanzas)

### Onegin Stanza Form
- **14 lines** per stanza (inviolable)
- **Rhyme scheme**: aBaBccDDeFFeGG
  - Lowercase (a,b,c,e,f) = **feminine** rhyme (9 syllables, stress on penultimate)
  - Uppercase (B,D,F,G) = **masculine** rhyme (8 syllables, stress on final)
- **Meter**: Iambic tetrameter throughout
- **Line 8 Mandatory**: Final stress on syllable 8 (never violate)

### Forbidden Rhymes (Critical - Zero Tolerance)
❌ **NEVER use**:
- Verb infinitives (-ть, -ти): любить/говорить
- Verb conjugations (-ет, -ит, -ает): говорит/звонит
- Verb past tense (-л, -ла, -ло, -ли): сказал/узнал (minimize, use only narratively)
- Excessive grammatical endings
- Trite pairs: любовь/кровь, розы/морозы

### Rhyme Quality Targets
- **Stanzas 6-7**: 50-60% rich rhymes (transformation moment, elevated)
- **Stanza 8**: 45-55% rich rhymes (emotional intensity)
- **Stanzas 9-10**: 40-50% rich rhymes (philosophical digression)
- **Overall minimum**: 45-55% rich rhymes across all five stanzas

### Character Voice Evolution

**Roman** (Peak Transformation):
- **Stanzas 6-7**: MID-TO-HIGH intensity shift, awestruck, overwhelmed, transfixed
  - Vocabulary: красота́ (beauty), свет (light), боги́ня (goddess), чу́до (miracle)
  - Syntax: Breathless, fragmented, exclamations, questions
  - Imagery: Light, radiance, divine, otherworldly

- **Stanza 8**: HIGH intensity, desperate urgency, physical reaction
  - Vocabulary: се́рдце бьётся (heart pounds), не мо́жет дыша́ть (cannot breathe)
  - Syntax: Questions ("Who is she?"), urgent commands (to self or others)
  - Imagery: Physical sensation, urgency, compulsion

**Narrator** (Philosophical Shift):
- **Stanzas 6-7**: Present-moment intensity, caught in Roman's transformation, visual poetry
- **Stanza 8**: Observational, noting Roman's reaction, some sympathy
- **Stanzas 9-10**: PHILOSOPHICAL mode, reflective, universal themes
  - Addresses reader directly
  - Personal touches (narrator's own thoughts)
  - Shift from specific (Roman) to universal (love at first sight generally)
