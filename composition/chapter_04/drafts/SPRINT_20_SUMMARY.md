## Lessons Learned

### 1. Framework Quality Gate Critical

Auditing frameworks BEFORE composition is crucial:
- Catches structural issues early
- Ensures comprehensive resources available during composition
- Prevents mid-composition discovery of missing rhyme options or vocabulary
- Quality gate approach validates significant investment (Sprints 16-19)

### 2. Enhanced Frameworks Pattern Established

Early stanzas (01-10) were from Sprint 16 (earliest sprint) and had thinner resources. Later sprints (17-19) established more comprehensive patterns. **Lesson**: Revisit early work after establishing quality patterns.

### 3. Technical Audit Checklist Valuable

Comprehensive checklist approach found issues that could have been missed:
- Quantitative metrics (rhyme pair counts, vocabulary counts) reveal gaps
- Systematic file-by-file review ensures nothing overlooked
- Cross-framework analysis catches inconsistencies

### 4. Configuration System Validated

Technical audit confirmed configuration system (St.31-50) is:
- Internally coherent (each path complete)
- Flexible (multiple paths available)
- Well-documented (clear transition notes)
- Ready for composition (composer can choose path during writing)

### 5. Zero Forbidden Rhymes Non-Negotiable

Strict adherence to forbidden rhyme rules is CRITICAL:
- No verb infinitives in rhyme position
- No verb conjugations in rhyme position
- Minimized grammatical endings
- Essential for Pushkin-style quality
