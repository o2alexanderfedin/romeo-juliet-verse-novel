
**Pattern Key**: u = unstressed syllable, — = stressed syllable

### Critical Meter Rules

1. **Position 8 stress MANDATORY**: Final stress must fall on syllable 8 (inviolable)
2. **Odd positions unstressed**: Positions 1, 3, 5, 7 must be unstressed
3. **Pyrrhic variations allowed**: Positions 2, 4, 6 can be unstressed (common)
4. **Feminine endings**: One extra unstressed syllable after syllable 8

### Forbidden Rhymes (ZERO TOLERANCE)

❌ **NEVER use**:
- **Verb infinitives** (-ть, -ти): любить/говорить, знать/сказать
- **Verb conjugations** (-ет, -ит, -ает, -ит): говорит/звонит, знает/играет
- **Verb past tense** (-л, -ла, -ло, -ли): был/забыл, знала/стала (minimize!)
- **Grammatical ending rhymes** (too easy): дома/города, цветы/мечты
- **Nabokov's trite pairs**: любовь/кровь, розы/морозы, мечта/красота

### Rhyme Quality Targets

**Overall Target for Stanzas 21-25**: 45-50% rich rhymes minimum

- **Stanza 21** (Lavrentiy's agreement): 45-50% rich (elevated, spiritual tone)
- **Stanza 22** (Plan/transition): 40-45% rich (practical or transitional)
- **Stanza 23** (Scene shift): 40-45% rich (atmospheric, establishing)
- **Stanza 24** (Yulia confesses): 45-50% rich (emotional peak)
- **Stanza 25** (Matryona responds): 45-50% rich (emotional, maternal)

**Rich Rhyme Definition**: Consonants BEFORE stressed vowel match
- Example: **пр**авда / **пр**авит (právda / právit)

### Scene Shift Technique - Detailed Notes

**Pushkin's Scene Shift Methods** (from Eugene Onegin analysis):

1. **Time/Location Markers**:
   - "But while..." / "Meanwhile..." / "That same morning..."
   - "In another part of..." / "Far from [previous location]..."
   - Clear demarcation so reader not confused

2. **Atmospheric Re-establishment**:
   - New location described (even briefly)
   - New mood/tone set
   - Sensory details ground reader in new space

3. **Character Introduction/Reintroduction**:
   - Character named explicitly
   - Physical state described (appearance, emotion)
   - Current action/situation established

4. **Narrative Voice Continuity**:
   - Narrator's voice stays consistent (provides continuity despite shift)
   - May comment on shift ("But let's leave Roman..." / "Now we turn to...")
   - Reader trusts narrator to guide through scene changes

**Application to Stanza 23**:
- ✅ Time marker: "But while Otets Lavrentiy..."
- ✅ Location: "In Kapuletov's house"
- ✅ Atmospheric: Window, morning, chamber details
- ✅ Character: Yulia reintroduced, state described
- ✅ New character: Matryona enters
- ✅ Narrator: Consistent voice guiding reader

**Avoiding Common Errors**:
- ❌ Don't shift abruptly without transition
- ❌ Don't assume reader knows where we are (state explicitly)
- ❌ Don't neglect atmospheric details (readers need to "see" new scene)
- ❌ Don't shift narrator voice (stay consistent)
