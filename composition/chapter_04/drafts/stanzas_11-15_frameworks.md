## Technical Requirements (All Stanzas)

### Onegin Stanza Form
- **14 lines** per stanza (inviolable)
- **Rhyme scheme**: aBaBccDDeFFeGG
  - Lowercase (a,b,c,e,f) = **feminine** rhyme (9 syllables, stress on penultimate)
  - Uppercase (B,D,F,G) = **masculine** rhyme (8 syllables, stress on final)
- **Meter**: Iambic tetrameter throughout
- **Line 8 Mandatory**: Final stress on syllable 8 (never violate)

### Forbidden Rhymes (Critical - Zero Tolerance)
❌ **NEVER use**:
- Verb infinitives (-ть, -ти): любить/говорить, сказать/узнать
- Verb conjugations (-ет, -ит, -ает): говорит/звонит, знает/летает
- Verb past tense (-л, -ла, -ло, -ли): сказал/узнал, была/жила (minimize heavily)
- Excessive grammatical endings (too easy/banal)
- Trite pairs: любовь/кровь, розы/морозы, природа/свобода

### Rhyme Quality Targets
- **Stanzas 11-12**: 45-55% rich rhymes (emotional peak, lyrical parting)
- **Stanza 13**: 40-50% rich rhymes (parting OR digression)
- **Stanzas 14-15**: 40-50% rich rhymes (digressive/transitional)
- **Overall minimum**: 45% rich rhymes across all five stanzas

### Character Names - Stress Patterns (CRITICAL)

**Roman Vladimirovich Montyagin** (Роман Владимирович Монтягин):
- **Роман** (ro-MÁN): 2 syllables, stress 2nd (iamb: u —)

## STANZAS 11-15: Rhyme Frameworks

### Stanza 11, Position 1 & 3 (Feminine 'a' rhyme)

**Configuration A (Parting - "depart/heart" theme)**:

**-арта cluster** (Not viable - no feminine forms)

**-ого cluster**:
- Let me think: "cannot depart" = "не могу уйти"
- Better: "neither can depart" = "никто не может расстаться"

**-тья cluster**:
- разлука́ (parting, f.nom.) - 3 syll., stress final: раз-лу-ка́ (masculine ending)
- расстава́ние (parting, n.nom.) - 5 syll., stress 3rd: рас-ста-ва́-ни-е (stress penult? count from end: ...ва́-ни-е, last 3. Stress on -ва́- is 3rd from end = antepenult)
- For feminine need stress on penult (2nd from end)

**Let me use proven clusters**:

**-ани cluster**:
- расставáния (parting, gen.) - расставáние → gen. расставáния
- Check stress: рас-ста-ва́-ни-я (5 syll., stress 3rd: ...ва́-ни-я)
- Count from end: я (last), ни (penult), ва́ (antepenult)
- Stress is antepenult, NOT feminine ending
- Skip.

**-órта cluster**:
- Actually for "heart": сéрдце (n., heart) - 2 syll., stress 1st (сéрд-це)
- Gen: сéрдца (stress 1st: сéрд-ца)
- This is feminine ending pattern! (2 syllables, stress 1st = stress penult)
- What rhymes with -éрдца?
- No good options.

**Standard proven cluster**:

**-óвы cluster**:
- слóва (words, gen.) - слóво → gen.pl. слов (monosyllable, skip) OR use nom.pl. словá (stress final, masculine)
- Actually: слóво (n.) → gen.sg. слóва (2 syll., stress 1st: слó-ва)
- Stress on 1st of 2 syllables = penult = feminine ending! ✓
- основы́ (foundations, gen.pl.) - NO, stress final
- Let me check: óснова (f., foundation) → gen.sg. основы (2 syll., stress where?)
- óснова (stress 1st) → gen. основы (should keep stress 1st: óсно-вы)
- Yes! **óсновы** (2 syll., stress 1st = penult = feminine)

**-óва cluster**:
- слóва (word, gen.sg.) / основы (foundations, gen.sg.)
- **Suggested**: слóва (words, gen.) / основы (foundations, gen.) OR снóва (again, adv.) / оснóвы

**-éзды cluster**:
- звёзды (stars, nom.pl.) - звездá → nom.pl. звёзды (2 syll., stress 1st: звёз-ды)
- гнёзда (nests, nom.pl.) - гнездó → nom.pl. гнёзда (2 syll., stress 1st)
- **Suggested**: звёзды (stars) / гнёзда (nests)

**TOP CHOICE**: звёзды (stars) / гнёзды (nests) - perfect for night/parting scene!

## Technical Notes: Cultural & Balcony Scene Context

### Shakespeare's Balcony Scene - Act 2, Scene 2

**Lines 150-190 (Parting sequence)**:
- "Good night, good night! Parting is such sweet sorrow, / That I shall say good night till it be morrow."
- Multiple false endings - each tries to close conversation, neither can
- Romeo: "Sleep dwell upon thine eyes, peace in thy breast! / Would I were sleep and peace, so sweet to rest!"
- Juliet: "Romeo!" (calls him back)
- Each "good night" followed by more words
- Finally Juliet withdraws into house

**Key Emotional Beats**:
1. Agreement to meet tomorrow (through Nurse)
2. Attempt to part - "Good night!"
3. Lingering - one more word, one more thought
4. Awareness of danger (discovery if caught)
5. Inability to actually separate
6. Final withdrawal (Juliet goes inside)
7. Romeo alone, watching empty window
8. His departure from garden at dawn
