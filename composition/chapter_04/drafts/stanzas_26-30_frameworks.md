## Technical Requirements (All Stanzas)

### Onegin Stanza Form
- **14 lines** per stanza (inviolable)
- **Rhyme scheme**: aBaBccDDeFFeGG
  - Lowercase (a,b,c,e,f) = **feminine** rhyme (9 syllables, stress on penultimate)
  - Uppercase (B,D,F,G) = **masculine** rhyme (8 syllables, stress on final)
- **Meter**: Iambic tetrameter throughout
- **Line 8 Mandatory**: Final stress on syllable 8 (never violate)

### Forbidden Rhymes (Critical - Zero Tolerance)
❌ **NEVER use**:
- Verb infinitives (-ть, -ти): любить/говорить, сказать/узнать
- Verb conjugations (-ет, -ит, -ает): говорит/звонит, знает/летает
- Verb past tense (-л, -ла, -ло, -ли): сказал/узнал, была/жила (minimize heavily)
- Excessive grammatical endings (too easy/banal)
- Trite pairs: любовь/кровь, розы/морозы, природа/свобода

### Rhyme Quality Targets
- **Stanzas 26-27**: 40-45% rich rhymes (narrative, some urgency)
- **Stanzas 28-30**: 45-55% rich rhymes (digressive, philosophical depth)
- **Overall minimum**: 45% rich rhymes across all five stanzas

### Character Names - Stress Patterns (CRITICAL)

## RHYME OPTIONS FOR ALL STANZAS

### CRITICAL INSTRUCTIONS FOR RHYME SECTION

**Stress Verification Protocol**:
1. Every Russian word MUST be stress-verified using Wiktionary, Rifmovnik.ru, or Zalizniak
2. NEVER guess stress patterns—Russian stress is unpredictable
3. Mark stressed syllable for every word: example → примéр (pri-MÉR)
4. ё is ALWAYS stressed—most reliable indicator

**Rhyme Quality Standards**:
- **Rich rhyme** (target 45-55%): Consonants before stressed vowel match
  Example: **к**óшка / **к**рóшка (consonant 'k' before stressed 'ó')
- **Exact rhyme**: Stressed vowel through end matches
  Example: лýна / однá (stressed 'ú'/'á' through end)
- **Deep rhyme**: Entire syllable before stress matches
  Example: **ми**лéние / **ми**шéние (syllable 'mi' before stress)

**Forbidden Rhymes (Zero Tolerance)**:
- ❌ Verb infinitives: любить/говорить (ends -ть)
- ❌ Verb conjugations: говорит/звонит (ends -ет, -ит)
- ❌ Verb past tense: сказал/узнал (ends -л, -ла) — minimize heavily
- ❌ Excessive grammatical endings: домá/городá (too easy)
- ❌ Trite pairs: любовь/кровь, розы/морозы, природа/свобода

**Quantities Required**:
- 7 rhyme positions per stanza × 5 stanzas = 35 rhyme positions
- 8-10 pairs per position minimum = 280-350+ pairs total
- Group by stanza and position
