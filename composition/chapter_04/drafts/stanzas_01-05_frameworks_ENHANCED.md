### Voice Requirements (Chapter 4, Stanzas 1-5):

**Yulia's Voice** (speaking in Stanzas 3-5):
- Age 13-14, gentle, modest, awakened by love
- Philosophical (questioning names and identity)
- Innocent wonder mixed with awareness
- Simple syntax (not elaborate like Roman)
- Feminine voice distinct from masculine
- Pre-transformation: dutiful → NOW: questioning social rules
- **Vocabulary**: Simple but elevated, not ornate
- **Imagery**: Practical mixed with romantic (like Juliet, not pure romance)
- **Tone**: Wondering, questioning, decisive when she speaks

- Elevated, poetic, formal (initially), passionate

- If speaks in St.5: respectful, ardent
- **Vocabulary**: More elevated than Yulia, celestial imagery
- **Imagery**: Light, stars, religious/sacred
- **Tone**: Worshipful, intense

**Narrator's Voice**:
- Lyrical, caught up in romance
- Sympathetic to lovers
- May reflect on balcony scene poetry
- Knows tragedy ahead (dramatic irony)
- **Tone Mix**: 40% Lyrical, 40% Narrative, 20% Sympathetic
- **Mode**: More lyrical than action chapters (lingering on atmosphere and emotion)
- **Reader Address**: Occasional, reflective
