### Narrative Function
Roman reveals himself (if not yet), responds to Yulia's "name" speech, begins vow to renounce identity for her love. Yulia's surprise/alarm mixed with joy. Critical moment of mutual recognition.

**Line 1 (a, fem)**: Narrator frames - Roman steps from shadows / speaks
**Line 2 (B, mas)**: His voice quiet but clear in winter night
**Line 3 (a, fem)**: "Call me your love" he says / vows to abandon name

**Line 11 (F, mas)**: Yet here they speak, defying all constraint
