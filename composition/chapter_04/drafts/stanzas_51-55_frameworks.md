# Stanzas 51-55: After Betrothal and Chapter 4 Conclusion

## Overview

**CHAPTER 4 FINALE**: These 5 stanzas conclude Chapter 4 with the secret joy of betrothal while building ominous tension toward Chapter 5's violence.

**Content Arc**:
- Stanza 51: Secret joy after betrothal - lovers apart but bound
- Stanza 52: Practical plans for secret marriage life
- Stanza 53: Hope vs. fate digression (dramatic irony peak)
- Stanza 54: Foreshadowing intensifies - violence approaches
- Stanza 55: POWERFUL CHAPTER CONCLUSION (Pushkin technique)

**Tone Progression**: From secret joy through hopeful planning to increasingly ominous foreshadowing, culminating in a resonant chapter ending that bridges to violence.

## Stanza 51: Secret Joy After Betrothal

### Content Outline

```
1  (a-fem) Теперь они навек связаны тайно,      [Now they are bound forever in secret]
2  (B-masc) Хоть порознь по домам идут,         [Though separately to homes they go]
3  (a-fem) Их души слиты необычайно,           [Their souls merged extraordinarily]
4  (B-masc) И радость с трепетом несут.          [And joy with trembling they bear]
5  (c-fem) В глазах Юлии блеск лучистый,       [In Yulia's eyes a radiant shine]
6  (c-fem) В походке Романа шаг плечистый,     [In Roman's walk a shouldered stride]
7  (D-masc) Но скрыть должны от всех печать      [But hide they must from all the seal]
8  (D-masc) Того, что их связала благодать.      [Of that which grace has bound them real]
9  (e-fem) Отец Лаврентий тихо молится,        [Father Lavrentiy quietly prays]
10 (F-masc) За них пред образом стоит,           [For them before the icon stands]
11 (F-masc) Их тайна с ним лишь остаётся,        [Their secret with him only lands]
12 (e-fem) И в сердце каждого хранится.         [And in each heart is kept and stays]
13 (G-masc) Так дети стали муж с женой,          [Thus children became man and wife]
14 (G-masc) Но мир не знает их судьбой.          [But world knows not their fate's new life]
```
