**14 lines per stanza**, rhyme scheme: **aBaBccDDeFFeGG**

```
Line  | Rhyme | Type      | Syllables | Stress Pattern
------|-------|-----------|-----------|---------------------------
1     | a     | feminine  | 9         | u — u — u — u — u
2     | B     | masculine | 8         | u — u — u — u —
3     | a     | feminine  | 9         | u — u — u — u — u
4     | B     | masculine | 8         | u — u — u — u —
5     | c     | feminine  | 9         | u — u — u — u — u
6     | c     | feminine  | 9         | u — u — u — u — u
7     | D     | masculine | 8         | u — u — u — u —
8     | D     | masculine | 8         | u — u — u — u —
9     | e     | feminine  | 9         | u — u — u — u — u
10    | F     | masculine | 8         | u — u — u — u —
11    | F     | masculine | 8         | u — u — u — u —
12    | e     | feminine  | 9         | u — u — u — u — u
13    | G     | masculine | 8         | u — u — u — u —
14    | G     | masculine | 8         | u — u — u — u —
```

**Pattern Key**: u = unstressed syllable, — = stressed syllable

### Critical Meter Rules

1. **Position 8 stress MANDATORY**: Final stress must fall on syllable 8 (inviolable)
2. **Odd positions unstressed**: Positions 1, 3, 5, 7 must be unstressed
3. **Pyrrhic variations allowed**: Positions 2, 4, 6 can be unstressed (common)
4. **Feminine endings**: One extra unstressed syllable after syllable 8

### Forbidden Rhymes (ZERO TOLERANCE)

❌ **NEVER use**:
- **Verb infinitives** (-ть, -ти): любить/говорить, знать/сказать
- **Verb conjugations** (-ет, -ит, -ает, -ит): говорит/звонит, знает/играет
- **Verb past tense** (-л, -ла, -ло, -ли): был/забыл, знала/стала (minimize!)
- **Grammatical ending rhymes** (too easy): дома/города, цветы/мечты
- **Nabokov's trite pairs**: любовь/кровь, розы/морозы, мечта/красота

### Rhyme Quality Targets

**Overall Target for Stanzas 16-20**: 45-50% rich rhymes minimum

- **Stanza 16** (Leaving/urgency): 40-45% rich (fast pacing, action-focused)
- **Stanza 17** (Journey/arrival): 45-50% rich (atmospheric, Moscow setting)
- **Stanza 18** (Explanation begins): 40-45% rich (dialogue, practical)
- **Stanza 19** (Revelation/surprise): 45-50% rich (emotional peak)
- **Stanza 20** (Agreement): 45-50% rich (hopeful, spiritual tone)

**Rich Rhyme Definition**: Consonants BEFORE stressed vowel match
- Example: **пр**авда / **пр**авит (právda / právit)
- Example: **к**óшка / **к**рóшка (kóshka / króshka)

## DIALOGUE FRAMEWORKS - CRITICAL GUIDANCE

### The Challenge: Dialogue in Iambic Tetrameter

Rendering natural speech in iambic tetrameter is **EXTREMELY DIFFICULT**. Russian speech rarely falls into perfect metrical patterns. For Stanzas 18-20, use strategic approaches.

### THREE APPROACHES (use strategically)

#### APPROACH 1: HEAVY NARRATOR FRAMING (RECOMMENDED for most dialogue)

**Pattern**: Narrator carries rhyme scheme and meter, brief direct speech (1-3 words max)

**Example Structure**:
```
Роман вздохнул и тихо начал речь:
«Отец, прошу...» — и сердце билось страстно.
```
Translation: Roman sighed and quietly began speech: / "Father, I beg..." - and heart beat passionately.

**Advantages**:
- Narrator's voice carries technical burden
- Brief quoted speech feels natural
- Easier to maintain meter and rhyme
- Can paraphrase longer speeches

**Use for**:
- Stanza 18: Roman's initial explanation
- Stanza 19: Revealing "She's a Kapuletova!"
- Stanza 20: Lavrentiy's agreement

**Template Pattern**:
```
[Narrator setup, 4-6 syllables] + [": «Speech 2-4 syllables»"] + [— narrator continuation to syllable 8/9]
```

#### APPROACH 2: REPORTED SPEECH (EASIER than direct)

**Pattern**: Narrator paraphrases what character said (no quotation marks)

**Example Structure**:
```
Он объяснил, что полюбил он сердцем
Ту, что была недавно незнакомкой.
```
Translation: He explained that he loved with his heart / The one who was recently unknown.

**Advantages**:
- No quotation marks needed
- Narrator's voice throughout
- Easier to compress Shakespeare's speeches
- Maintains poetic elevation

**Use for**:
- Compressing Roman's explanation in Stanza 18
- Reporting Lavrentiy's questions
- Summarizing reasoning and motivation

#### APPROACH 3: DIRECT SPEECH (VERY HARD - use sparingly)

**Pattern**: Character speaks in quotation marks, speech must scan perfectly

**Requirements**:
- EVERY word must scan perfectly in iambic tetrameter
- Rhyme scheme must work
- Must sound natural despite meter
- Formal вы or intimate ты? (Roman uses вы to priest)

**Advantages**:
- Most dramatic
- Character voice most direct
- Shakespearean feel

**Disadvantages**:
- EXTREMELY difficult to maintain meter
- Natural speech resists iambic pattern
- High risk of metrical failure
- Can sound stilted

**Use ONLY when**:
- Very brief speech (3-5 words)
- Perfect scansion verified
- Natural stress pattern matches iambic tetrameter
