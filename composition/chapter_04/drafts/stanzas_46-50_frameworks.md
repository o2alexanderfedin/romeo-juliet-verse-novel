## Technical Requirements (All Stanzas)

### Onegin Stanza Form
- **14 lines** per stanza (inviolable)
- **Rhyme scheme**: aBaBccDDeFFeGG
  - Lowercase (a,b,c,e,f) = **feminine** rhyme (9 syllables, stress on penultimate)
  - Uppercase (B,D,F,G) = **masculine** rhyme (8 syllables, stress on final)
- **Meter**: Iambic tetrameter throughout
- **Line 8 Mandatory**: Final stress on syllable 8 (never violate)

### Forbidden Rhymes (Critical - Zero Tolerance)
❌ **NEVER use**:
- Verb infinitives (-ть, -ти): любить/говорить, сказать/узнать
- Verb conjugations (-ет, -ит, -ает): говорит/звонит, знает/летает
- Verb past tense (-л, -ла, -ло, -ли): сказал/узнал, была/жила (minimize heavily)
- Excessive grammatical endings (too easy/banal)
- Trite pairs: любовь/кровь, розы/морозы, природа/свобода

### Rhyme Quality Targets
- **Configuration A (Ceremonial)**: 45-50% rich rhymes (sacred/formal language)
- **Configuration B (Emotional)**: 45-50% rich rhymes (urgent/tender language)
- **Overall minimum**: 45% rich rhymes across all five stanzas
