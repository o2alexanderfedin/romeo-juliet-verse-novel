## ⚠️ Critical Reminders

### Never Guess Stress!
Russian stress patterns are **unpredictable**. Always verify:
- ё (yo) is ALWAYS stressed (most reliable)
- All other vowels: MUST verify with dictionary
- Wrong stress = broken meter = unusable stanza

### Forbidden Rhymes
❌ NO verb infinitive rhymes (любить/говорить)
❌ NO verb conjugation rhymes (говорит/звонит)
❌ NO grammatical ending rhymes (boring, banal)
❌ NO trite pairs (любовь/кровь, розы/морозы)

✅ Prefer rich rhymes (consonants before stress match)
✅ Mix parts of speech (noun + adjective, etc.)
