# Chapter 8: "The Monument" - Stanzas 11-15 Frameworks
# Truth Revelation - Lavrentiy's Explanation

**Sprint**: 35
**Stanza Range**: 11-15 (5 stanzas)
**Scene**: Lavrentiy explains full story - betrothal, plan, message failure
**Detail Level Distribution**: 3 detailed, 2 outlined (60% detailed)
**Timeline**: Day 5, vault scene continues

---

## Overview: Truth Revealed (Stanzas 11-15)

### Narrative Function
Lavrentiy tells complete story piece by piece:
- St.11: Secret betrothal (how it happened, why secret)
- St.12: The plan (potion, feigned death, message to Roman)
- St.13: Message failure (how/why it failed)
- St.14: Roman's suicide (didn't know, thought she dead)
- St.15: Yulia's suicide (woke, found Roman dead, couldn't live)

Full truth emerges. Families listen, horrified. Each revelation makes tragedy clearer: this was preventable. If no feud, could have married openly. If message arrived, both alive. Chain of accidents and one root cause: the feud.

### Emotional Tone
- Lavrentiy: Guilty, sorrowful, honest (takes responsibility)
- Families: Shocked, horror growing as they understand
- Governor: Listening, judging
- Narrator: Reporting with sorrow

### Narrator Voice
Mostly letting scene play (Lavrentiy's speech), but narrator frames it: "He told them..." "He explained..." "They listened, horror-struck..." Elegiac frame around confession.

### Pacing
MODERATE - Truth unfolds steadily, piece by piece. Not rushed (families need to absorb), not slow (information must flow). Each stanza adds crucial piece.

## Stanza 11: Secret Betrothal Revealed

**Detail Level**: DETAILED (280-350+ stress-marked words, 150-200+ rhyme pairs)

### KEY MOMENT
Lavrentiy speaks: "They loved each other. They came to me. Roman and Yulia. Asked me to marry them in secret. I did. Blessed union in my church, before God. Why secret? Because of your feud. They knew: if they asked openly, you'd refuse. Both families would forbid it. So they came in secret, at night. I married them. They were husband and wife, blessed by God and Church."

Families' reaction: Shock. Married? Their children? Secretly? Kapuletovs: "Yulia was betrothed to Pavel!" Montyagins: "Roman married... a Kapuletova?" Horror and confusion.

### Dramatic Function
**First revelation** - Changes everything. Not just lovers, but married. Sacred bond. Plan makes sense now.

### Rhyme Scheme (Onegin Stanza aBaBccDDeFFeGG)
- Line 1 (a, feminine): Lavrentiy begins
- Line 2 (B, masculine): "They loved"
- Line 3 (a, feminine): came to him
- Line 4 (B, masculine): asked to marry
- Line 5-6 (cc, feminine): he agreed, blessed
- Line 7-8 (DD, masculine): **Line 8 pivot** - "Married in secret because of feud"
- Line 9 (e, feminine): before God
- Line 10-11 (FF, masculine): husband and wife
- Line 12 (e, feminine): families' shock
- Line 13-14 (GG, masculine): revelation's impact
