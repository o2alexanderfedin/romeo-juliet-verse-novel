# Chapter 8: "The Monument" - Stanzas 1-5 Frameworks
# Discovery Scene

**Sprint**: 35
**Stanza Range**: 1-5 (5 stanzas)
**Scene**: Bodies discovered in vault, Governor summoned, families called
**Detail Level Distribution**: 3 detailed, 2 outlined (60% detailed)
**Timeline**: Day 5, morning after deaths

---

## Overview: Discovery Scene (Stanzas 1-5)

### Narrative Function
Bodies found in Kapulettis' vault. Authorities summoned. Both families called to scene. Shock, confusion, grief. "How did this happen?" No one knows yet. Sets up truth revelation (St.10-14).

### Emotional Tone
- Shock (bodies found embracing)
- Grief (both young, both beautiful, both dead)
- Confusion (no wounds visible on Yulia, Roman's empty vial mysterious)
- Dread (both families must be told)
- Elegiac (aftermath has begun, violence over, only sorrow remains)

### Narrator Voice
Elegiac, sorrowful, reporting but sympathetic. No longer anguished (as in Ch.7) but mournful. Knows whole story, reader knows, now families will learn. Tone: "And so it was discovered..."

### Pacing
MODERATE - Not rushed (tragedy already happened), not slow (families need to be called, truth will emerge). Discovery itself can be atmospheric, but move steadily toward families' arrival.

## Stanza 1: Discovery - The Bodies Found

**Detail Level**: DETAILED (280-350+ stress-marked words, 150-200+ rhyme pairs)

### KEY MOMENT
Morning, Day 5. Servant or family member (perhaps checking vault for some reason, or Lavrentiy arriving to check on Yulia's condition) enters vault. Finds two bodies: Roman and Yulia, embracing, both dead, both cold. Shock. Horror. Runs to alert household/authorities.

**Discoverer**: Could be:
- Servant checking vault (routine or sent by someone)
- Lavrentiy (coming to see if Yulia has woken - discovers horror)
- Family member (Kapuletov checking on daughter)

**Visual**: Two young people, beautiful even in death, embracing on marble floor. Yulia's hand still holds Roman's. Roman's face peaceful (poison quick). Yulia's face tear-stained but serene. Both cold. Morning light through vault opening (if any). Candles burned down.

### Dramatic Irony
NONE - Reader knows, narrator knows, discoverer learns. Irony phase over. Now: revelation and aftermath phase.
