- **Following**: Stanzas 41-45 - Governor's judgment (exile not execution), Yulia learns news (cousin dead, husband killed him, husband exiled), Yulia chooses Roman over family

- Second death in same afternoon - Tikhon falls

- Realization: killed Yulia's cousin, now kinsman by secret marriage
- Public spectacle - crowd witnesses second duel, second death
- Benedikt's practical intervention - urges flight before authorities arrive
- Roman flees - from murder scene, from Moscow, from life he knew
