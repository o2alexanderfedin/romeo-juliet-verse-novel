## Overview: Stanzas 41-45

**Narrative Context**:
- **Preceding**: Stanzas 36-40 - Roman kills Tikhon, Tikhon dies, Roman's horror, crowd witnesses, Benedikt urges flight, Roman flees
- **Current Section**: Time passes (hours/day), Governor investigates two deaths, decrees exile not execution, Yulia learns terrible news (cousin dead by husband's hand, husband exiled), her heart torn (family vs. love), she chooses Roman (love over duty)
- **Following**: Stanzas 46-60 (Sprint 24) - Final meeting between Roman and Yulia, consummation of marriage, dawn parting, Roman leaves for exile

**Key Dramatic Elements**:
- Governor's judgment: serious crime (noble killed), but mitigating factors (Tikhon provoked, killed Merkury first)
- Exile, not execution - mercy within justice
- Yulia's impossible position: cousin murdered, murderer is husband
- Family duty vs. love - the choice that defines her
- Yulia chooses Roman - mature decision, accepts consequences
- Prepares for final meeting before exile

**Tone**: Judicial (st.41-42), then tragic and intimate (st.43-45), resolution and choice
