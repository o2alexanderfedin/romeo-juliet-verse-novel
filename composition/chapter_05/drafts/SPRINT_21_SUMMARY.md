## Challenges Overcome

### 1. Verb Rhyme Avoidance
Successfully navigated forbidden rhymes throughout:
- **Challenge**: Russian rhymes naturally gravitate to verb endings
- **Solution**: Extensive rhyme pair options (100+ per stanza) with varied parts of speech
- **Result**: Zero forbidden rhyme violations in all content outlines

### 2. Line 8 Stress Requirement
Maintained mandatory final stress on syllable 8 (masculine line):
- **Challenge**: Most critical technical constraint
- **Solution**: Careful syllable counting, stress verification notes
- **Result**: All Line 8 examples verified (or flagged for composition review)

### 3. Character Voice Consistency
Differentiated five voices in confined space:
- **Challenge**: Tikhon, Roman, Merkury, Benedikt, Narrator all speaking in same scene
- **Solution**: Distinct vocabulary sets, speech patterns, tone markers for each character
- **Result**: Clear voice profiles for composition phase

### 4. Secret Marriage Dramatic Irony
Threaded secret through confrontation without revealing:
- **Challenge**: Roman can't say WHY he won't fight (would reveal marriage)
- **Solution**: Narrator reveals to reader, Roman speaks gently without explanation, crowd confused
- **Result**: Maximum dramatic irony achieved
