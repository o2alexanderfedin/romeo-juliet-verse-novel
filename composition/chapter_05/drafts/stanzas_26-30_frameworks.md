**Narrative Scope**: Merkury's actual death, immediate grief, narrator's mourning, reflection on first death
**Dramatic Arc**: Final breath → Death → Shock/silence → Grief erupts → Philosophical mourning → Transition to rage
**Emotional Tone**: Tragic, grieving, philosophical, shocked, transitioning from sorrow to anger
**Key Moment**: Stanza 26 - Merkury dies (spirit departs, life extinguished)

**Context**:

- **Current** (St. 26-30): Merkury's death, immediate aftermath, narrator's mourning
- **Following** (St. 31-35): Roman's anguished rage, challenges Tikhon for second duel

## Technical Requirements (All Stanzas)

### Onegin Stanza Form
- **14 lines** per stanza (inviolable)
- **Rhyme scheme**: aBaBccDDeFFeGG
  - Lowercase (a,b,c,e,f) = feminine rhyme (9 syllables, stress penultimate)
  - Uppercase (B,D,F,G) = masculine rhyme (8 syllables, stress final)
- **Meter**: Iambic tetrameter throughout
- **Line 8 Mandatory**: Final stress on syllable 8 (never violate)

### Forbidden Rhymes (Critical - Zero Tolerance)
❌ **NEVER use**:
- Verb infinitives (-ть, -ти): любить/говорить
- Verb conjugations (-ет, -ит, -ает): говорит/звенит
- Verb past tense (-л, -ла, -ло): сказал/узнал (minimize heavily)
- Excessive grammatical endings
- Trite pairs: любовь/кровь, розы/морозы

### Rhyme Quality Targets
- **Chapter 5 narrative sections**: 45-50% rich rhymes
- **Digressive sections** (St. 29-30): 50-60% rich rhymes (elevated for philosophical content)

## STANZA 26: Merkury Dies - "Spirit Departs" (FULLY DETAILED)

### Content Outline (aBaBccDDeFFeGG)

**Line-by-Line Structure**:

```
1  (a-fem) Меркурий умер — дух покинул тело хладно      [Merkury died — spirit left body coldly]
2  (B-masc) Последний вздох — и жизни свет исчез        [Last breath — and life's light vanished]
3  (a-fem) Глаза́ открыты, но душа́ ушла́ — безжалостно  [Eyes open, but soul departed — mercilessly]
4  (B-masc) Рома́н держа́л друга́ — но друг уж нем и слеп [Roman held friend — but friend already mute and blind]
5  (c-fem) Молчание толпы — как камень, тяжелое         [Silence of crowd — like stone, heavy]
6  (c-fem) Никто не смел дохнуть — мгновение священно   [No one dared breathe — moment sacred]
7  (D-masc) Смерть входит тихо — но её присутствие       [Death enters quietly — but her presence]
8  (D-masc) Ощущает каждый — холод, мрак, конец          [Feels everyone — cold, darkness, end]
9  (e-fem) Меркурий — остроумец, друг, весёлый витязь   [Merkury — wit, friend, merry knight]
10 (F-masc) Теперь лишь труп — что на руках лежит        [Now only corpse — that in arms lies]
11 (e-fem) Его улыбка смолкла — голос смолк навеки      [His smile silenced — voice stilled forever]
12 (F-masc) Весь мир беднее стал — когда он погиб        [Whole world became poorer — when he perished]
13 (G-masc) Роман рыдал — но слёзы не вернут живого      [Roman sobbed — but tears won't return the living]
14 (G-masc) Меркурий мёртв — и первый умер, молодого    [Merkury dead — and first died, the young one]
```

**Meter**:
- [ ] Each line 8 syllables (or 9 for feminine rhyme)

- [ ] No stressed syllables on odd positions (1, 3, 5, 7)

**Rhyme**:
- [ ] Scheme aBaBccDDeFFeGG correct
- [ ] Feminine rhymes (a,b,c,e,f): Penultimate stress, 9 syllables
- [ ] Masculine rhymes (B,D,F,G): Final stress, 8 syllables
- [ ] NO infinitives (-ть/-ти) in rhyme positions
- [ ] NO verb conjugations (-ит/-ет/-ает) in rhyme positions
- [ ] Rich rhymes 45-50% (narrative) or 50-60% (digressive)

**Voice**:

- [ ] Vocabulary appropriate to speaker

- [ ] Pacing appropriate (slow for St.26-27, medium St.28-30)
