## Technical Requirements (All Stanzas)

### Onegin Stanza Form
- **14 lines** per stanza (inviolable)
- **Rhyme scheme**: aBaBccDDeFFeGG
  - Lowercase (a,b,c,e,f) = feminine rhyme (9 syllables, stress penultimate)
  - Uppercase (B,D,F,G) = masculine rhyme (8 syllables, stress final)
- **Meter**: Iambic tetrameter throughout
- **Line 8 Mandatory**: Final stress on syllable 8 (never violate)

### Forbidden Rhymes (Critical - Zero Tolerance)
❌ **NEVER use**:
- Verb infinitives (-ть, -ти): любить/говорить
- Verb conjugations (-ет, -ит, -ает): говорит/звенит
- Verb past tense (-л, -ла, -ло): сказал/узнал (minimize heavily)
- Excessive grammatical endings
- Trite pairs: любовь/кровь, розы/морозы

### Rhyme Quality Targets
- **Chapter 5 narrative sections**: 45-50% rich rhymes (consonants before stress match)
