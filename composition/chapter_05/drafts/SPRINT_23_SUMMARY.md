**Approach**:

**Result**:
- ⚠ **St.31, Line 9**: Infinitive разъединить - FLAGGED for revision
- ⚠ **St.31, Line 12**: Verb conjugation висит - FLAGGED for complete revision
- ⚠ **St.36, Lines multiple**: Past-tense verbs and rhyme scheme mismatches - FLAGGED
- ⚠ **St.41, Line 9**: Infinitive помиловать - FLAGGED for complete revision
- ⚠ **Multiple lines**: Past-tense -л and -ла endings - flagged to reduce reliance

**Lesson**: Detailed stanzas need one more revision pass focused exclusively on eliminating forbidden rhymes

### 2. Rhyme Scheme Accuracy (Needs Improvement)

**Challenge**: aBaBccDDeFFeGG requires careful attention to feminine (9 syllables, penultimate stress) vs. masculine (8 syllables, final stress) rhymes

**Issues Found**:
- St.31: Lines 1, 3 given masculine rhymes when feminine required
- St.31: Line 10 given feminine rhyme when masculine required
- St.36: Similar feminine/masculine mismatches
- St.41: Multiple lines with incorrect rhyme gender

**Solution**: Flagged all mismatches with ACTION REQUIRED notes and better rhyme options provided

**Lesson**: Must verify rhyme scheme BEFORE generating detailed content, not after

### 3. Line 8 Stress Requirement (Vigilance Maintained)

**Challenge**: Line 8 MUST have final stress on syllable 8 (most critical technical constraint)

**Approach**: Flagged every Line 8 in detailed stanzas for verification

**Result**:

- St.36, Line 8: "зле" - FLAGGED for syllable count and stress verification
- St.41, Line 8: "закон" - FLAGGED for syllable count verification

**Lesson**: Line 8 verification essential during composition phase, cannot be skipped

## Lessons Learned

### What Worked Well

1. **Direct framework file creation**: Confirmed as most efficient approach (from Sprint 21)
2. **Consistent quality template**: Using Sprint 21 model maintained standards
3. **Character voice development**: Comprehensive notes will guide composition effectively
4. **Narrative arc planning**: 15-stanza outline provides clear dramatic structure
5. **Thematic vocabulary organization**: Stress-marked words grouped by theme highly useful

### Process Improvements Needed

1. **Rhyme scheme verification FIRST**: Must verify feminine/masculine rhyme pattern BEFORE drafting detailed content outlines
2. **Forbidden rhyme vigilance DURING drafting**: Easier to avoid infinitives/conjugations initially than revise later
3. **Line 8 stress checking DURING drafting**: Should verify immediately, not flag for later
4. **Reduce past-tense verb rhymes**: Provide more noun/adjective options, fewer verb options
5. **Revision pass protocol**: Detailed stanzas should receive one focused revision pass for technical compliance before considering "complete"

### Technical Notes for Future Sprints

- **Infinitives**: NEVER end rhyme lines with -ть/-ти verbs (любить, помиловать, разъединить)
- **Conjugations**: NEVER end rhyme lines with -ит/-ет verb forms (висит, говорит)
- **Past tense**: USE SPARINGLY, prefer nouns/adjectives (exceptions: dramatic moments where verb conveys essential action)
- **Rhyme scheme**: Verify gender (feminine/masculine) matches scheme BEFORE drafting content
- **Line 8**: Most critical constraint - verify syllable count = 8, final stress mandatory
- **Rich rhyme target**: 45-50% for Chapter 5 narrative sections (match consonants before stressed vowel)
