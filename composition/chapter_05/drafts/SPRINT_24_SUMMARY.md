## Lessons Learned

### What Worked Well

1. **Forbidden rhyme vigilance DURING drafting**: Checking for infinitives/conjugations WHILE drafting St.46 (not after) prevented all violations - major success
2. **Tasteful consummation guidance**: Detailed notes on Pushkin's approach provides clear roadmap for composer
3. **Cultural adaptation research**: Russian birds (жаворонок/соловей) adds authentic Russian flavor to adapted R&J scene
4. **Chapter conclusion planning**: St.60 structure (recap, reflection, transition) provides powerful ending
5. **Direct framework creation**: Consistent efficient approach (Sprints 21, 23, 24)

### Process Improvements for Future Sprints

1. **Rhyme scheme verification DURING drafting**: Check feminine vs. masculine WHILE writing line, not after (would have caught Lines 6, 11, 13 earlier)
2. **Continue forbidden rhyme vigilance**: St.46 zero violations proves this works - maintain for all detailed stanzas
3. **Outline quality sufficient**: St.47-60 outlines provide enough structure for composition without full detail
4. **Special scene handling**: Notes like St.51 (consummation) and St.54 (lark/nightingale) very helpful for unique scenes
5. **Maintain cultural authenticity**: Research Russian equivalents (birds, etc.) strengthens adaptation

### Technical Notes for Future Sprints

- **Infinitives**: NEVER end rhyme lines with -ть/-ти verbs ✓ ACHIEVED in St.46
- **Conjugations**: NEVER end rhyme lines with -ит/-ет forms ✓ ACHIEVED in St.46
- **Past tense**: USE SPARINGLY in narrative (acceptable context, not rhyme crutch) ✓ Minimal in St.46
- **Rhyme scheme**: CHECK gender (feminine/masculine) DURING drafting (improvement needed)
