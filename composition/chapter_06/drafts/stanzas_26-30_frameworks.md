### Content Summary
Lavrentiy listens to Yulia's desperate plea. He sees her anguish, recognizes her determination to die rather than marry Paris. As a priest, he feels duty to save her soul from mortal sin. He sees her desperation is absolute. He decides he must help her - there is one way, dangerous but possible.

## Stanza 27: THE SLEEPING POTION PLAN (FULLY DETAILED)

### **KEY MOMENT**: The Plan Revealed
**This is the most critical stanza in the sprint** - Lavrentiy presents the sleeping potion plan that will set the tragedy in motion. The priest explains: drink this potion, you will appear dead, your family will mourn and place you in the vault, I will send word to Roman, he will come and rescue you. The plan seems perfect in its simplicity - but the narrator and reader know it is doomed.

### 14-Line Stanza Structure with Content Guidance

**Lines 1-4 (aBaB - alternating feminine/masculine):**
- Line 1 (feminine): Lavrentiy begins - "There IS a way..." (dramatic moment)
- Line 2 (masculine): His voice careful, measured (priest-like authority)
- Line 3 (feminine): He has a potion, ancient knowledge (medical authority)
- Line 4 (masculine): This potion will save her life (hope offered)

**Lines 5-8 (ccDD - couplets, both feminine then both masculine):**
- Line 5 (feminine): The potion's effect - will make her seem dead
- Line 6 (feminine): Cold, breathless, like a corpse (death appearance)
- Line 7 (masculine): Family will mourn, believe she's gone (deception works)
- Line 8 (masculine): They'll place her in the family vault (MANDATORY: 8 syllables, stress on 8th)

**Lines 9-12 (eFFeG - alternating pattern):**
- Line 9 (feminine): Meanwhile, Lavrentiy sends message to Roman (communication plan)
- Line 10 (masculine): Roman learns the truth, the secret plan (knowledge transfer)
- Line 11 (masculine): Roman returns from exile in secret (rescue mission)
- Line 12 (feminine): Opens the vault, finds her waking (reunion planned)

**Lines 13-14 (GG - masculine couplet, strong closure):**
- Line 13 (masculine): Together they escape, start new life (happy ending promised)
- Line 14 (masculine): Simple plan - what could possibly go wrong? (dramatic irony, narrator's dark hint)

### Character Voice Notes
**Lavrentiy speaking**: Careful, measured, priest-like authority. Explains clearly but also showing hubris - believes he can control fate, outsmart death itself. Compassionate but overconfident. His voice should sound wise and reassuring, but narrator's framing reveals the dangerous pride beneath.

### Narrator Guidance
**Dramatic irony begins strongly here**: Reader knows this plan will fail catastrophically. Narrator should frame Lavrentiy's confidence with subtle hints of doom. The priest's certainty contrasts with narrator's knowledge. Everything seems logical, but fate is already laughing.
