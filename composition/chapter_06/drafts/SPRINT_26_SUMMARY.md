## Lessons Learned

### What Worked Well

1. **Parallel Task Execution**: Creating 3 framework files concurrently was highly efficient (~2 hours vs. sequential ~6 hours)

2. **Sprint 25 Quality Template**: Following Chapter 5 standards ensured immediate technical compliance (zero forbidden rhymes from the start)

3. **Strategic Detailing**: Fully detailing key pivot moments (St.1 opening, St.7 decision, St.12 climax) while outlining others balances quality and efficiency

4. **Thematic Vocabulary Organization**: Grouping words by theme (13 categories in St.12) helps composers find appropriate diction quickly

5. **Cultural Research Integration**: 19th-century Russian merchant class context adds authenticity beyond simple translation

### Challenges Encountered

1. **Dramatic Irony Complexity**: Yulia's dual grief (public/private) requires careful vocabulary to distinguish visible vs. hidden emotions

2. **Patriarchal Violence Tone**: Capturing Grigory's rage without making him a cartoon villain - he's typical, not exceptional

3. **Narrator's Dual Role**: Balancing narrative momentum (St.1-14) with digressive commentary (St.15) - kept digression to single stanza

4. **Rich Rhyme Targets**: Achieving 45-50% rich rhymes while avoiding forbidden forms requires extensive vocabulary preparation

5. **Mother's Ineffectualness**: Matryona appears briefly (St.13) - need to develop her voice more in future stanzas

### Process Improvements

**For Future Sprints**:

1. **Pre-Sprint Character Voice Profiles**: Create detailed voice guidelines for all major characters before drafting (Grigory, Yulia, Matryona)

2. **Cultural Context Checklists**: Develop checklist of 19th-century Russian customs per scene type (mourning, marriage announcement, domestic confrontation)

3. **Dramatic Arc Templates**: Use 3-act structure templates for 15-stanza units (5-5-5 pattern works well: setup, build, climax/commentary)

4. **Rhyme Pair Quality Tiers**: Categorize rhyme pairs by quality (Rich/Exact/Acceptable) to help composers prioritize

5. **Line 8 Pre-Verification**: Check Line 8 stress during framework creation, not after (prevents revision cycles)
