# Chapter 6, Stanzas 1-5: Opening - Mourning and Hidden Grief

## Overview

**Timeline**: Day 4 morning (day after Roman's exile)
**Setting**: Capulet household in Moscow
**Tone**: Somber, grieving, tense, dual-layered (public mourning vs. private grief)
**Romeo & Juliet Parallel**: Act III Scene 4-5 - Capulet household after Tybalt's death

**Narrative Arc (St. 1-5)**:
- St. 1: Chapter 6 opens - tone shift from violence to mourning, Day 4 morning
- St. 2: Capulet household in mourning - Tikhon's body, grief, funeral preparations
- St. 3: Grigory's rage - nephew killed by Montyagin, vows revenge
- St. 4: Yulia's dual grief - publicly mourns cousin, privately grieves exiled husband
- St. 5: Secret marriage hidden - cannot reveal true feelings, inner turmoil, isolation
