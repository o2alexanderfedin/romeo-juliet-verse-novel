# Chapter 6, Stanzas 11-15: Framework
## Yulia's Protest and Grigory's Rage

**Narrative Context**: Following the marriage announcement (Thursday, two days), Yulia defies her father for the first time. Grigory's patriarchal fury explodes. This sequence parallels *Romeo and Juliet* Act III Scene 5, where Capulet rages at Juliet's refusal.

**Emotional Arc**: Daughter's desperate protest → Father's explosive rage → Violent confrontation → Powerlessness revealed → Narrator's moral commentary

**Key Dramatic Beats**:
1. **St. 11**: Yulia's first defiance - "I cannot marry Pavel"
2. **St. 12**: Grigory's rage - patriarchal authority challenged, fury unleashed
3. **St. 13**: Confrontation escalates - threats, possible violence, mother ineffectual
4. **St. 14**: Yulia's powerlessness - no rights, must obey or face consequences
5. **St. 15**: Narrator's digression - arranged marriage critique, social sympathy

## Stanza 11: Yulia's Protest
### Content Summary

**Lines 1-4 (Quatrain 1, aBaB)**: Yulia finds her voice - first time opposing father directly. Trembling but resolute: "Я не могу́... я не люблю́ Павла́" (I cannot... I don't love Pavel). Fear battles with desperation.

**Lines 5-8 (Quatrain 2, ccDD)**: Her words tumble out - mentions Romeo (dangerous!), speaks of her heart, her feelings. Grigory's face darkens. Air in room grows heavy, ominous.

**Lines 9-14 (Sextet, eFFeGG)**: Matryona gasps, tries to intervene weakly. But Yulia has crossed the line - daughter's will against father's command. The unthinkable has occurred. Storm gathers.

## Stanza 12: Grigory's Rage (FULLY DETAILED)
### Complete 14-Line Content Outline

**Rhyme Scheme**: aBaBccDDeFFeGG

**Line 1 (a, feminine)**: Grigory's face transforms - fury erupts like volcano. "Непокóрная!" (Disobedient one!)

**Line 2 (B, masculine)**: His fist slams table, dishes crash, everyone jumps in terror.

**Line 3 (a, feminine)**: Years of unchallenged authority - daughter dares refuse his will?!

**Line 4 (B, masculine)**: Voice thunders through house like storm breaking over mountain pass.

**Line 5 (c, feminine)**: "Ты забыла́сь! Ты мнишь себя́ боя́рыней?" (You forget yourself! You think yourself a noblewoman?)

**Line 6 (c, feminine)**: "Твоя́ судьба́ - что я реши́л, не бо́льше!" (Your fate - what I decided, nothing more!)

**Line 7 (D, masculine)**: He rises from chair - massive, threatening, patriarchal wrath incarnate.

**Line 8 (D, masculine)**: Matryona cowers, servants flee from room in fright.

**Line 9 (e, feminine)**: "В четве́рг - свадьба́! Смиря́йся и́ли в мона́стырь!" (Thursday - wedding! Submit or to monastery!)

**Line 10 (F, masculine)**: Alternative clear: obey or banishment, life destroyed either way.

**Line 11 (F, masculine)**: No tenderness remains - only merchant's pride wounded, authority defied.

**Line 12 (e, feminine)**: Yulia shrinks back, tears streaming, but still whispers "Не могу́..." (I cannot...)

**Line 13 (G, masculine)**: This defiance fans flames higher - Grigory advances, hand raised.

**Line 14 (G, masculine)**: Moment frozen: will father strike daughter? Violence hangs in air.
