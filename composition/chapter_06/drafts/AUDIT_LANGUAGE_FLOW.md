#### Exact Rhymes (Phonetic Precision)
**All provided pairs meet exact rhyme standard**: Stress vowel + all following sounds match phonetically.

**Critical Check**: ё (yo) always stressed - used correctly throughout (Матрёна, зелье, etc.)

#### Weak Rhymes (Borderline Acceptable)

**Identified Weak Rhymes** (use sparingly):

1. **Feminine endings with only vowel match, poor consonant support**:
   - скры́ла / откры́ла (St.46) - grammatical pair, verb stems ❌ AVOID
   - реши́ла / верши́ла (St.32) - verb conjugations ❌ AVOID
   - забы́ла / бы́ла (St.46) - common weak pair ⚠️ USE SPARINGLY

2. **Masculine endings that are too common/trite**:
   - лёд / мёд (appears multiple times) - very common ⚠️ OVERUSED
   - сон / он (appears multiple times) - too simple ⚠️ OVERUSED
   - день / тень (appears multiple times) - trite pair ⚠️ OVERUSED

**Recommendation**: Replace 10-15% of weak rhymes with richer alternatives during composition.

- **Issue**: A few verb conjugation pairs slip through (умрёт/живёт in St.27)
- **Impact**: VERY MINOR - isolated cases, contextually justified
- **Fix**: Verify each verse line; if verb rhyme, ensure it's rich (consonants match) or replace

- **Issue**: Frameworks assume stress marking will be verified during composition
