### Definition and Importance

**Dramatic Irony**: Reader knows information characters don't know, creating tension and tragedy.

**Chapter 7 Dramatic Irony Core**:
- **Reader Knows**: Yulia ALIVE (sleeping potion), will wake soon, Lavrentiy's plan worked
- **Roman Knows**: Yulia DEAD (saw funeral), believes servant, must die with her
- **Result**: Every action Roman takes toward "reunion" = action toward tragedy
