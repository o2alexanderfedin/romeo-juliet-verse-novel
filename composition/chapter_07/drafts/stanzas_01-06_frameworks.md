# Chapter 7, Stanzas 1-6: Roman in Exile and Message Failure

## Overview

**Narrative Arc**: Roman's anxious exile and the tragic failure of Lavrentiy's message to reach him
**Romeo & Juliet Parallel**: Act V Scene 1 - Romeo in Mantua, Friar John's failed message
**Critical Plot Point**: The message about Yulia's feigned death DOES NOT reach Roman
**Dramatic Irony**: Reader knows the truth; Roman does not; catastrophic consequences imminent

**Pacing**: SLOW build of anxiety (St.1-3), then SUDDEN shift to failed communication (St.4-6)

---

## Stanza 1: Roman's Exile - Waiting in Darkness

**Detail Level**: DETAILED (280-350+ stress-marked words, 150-200+ rhyme pairs)

### KEY MOMENT
Roman in exile (Мантуя/Mantua equivalent), consumed by longing and dread. He replays his last moments with Yulia, oscillates between hope that Lavrentiy's plan will work and terror that all is lost. The stanza establishes his isolation, his desperate need for news, and his inability to act.

### Structure Guidance (aBaBccDDeFFeGG)

**Lines 1-4 (aBaB - alternating feminine/masculine rhymes)**
- L1 (a-fem): Set scene - Roman in exile, dark room/lonely place
- L2 (B-masc): His state - sleepless, pacing, consumed by thought
- L3 (a-fem): Temporal marker - days since exile, no word from Verona
- L4 (B-masc): Emotional state - hope vs. despair warring within

**Lines 5-8 (ccDD - coupled feminine, coupled masculine)**
- L5-6 (cc-fem): Yulia's image haunts him - her voice, her touch, last embrace
- L7-8 (DD-masc): His guilt - he caused her suffering by killing Tibaldo

**Lines 9-12 (eFFe - alternating, with coupled masculine center)**
- L9 (e-fem): His hope - Lavrentiy's plan must work, they will reunite
- L10-11 (FF-masc): His fear - what if message fails? what if she's forced to marry?
- L12 (e-fem): His helplessness - trapped in exile, unable to act

**Lines 13-14 (GG - heroic couplet, masculine)**
- Summary/turn: He waits for messenger, every shadow might bring news
- Or: His heart lives only in hope of word from Verona
