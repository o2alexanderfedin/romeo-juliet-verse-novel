# Chapter 7, Stanzas 13-15: The Apothecary Scene - Framework

**Timeline**: Day 5, after Roman's decision to die
**Scene**: Roman seeks and obtains poison from desperate apothecary
**Critical Element**: Apothecary as tragic victim of poverty, not villain
**Romeo & Juliet Parallel**: Act V Scene 1 - "My poverty but not my will consents"

**Detail Level Distribution**:
- **Stanza 13**: DETAILED - Roman seeks apothecary
- **Stanza 14**: DETAILED - Apothecary's poverty and moral conflict (potential digression)
- **Stanza 15**: OUTLINED - Transaction and Roman's departure

---

## STANZA 13: THE SEARCH (DETAILED)

**KEY MOMENT**: Roman seeks apothecary in poor district, knowing poverty creates black market for illegal poison

### 14-Line Stanza Structure (aBaBccDDeFFeGG)

**Lines 1-4 (Alternating aBaB)**: Roman's purposeful search in poor quarter
- **Line 1 (a-fem)**: Roman walks through poorest district of city
- **Line 2 (B-masc)**: Knows where desperation lives, creates black market
- **Line 3 (a-fem)**: Past shops shuttered, poverty visible everywhere
- **Line 4 (B-masc)**: Seeks apothecary desperate enough to risk law

**Lines 5-8 (Coupled ccDD)**: Finding the apothecary's shop
- **Lines 5-6 (cc-fem)**: Small shop, shabby, sign barely legible / Interior dim, herbs dried, bottles empty
- **Lines 7-8 (DD-masc)**: Apothecary gaunt, hollow-eyed, marks of hunger / Roman sees desperation needed for his plan

**Lines 9-12 (Alternating eFFeE)**: Roman enters, begins negotiation
- **Line 9 (e-fem)**: Roman enters quietly, apothecary looks up fearfully
- **Line 10 (F-masc)**: "I seek something rare," voice low and careful
- **Line 11 (F-masc)**: Apothecary tenses, knows what illegal trade means
- **Line 12 (e-fem)**: But hunger makes men brave where law makes cowardly

**Lines 13-14 (Couplet GG-masc)**: Roman's direct request
- **Line 13 (G-masc)**: "I need poison, strong and fast, will pay gold"
- **Line 14 (G-masc)**: Apothecary pales but doesn't refuse outright - need holds

## STANZA 14: THE APOTHECARY'S DILEMMA (DETAILED) - Potential Digression

**KEY MOMENT**: Apothecary's internal conflict - poverty vs. law vs. conscience. OPTION for brief DIGRESSION on poverty/system cruelty.

### 14-Line Stanza Structure (aBaBccDDeFFeGG)

### APPROACH A: Direct Narrative (No Digression)

## THEMATIC NOTES: POVERTY AS VIOLENCE

**Core Theme**: System that creates impossible choices is the true villain

### Key Thematic Elements:

#### 1. STRUCTURAL VIOLENCE
- **Wealth inequality** forces good people into crime
- **Law punishes survival** (selling poison = death penalty, but family starves legally)
- **Moral responsibility** — who's guilty? Apothecary? Roman? System?
- **"My poverty but not my will consents"** — William Shakespeare, Romeo & Juliet Act V.1

#### 2. PARALLEL TRAGEDIES
- **Roman**: Forced to die by social system (marriage laws, family feuds)
- **Apothecary**: Forced to facilitate death by economic system
- **Both victims**: Neither chose their circumstances
- **Mutual understanding**: Two desperate men, different desperations, same cage

#### 3. DIGRESSION OPPORTUNITIES (St.14 or 15)

**Option A: On Poverty & Law (St.14, lines 5-10)**
- How society criminalizes survival
- Wealthy moralize about virtue while poor face impossible choices
- System creates criminals, then punishes them
- Hypocrisy of "law and order" that maintains inequality

**Option B: On Gold's Power (St.15)**
- Gold speaks louder than conscience
- Poverty is stronger negotiator than any argument
- Weight of coins vs. weight of souls
- "Презрéнный метáлл" rules all

**Tone of Digression** (if used):
- **Bitter but compassionate** — anger at system, not at victim
- **Philosophical** — examining structures, not individuals
- **Pushkin-like** — wry observation of human nature under pressure
- **NOT preachy** — show through specific case, not abstract lecture

#### 4. MORAL COMPLEXITY
- **No clear villains** — even those who enable tragedy are sympathetic
- **Apothecary sells poison** — to feed children
- **Roman buys poison** — to escape impossible situation
- **Both "wrong"** — both understandable
- **Reader's judgment suspended** — system guilty, not individuals

#### 5. NARRATIVE FUNCTION
- **Slows pacing** — moment of quiet reflection before Roman's death
- **Humanizes secondary character** — apothecary memorable, not just plot device
- **Echoes R&J** — familiar scene, culturally adapted to Russian context
- **Thematic reinforcement** — every character trapped by society
