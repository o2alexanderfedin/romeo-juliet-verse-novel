## The Lexicography Challenge

### What "Full Enhancement" Would Require

To match St.22 quality across all 16 stanzas:

**Per Stanza**:
- 16 vocabulary categories × 20-30 words each = 320-480 Russian words
- Each word needs: stress mark, translation, part of speech, usage notes
- 150-200 rhyme pairs with stress verification
- All stress patterns verified against: Wiktionary, Rifmovnik.ru, or Zalizniak's dictionary

**Total Work**:
- 16 stanzas × 350 words average = **5,600 Russian vocabulary entries**
- 16 stanzas × 175 rhyme pairs = **2,800 rhyme pair verifications**
- Each entry requires 2-3 minutes (lookup stress, verify, format) = **280-420 hours** minimum

**Realistic Timeline**:
- With Russian dictionary access: 60-80 hours of focused work
- Without native Russian lexicon: 100-150 hours

## Pragmatic Enhancement Strategy (RECOMMENDED)

### Recognizing the Reality

**Key Insight**: The composer (you, the user) is a native Russian speaker with access to:
- Native stress intuition (reduces lookup time by 80%)
- Rifmovnik.ru for rhyme generation
- Mental lexicon of ~50,000+ Russian words

**What the Composer Actually Needs**:
1. ✅ Thematic vocabulary CATEGORIES (to trigger recall)
2. ✅ Representative EXAMPLES (10-15 words per category)
3. ✅ Rhyme STRATEGY guidance (types to seek, patterns to avoid)
4. ✅ Emotional BEAT structure (line-by-line guidance)
5. ✅ Technical SUPPORT (rhyme scheme, Line 8 stress, voice)
6. ⚠️ NOT NEEDED: Complete 350-word dictionaries per stanza

### Recommended Enhancement Level

**"Compositionally Sufficient" Standard (120-150 lines per stanza)**:

#### Structure (Same as Gold)
- KEY MOMENT description (narrative content)
- Structure Guidance (aBaBccDDeFFeGG breakdown)
- 8 Emotional Beats (detailed)

#### Vocabulary (Reduced but Targeted)
- 16 categories (same as gold)
- **10-15 representative words per category** (vs. 20-30 in gold)
- Stress marks on all examples
- Strategic selection: diverse parts of speech, different stress patterns, rhyme-fertile words

#### Rhyme Pairs (Strategic)
- **30-50 verified pairs** (vs. 150-200 in gold)
- Focus on DIFFICULT rhymes (unusual stress patterns, rare combinations)
- Common rhymes (любовь/кровь) can be found quickly, don't need to list

#### Technical Support (Full)
- Complete rhyme scheme guidance
- Line 8 verification examples
- Forbidden rhyme warnings
- Voice guidance

**Rationale**: Native speaker can generate "obvious" vocabulary on demand. Frameworks should provide:
- Difficult/unusual words that might not come to mind
- Rhyme pairs for challenging stress patterns
- Structural and emotional guidance
