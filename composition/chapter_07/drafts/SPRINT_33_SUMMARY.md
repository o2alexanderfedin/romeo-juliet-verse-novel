## Lessons Learned

### What Worked Exceptionally Well

1. **Psychological Extension**: Extending Juliet's decision (5 Shakespeare lines → 15 stanzas) provides depth without losing momentum
2. **Elegiac Tone Consistency**: Sorrowful, respectful, not sensational throughout death scenes
3. **Love > Life Theme**: Central theme explored deeply without being preachy
4. **Yulia's Autonomy**: Clear she makes conscious choice, not impulsive/mad, increases tragic power
5. **Chapter Conclusion Balance**: Philosophical reflection without over-moralizing, prepares for aftermath

### Best Practices Reinforced

1. **Critical Scenes = High Detail**: Death scenes (St.38-42) 83% detailed ensures quality
2. **Forbidden Rhyme Vigilance**: Temptation highest in emotional sections (жить, умереть, etc.) - all marked ❌
3. **Narrator's Voice**: Consistent throughout - sympathetic witness, not judge
4. **Dagger vs. Poison**: Provided both options with guidance (dagger recommended but composer's choice)
5. **Catharsis Through Completion**: Chapter ending provides closure while preparing for next chapter

### Challenges Addressed

1. **Suicide Portrayal**: Sympathetic without glorifying, understands without celebrating, respects character autonomy
2. **Graphic Content Balance**: Real enough to be true, not gratuitous to be sensational
3. **Tone Consistency**: Elegiac throughout 15 stanzas without becoming monotonous
4. **Philosophical Without Preachy**: Narrator reflects on fate/choice without heavy-handed moralizing
5. **Transition to Chapter 8**: Ends Chapter 7 while preparing for discovery/aftermath
