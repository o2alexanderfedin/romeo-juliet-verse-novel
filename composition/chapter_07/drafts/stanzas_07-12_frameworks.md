# Chapter 7, Stanzas 7-12: False News and Roman's Despair

## Overview

**Narrative Arc**: Roman receives false news of Yulia's death and descends into total despair
**Romeo & Juliet Parallel**: Act V Scene 1 - "Her body sleeps in Capels' monument" / "I defy you, stars!"
**Critical Plot Point**: PEAK DRAMATIC IRONY - Reader knows Yulia alive, Roman believes she's dead
**Emotional Journey**: Hope → Shock → Disbelief → Crushing despair → Calm resolve to die

**Pacing**: FAST and intense (news arrives suddenly), then SLOWS as despair sets in, ends with eerie calm

---

## Stanza 7: The Servant Arrives with False News

**Detail Level**: DETAILED (280-350+ stress-marked words, 150-200+ rhyme pairs)

### KEY MOMENT
Roman's faithful servant arrives from Moscow, travel-worn and grieving. He brings the news that will destroy Roman: "Yulia Kapuletti is dead." The servant witnessed the funeral, saw her entombed in the family vault. He doesn't know about Lavrentiy's plan - to him, Yulia's death is real. Roman's world begins to shatter.

### Structure Guidance (aBaBccDDeFFeGG)

**Lines 1-4 (aBaB - alternating feminine/masculine rhymes)**
- L1 (a-fem): Servant arrives, exhausted from hard journey
- L2 (B-masc): Roman sees him, leaps up - "News from Moscow! Speak!"
- L3 (a-fem): Servant's face shows grief, hesitates to speak
- L4 (B-masc): Roman demands: "Tell me! Is Yulia well? The plan succeeded?"

**Lines 5-8 (ccDD - coupled feminine, coupled masculine)**
- L5-6 (cc-fem): Servant struggles to form words, tears in eyes
- L7-8 (DD-masc): Finally speaks: "My lord... she is dead."

**Lines 9-12 (eFFe - alternating, with coupled masculine center)**
- L9 (e-fem): The words hang in air, impossible, cannot be true
- L10-11 (FF-masc): Servant continues: funeral held, vault sealed, Moscow mourns
- L12 (e-fem): Roman stands frozen, cannot process, world tilting

**Lines 13-14 (GG - heroic couplet, masculine)**
- Summary: The news that destroys everything / Roman's shock, or servant's grief
- Dramatic irony note: Reader knows the truth, watches helpless
