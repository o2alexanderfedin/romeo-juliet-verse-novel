# Chapter 7, Stanzas 31-37: Yulia's Decision to Die

## Overview

**Narrative Arc**: Yulia alone with Roman's dead body, anguish and grief, considers living, cannot bear it, decides to die with him
**Romeo & Juliet Parallel**: Act V Scene 3 - Juliet finds Romeo dead, brief consideration, decides to die
**Atmospheric**: Dark vault, cold marble, silence, two figures (one dead, one living... for now)
**Pacing**: Slow, introspective, building to decision

**Distribution**: 4 detailed, 3 outlined (57% detailed - appropriate for emotional resolution)

## Stanza 31: Alone With Roman's Body - Initial Anguish

**Detail Level**: DETAILED (300-350+ stress-marked words, 175-200+ rhyme pairs)

### KEY MOMENT

Roman is dead. Yulia holds his body, cannot accept reality. Shakes him, calls his name, begs him to wake. "This can't be real. This is nightmare. Wake up. PLEASE wake up." Screams turn to sobs. He's cold. Stiffening. The poison has done its work. She holds him against her. Her Roman. Dead. Because he thought she was dead. Because the message never reached him. Because she took the sleeping potion. Her choice = his death. This is unbearable.

### Structure Guidance (aBaBccDDeFFeGG)

**Lines 1-4 (aBaB - alternating feminine/masculine rhymes)**
- L1 (a-fem): Holds his body, won't let go, cannot accept
- L2 (B-masc): Calls his name desperately, no answer comes
- L3 (a-fem): Shakes him, begs him wake, "This is nightmare"
- L4 (B-masc): But cold truth: he's dead, won't wake again

**Lines 5-8 (ccDD - coupled feminine, coupled masculine)**
- L5-6 (cc-fem): Screams turn to sobs, holds him tighter, her Roman gone
- L7-8 (DD-masc): **L8 CRITICAL**: Poison's work complete, he's cold and still

**Lines 9-12 (eFFeGG - fem, masc masc, fem, masc masc)**
- L9 (e-fem): She understands: her choice led to this
- L10-11 (FF-masc): Took potion, message failed, he thought her dead
- L12 (e-fem): His love drove him to death, she caused this
- L13-14 (GG-masc): Unbearable knowledge, unbearable grief, alone with corpse

**Act V Scene 3**: Juliet wakes, finds Romeo dead, initial reaction:
- "What's here? A cup, closed in my true love's hand? Poison, I see, hath been his timeless end."

**Chapter 7 St.31-37**: Extended version, explores decision process more deeply:
- St.31: Initial anguish (not in Shakespeare - Juliet wakes and immediately sees situation clearly)
- More psychological realism (Russian novel tradition)
- Yulia's process: denial → recognition → guilt → decision

## Stanza 32: Continuing Anguish - Won't Leave Him

**Detail Level**: OUTLINED (65-80+ stress-marked words, 70-100+ rhyme pairs)

### KEY MOMENT

Continues from St.31. Yulia will not leave Roman's body. Hours? Minutes? Time meaningless. She holds him, speaks to him (though he cannot hear), tells him everything: how she woke, how she thought plan worked, how she saw poison vial and understood. "I caused this. My choice killed you.
