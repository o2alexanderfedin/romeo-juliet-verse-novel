**Detail Level**: OUTLINED (65-80+ stress-marked words, 70-100+ rhyme pairs)

## Stanza 18: Arrives at Moscow, Approaches Kapuletti Estate

**Detail Level**: DETAILED (280-350+ words, 150-200+ pairs)

### KEY MOMENT
Arrives in Moscow (dawn breaking? or still night?). Knows the way to Kapuletti estate. Family vault is on the grounds or nearby (Russian noble vaults often on estate). Approaches. Guards? None needed - who breaks into vaults? The place is silent, mourning. Recent funeral (yesterday? day before?). He dismounts. Approaches vault location. His heart pounds. Soon: her tomb. Soon: his death.

**Vocabulary Categories** (12-14 categories, 280-350+ words):
- Moscow arrival, Kapuletti estate, family grounds
- Vault location, approaching, dismounting
- Silence, mourning, recent funeral
- His emotions (heart pounds, final moments approaching)
- Dawn/night, landscape, architecture
- Determination, fear, love, death ahead

## Stanza 19: Finds the Vault, Approaches

**Detail Level**: DETAILED (280-350+ words, 150-200+ pairs)

### KEY MOMENT
The vault. Stone structure, partially underground, marble entrance, family crest perhaps. This is where she lies. The door. How does he enter? Is it locked? Sealed? Will he break in (R&J uses tools)? Or find it accessible (recently used for funeral, not yet properly sealed)? He approaches. Hand on door. This is it. Beyond this: her body. And his death.

**Vocabulary**: Vault description (склеп, мрамор, камень, вход), door/entrance (дверь, вход, печать), breaking in or entering, his state (трепет, решимость), Russian vault architecture

**Romeo & Juliet Parallel**: "Thou detestable maw, thou womb of death, Gorged with the dearest morsel of the earth, Thus I enforce thy rotten jaws to open" (breaks in with tools)

## Stanza 20: Enters the Vault - Cold, Dark, Marble

**Detail Level**: DETAILED (280-350+ words, 150-200+ pairs)

### KEY MOMENT
Enters vault. Cold hits him. Darkness profound. He has candle or finds light somehow. The vault interior: stone, marble, generations of Kapulettis. Coffins, tombs, niches. Death's domain. Silence absolute. Cold, musty smell. He descends (if vault has steps). Where is she? Which tomb? He searches. There - fresh burial. New coffin. Her name? Or just knows: the newest one. Approaches.

**Vocabulary Categories** (14-16 categories, 280-350+ words):
- Entering, threshold crossed
- Cold (холод, стужа, ледяной)
- Darkness (тьма, мрак, темнота)
- Marble/stone (мрамор, камень)
- Vault interior (своды, стены, ниши)
- Coffins/tombs (гробы, гробницы)
- Generations of dead (поколения, предки)
- Silence (тишина, безмолвие)
- Smell (запах, тлен, сырость)
- Steps descending (лестница, спуск)
- Candle/light (свеча, свет, пламя)
- Searching (ищет, находит)
- Fresh burial (новая, недавняя)
- Her tomb (её гроб, она здесь)
- His emotions (страх, решимость, любовь)
- Death atmosphere (смерть, царство мёртвых)

**Pushkin-style atmospheric description**: Make vault vivid but not gothic horror. Cold beauty of marble, solemn silence, death's peace. Not terrifying, but final.

## Stanza 21: Tomb Imagery Digression (OPTIONAL)

**Detail Level**: DETAILED (280-350+ words, 150-200+ pairs)

### KEY MOMENT
Narrator digresses (Pushkin-style) on Russian family vaults, death, marble chambers. The nobility's final home. Generations united in death as in life. The cold eternal sleep. Marriage of marble and mortality. This could be philosophical (death as final democracy - all equal in tomb), or cultural (Russian Orthodox views on death), or simply atmospheric (cold beauty of death's architecture). Sets mood before monologue scene.

**Vocabulary**: Tomb philosophy, Russian vaults, marble permanence, death's kingdom, nobility and death, cold eternity, Orthodox themes (optional), narrator's reflection

**Alternative**: Skip digression, merge into St.20, proceed directly to St.22 (sees Yulia)

## Technical Notes for Composer

**Vault Atmosphere**:
- Cold but beautiful (marble, architecture)
- Solemn, not horrifying
- Death's peace, not terror
- Russian cultural context (Orthodox attitudes, noble family vaults)

**Russian Vault Details**:
- Often on estate grounds or nearby
- Partially underground, stone/marble construction
- Multiple generations, family crests
- Solemn, permanent, cold

**Roman's State**:
- Determined, focused
- Not afraid of death (seeking it)
- Single-minded purpose
- Emotional but controlled (until he sees her)

**Pacing Guidance**:
- St.16-17: FAST (journey outlined, gets to Moscow quickly)
- St.18-19: MEDIUM (arrival, approach detailed but moving)
- St.20-21: SLOW (vault interior, atmosphere, digression optional)
- Then St.22: Sees her (time stops)

**Digression Option (St.21)**:
- CAN be tomb imagery/philosophy (Pushkin style)
- OR can be merged into St.20, proceed directly to St.22
- Composer's choice based on pacing needs
- If used: keep philosophical not macabre, enhance mood
