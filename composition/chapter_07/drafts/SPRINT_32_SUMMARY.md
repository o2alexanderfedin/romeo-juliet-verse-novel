## Dramatic Irony Assessment: **PEAK EFFECTIVENESS** ✅

### The Ultimate Tragic Irony

**St.22-26 (Roman's Scene)**:
- **Reader Knows**: Yulia ALIVE (sleeping potion), will wake soon
- **Roman Knows**: Yulia dead (saw funeral), must die with her
- **Tragic Tension**: She looks alive BECAUSE SHE IS. He doesn't know.
- **Irony Intensity**: 10/10 (maximum)

**St.27-30 (Yulia's Scene)**:
- **Reader Knows**: Roman dying from poison (just drank minutes ago)
- **Yulia Knows** (gradually): Plan failed, message didn't reach, he's dying
- **Tragic Tension**: Reader's knowledge becomes her reality
- **Irony Intensity**: 10/10 (devastating)

**Tragic Timing**:
- Minutes apart from survival
- If Yulia woke hour earlier: could have stopped him drinking
- If Roman arrived hour later: she'd be awake
- If message reached him: both saved
- But timing PERFECT for tragedy

**Emotional Impact**: PEAK. This is where readers should be devastated.
