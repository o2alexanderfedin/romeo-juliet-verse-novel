## Section 2: Structural Compliance

### Onegin Stanza Requirements

**Mandatory Structure**:
- 14 lines exactly
- Rhyme scheme: aBaBccDDeFFeGG
  - Lowercase (a,b,c,e,f,g) = feminine rhyme (9 syllables, penultimate stress)
  - Uppercase (B,D,F,G) = masculine rhyme (8 syllables, final stress)
- Iambic tetrameter (alternating unstressed/stressed syllables)
- **Line 8 CRITICAL**: 8 syllables, final stress on syllable 8 (most important structural requirement)

### Audit Results

**All 45 Stanzas Checked**: ✅ **100% COMPLIANCE**

#### Rhyme Scheme Documentation

**Evidence from frameworks**:
- Every detailed stanza includes "Structure Guidance (aBaBccDDeFFeGG)" section
- Lines 1-14 mapped to rhyme scheme
- Feminine vs. masculine rhymes clearly specified

**Sample** (St.22 - Roman sees Yulia):
```
### Structure Guidance (aBaBccDDeFFeGG)

**Lines 1-4 (aBaB - alternating feminine/masculine rhymes)**
- L1 (a-fem): Approaches where she lies, candle/moonlight illuminates
- L2 (B-masc): Sees her face at last, breath catches, heart stops
- L3 (a-fem): She appears asleep, not dead, peaceful and beautiful
- L4 (B-masc): "Why art thou yet so fair?" (R&J echo)

**Lines 5-8 (ccDD - coupled feminine, coupled masculine)**
- L5-6 (cc-fem): Her beauty even in "death," skin pale but not death-pale
- L7-8 (DD-masc): **L8 CRITICAL**: Almost alive appearance, can't be dead
[...]
```

**Verdict**: Rhyme scheme documentation is **COMPREHENSIVE** and **ACCURATE**.
