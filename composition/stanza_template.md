## Meter Verification (Iambic Tetrameter)

```
Line 1:  da-DUM da-DUM da-DUM da-DUM (da)    [8-9 syllables]
Line 2:  da-DUM da-DUM da-DUM da-DUM          [8 syllables]
Line 3:  da-DUM da-DUM da-DUM da-DUM (da)    [8-9 syllables]
Line 4:  da-DUM da-DUM da-DUM da-DUM          [8 syllables]
Line 5:  da-DUM da-DUM da-DUM da-DUM (da)    [8-9 syllables]
Line 6:  da-DUM da-DUM da-DUM da-DUM (da)    [8-9 syllables]
Line 7:  da-DUM da-DUM da-DUM da-DUM          [8 syllables]
Line 8:  da-DUM da-DUM da-DUM da-DUM ⚠️      [8 syllables, stress on 8]
Line 9:  da-DUM da-DUM da-DUM da-DUM (da)    [8-9 syllables]
Line 10: da-DUM da-DUM da-DUM da-DUM          [8 syllables]
Line 11: da-DUM da-DUM da-DUM da-DUM          [8 syllables]
Line 12: da-DUM da-DUM da-DUM da-DUM (da)    [8-9 syllables]
Line 13: da-DUM da-DUM da-DUM da-DUM          [8 syllables]
Line 14: da-DUM da-DUM da-DUM da-DUM          [8 syllables]
```

**Rule**: Stress on even syllables (2, 4, 6, 8). Never on odd syllables (1, 3, 5, 7).
**Exception**: Line 8 final stress is MANDATORY on syllable 8.

## Quality Checklist

### Technical Correctness
- [ ] 14 lines exactly
- [ ] Rhyme scheme correct: aBaBccDDeFFeGG
- [ ] All feminine rhymes have 9 syllables
- [ ] All masculine rhymes have 8 syllables
- [ ] Line 8 has stress on syllable 8 (MANDATORY)
- [ ] Iambic tetrameter maintained throughout
- [ ] Zero forbidden rhymes (no verb rhymes, no grammatical rhymes)
- [ ] All stress patterns verified (not guessed!)

### Quality Standards
- [ ] Natural-sounding Russian (not forced for rhyme/meter)
- [ ] 70%+ rich or exact rhymes (not approximate)
- [ ] Voice consistent (narrator or character)
- [ ] Content serves narrative/digressive purpose
- [ ] Connects to adjacent stanzas
- [ ] Imagery vivid and concrete
- [ ] Precision and brevity (Pushkin's principle)

### Voice Consistency
- [ ] Narrator voice appropriate to chapter
- [ ] Character voice (if dialogue) distinct and consistent
- [ ] Vocabulary appropriate to speaker
- [ ] Syntax matches character personality
