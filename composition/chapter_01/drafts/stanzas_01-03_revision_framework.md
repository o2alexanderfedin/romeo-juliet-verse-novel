## Lessons Learned from This Revision

### Critical Lessons:

1. **Verb Rhymes are Everywhere**
   - Easy to slip into verb endings
   - Must consciously choose nouns/adjectives/adverbs
   - Always check: "Is this word a verb form?"

2. **Restructuring is Often Necessary**
   - Can't always just replace line-end word
   - Sometimes must rebuild entire line or couplet
   - Content can usually be expressed multiple ways

3. **Rich Rhymes are Achievable**
   - With planning, can find consonant-matching rhymes
   - Use Rifmovnik.ru filters aggressively
   - Worth the extra search time

4. **Weak Rhymes Undermine Quality**
   - Even without forbidden rhymes, weak rhymes harm poem
   - Always strive for rich > exact > approximate
   - Never settle for "close enough"

5. **Revision Takes Time**
   - First draft almost never perfect
   - Multiple iterations normal
   - Quality emerges through revision

### Process Lessons:

1. **Systematic Approach Works**
   - Check each rhyme pair individually
   - Verify against forbidden list
   - Document all changes

2. **Pre-Plan Rhyme Schemes**
   - Choose line-end words FIRST
   - Verify they're not forbidden
   - Then build lines around them

3. **Use Verification Checklists**
   - Don't rely on memory
   - Check every technical requirement
   - Document as you go
