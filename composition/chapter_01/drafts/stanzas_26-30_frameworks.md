| **Irony Level** | Gentle (30%) | Heightened (50%) |

### Voice Guidance Notes

**Narrator Distance Techniques**:
1. Use formal titles: Григóрий Капулéтов (full name), not diminutives
2. Ironic descriptors: "почтéнный" (respectable - with irony), "извéстный" (well-known - neutral)
3. Cooler vocabulary: "семья́" (family - formal) vs "семéйство" (family - warmer, not used here)
4. More social positioning: "извéстен в Москвé" (known in Moscow - external view)
5. Less internal access: Describe exterior, not inner warmth

**BUT Protective of Yulia**:
- When introducing Yulia: Tone softens slightly (30% → 40% sympathy)
- Use gentler words: "невиннóсть" (innocence), "красотá" (beauty)
- Foreshadow her victimhood subtly

**Contrast with Montyagin Introduction (Stanzas 16-20)**:
- Roman: "милый юноша" (dear young man) → warm
- Grigory: "Григóрий Капулéтов" (full formal name) → cold
- Merkury: "Меркýрий, друг Ромáна" (friend) → intimate
- Maria: "женá егó, покóрная" (his wife, submissive) → distant observation
