- Neither aware of other
- Sensory differentiation (different details for each location)
- Temporal markers (evening falling, candles lit)
- Creates sense of fate's design (threads converging)

### 6. Atmospheric Immersion (Stanza 47)

- Approaching fate (mansion lights growing brighter)
- Momentum and inevitability

### 7. Cliffhanger Technique (Stanza 48)
- Threshold/liminal moment (crossing boundary)
- Build anticipation: music, lights, sounds from inside
- Final action: entering ballroom (but not yet inside)
- Narrator's direct address: "And here, dear reader..."
