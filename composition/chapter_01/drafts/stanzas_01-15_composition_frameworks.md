## CRITICAL REMINDERS BEFORE COMPOSING

### Voice Requirements (Chapter 1):
- **Narrator Tone Mix**: 60% Ironic, 30% Sympathetic, 10% Philosophical
- **Mode**: Detached, scene-setting, establishing relationships
- **Reader Address**: Frequent (Мой друг, Читатель мой, Позвольте мне...)
- **Age**: Mid-40s to early 50s, male, retrospective, knows ending

### Technical Requirements (ALL 15 stanzas):
- ✅ Onegin stanza: aBaBccDDeFFeGG rhyme scheme
- ✅ 14 lines exactly per stanza
- ❌ ZERO forbidden rhymes (no verb infinitives, conjugations, past tense)
- ✅ Iambic tetrameter: stress on even syllables (2,4,6,8)
- ✅ Line 8: MANDATORY stress on syllable 8 (inviolable rule)
- ✅ 70%+ rich or exact rhymes (target: 80%+)
- ✅ NEVER guess Russian stress - always verify!

### Quality Gates (before moving to next stanza):
- ✅ Perfect technical form
- ✅ Voice consistent with narrator profile
- ✅ Sounds natural when read aloud (3+ times)
- ✅ Zero forbidden rhymes (double-checked)
