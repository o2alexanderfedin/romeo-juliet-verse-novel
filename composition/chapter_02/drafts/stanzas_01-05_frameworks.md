## Stanza 1: Roman's Morning Melancholy

### Function & Requirements

**Purpose**: Open Chapter 2 with Roman waking, still wrapped in melancholic mood
**Type**: Narrator commentary (ironic but sympathetic)
**Tone**: Gently teasing with sympathy (60% Teasing, 30% Sympathetic, 10% Atmospheric)

**Content Requirements**:
- Roman waking (or has not slept)
- Morning Moscow (dawn or early light)
- Still thinking of unrequited love (Rosaline equivalent)
- Narrator's gentle mockery of fashionable melancholy
- But also real sympathy for youth's passion

**Key Images**:
- Morning light (cold, winter dawn)
- Roman's sleepless or troubled waking
- Solitude and lovesickness

### Pre-Verified Rhyme Scheme (aBaBccDDeFFeGG)

#### Lines 1-4 (aBaB - alternating feminine/masculine)

**Line 1 (a - feminine): ...любвú** (lyub-VÍ - of love, genitive)
Options for Line 3:
- судьбú (sud'-BÍ) - of fate [noun, genitive] ✅ Thematic
- мольбú (mal'-BÍ) - of plea [noun, genitive] ✅
- борьбú (bar'-BÍ) - of struggle [noun, genitive] ✅ Rich rhyme
- **RECOMMENDED**: судьбú (fate - echoes Chapter 1, thematic)

**Line 2 (B - masculine): ...Ромáн** (ra-MÁN - Roman, name)
Options for Line 4:
- обмáн (ab-MÁN) - deception [noun] ✅ Rich rhyme, thematic (self-deception)
- турмáн (tur-MÁN) - fog/mist [archaic] ✅ Atmospheric
- капитáн (ka-pi-TÁN) - captain ✅ But less thematic
- **RECOMMENDED**: обмáн (deception - fashionable melancholy vs real feeling)

**ALTERNATIVE Rhyme Pair 1**:
- Line 1: ...зарú (za-RÍ - of dawn, genitive) [feminine]

**ALTERNATIVE Rhyme Pair 2**:

- Line 4: ...метéль (me-TYÉL') - blizzard [feminine] ✅ But gender mismatch risk

#### Lines 5-8 (ccDD - coupled feminine then masculine)

**Lines 5-6 (cc - feminine couplet): ...душá** (du-SHÁ - soul)

- дышá (dy-SHÁ) - breathing [gerund] ⚠️ Verb form - avoid
- греха́ (gre-KHÁ) - of sin [genitive] ✅ But thematic mismatch

**Lines 7-8 (DD - masculine couplet): ...мечтá** (mech-TÁ - dream/reverie)
⚠️ **CRITICAL**: Line 8 MUST have stress on syllable 8 (mandatory!)

- красотá (kra-sa-TÁ) - beauty ✅ EXCELLENT (dreaming of beauty)
- простотá (pras-ta-TÁ) - simplicity ✅
- пустотá (pus-ta-TÁ) - emptiness ✅ (emptiness of longing)
- **RECOMMENDED**: красотá (beauty - object of melancholy dreams)

#### Lines 9-12 (eFfe - alternating feminine/masculine)

**Lines 9, 12 (e - feminine): ...утрó** (ut-RÓ - morning)
Options:
- окнó (ak-NÓ) - window ✅ (looking out window)
- давнó (dav-NÓ) - long ago ✅
- однó (ad-NÓ) - one/alone ✅ (solitude)
- **RECOMMENDED**: окнó (window - visual image, looking out)

**Lines 10-11 (F - masculine): ...сон** (SON - dream/sleep)
Options:
- закóн (za-KÓN) - law ✅
- склóн (SKLON) - slope/hillside ✅
- стон (STON) - moan/groan ✅ Lovesick imagery
- **RECOMMENDED**: стон (groan - comic exaggeration of lovesickness)

#### Lines 13-14 (GG - masculine couplet)

- власть (VLAST') - power ✅ (passion's power)

**ALTERNATIVE final couplet**:

## Stanza 3: Narrator's Digression on Young Love

### Function & Requirements

**Purpose**: Narrator's philosophical/personal digression on fashionable melancholy
**Type**: Narrator commentary (digressive/personal)
**Tone**: Ironic but affectionate (70% Ironic, 20% Affectionate, 10% Philosophical)

**Content Requirements**:
- Narrator steps back from narrative
- Comments on youth's love of being in love
- Petrarchan/Byronic posing vs. genuine feeling
- Confesses own youthful "Rosalines"
- Defends romantic excess while mocking it
- Establishes narrator-reader intimacy

**Key Images**:

5. **Check arc**: Does stanza sequence flow naturally (solitary → social)?
6. **Polish**: Word choice, precision, brevity

8. **Finalize**: Only when all checklist items passed

### For Quality Control

- **Minimum standard**: All technical requirements met
- **Target standard**: Natural, effortless-sounding despite complexity
- **Pushkin standard**: Every word perfect, no alternatives better
- **Character standard**: Roman sympathetic despite theatrical passion
- **Tonal standard**: Gentle mockery balanced with genuine sympathy

### Common Issues to Watch

**1. Over-Mocking Roman**:
- Issue: Making Roman seem foolish rather than young
- Solution: Ensure narrator's sympathy evident, confessional tone ("I too...")

**2. Verb Rhymes**:
- Issue: Past tense verbs (бродúл, взглянýл, etc.) tempting but forbidden
- Solution: Restructure to bring nouns/adjectives to rhyme position

**3. Losing Voice Consistency**:
- Issue: Narrator voice shifts too much between stanzas
- Solution: Re-read all 5 stanzas together, ensure same persona

**4. Missing Transitions**:
- Issue: Stanzas feel disconnected
- Solution: Ensure thematic/vocabulary bridges (repeated words, thematic echoes)

**5. Digression Feeling Intrusive**:
- Issue: Stanza 3 digression interrupts flow
- Solution: Ensure organic transition in/out, make digression illuminate Roman's state
