## Technical Requirements (All Stanzas)

### Onegin Stanza Form
- **14 lines** per stanza (inviolable)
- **Rhyme scheme**: aBaBccDDeFFeGG
  - Lowercase (a,b,c,e,f) = **feminine** rhyme (9 syllables, stress on penultimate)
  - Uppercase (B,D,F,G) = **masculine** rhyme (8 syllables, stress on final)
- **Meter**: Iambic tetrameter throughout
- **Line 8 Mandatory**: Final stress on syllable 8 (never violate)

### Forbidden Rhymes (Critical - Zero Tolerance)
❌ **NEVER use**:
- Verb infinitives (-ть, -ти): любить/говорить
- Verb conjugations (-ет, -ит, -ает): говорит/звонит
- Verb past tense (-л, -ла, -ло, -ли): сказал/узнал
- Excessive grammatical endings
- Trite pairs: любовь/кровь, розы/морозы

### Rhyme Quality Target
- **Rich rhymes**: 40-50% overall (consonants before stressed vowel match)
- **Stanza 49 special**: 60-70% rich rhymes (elevated quality for critical moment)
- **Exact rhymes**: Standard (match from stressed vowel onward)
- **Approximate rhymes**: Minimize

### Character Voice Evolution

**Roman** (Critical Transformation):
- **Stanzas 46-48** (Before): LOW intensity, elevated but melancholic, resigned
  - Vocabulary: тоска́ (melancholy), тень (shadow), холо́д (cold)
  - Syntax: Long phrases, pauses, reluctance
  - Imagery: Darkness, distance, shadows

- **Stanza 49** (During): MID-STANZA SHIFT - melancholic → AWESTRUCK
  - Lines 1-8: Still traces of melancholy
  - Lines 9-14: Complete transformation, overwhelmed, transfixed

- **Stanza 50** (After): HIGH intensity, poetic, passionate, awakened
  - Vocabulary: красота́ (beauty), свет (light), жи́знь (life)
  - Syntax: Breathless, exclamations, wonder
  - Imagery: Light, brilliance, life, movement

**Narrator** (Tonal Modulation):
- **Stanzas 46-48**: Atmospheric, descriptive, anticipatory (setting scene)
- **Stanza 49**: Present-moment intensity, visual poetry, caught in the moment
- **Stanza 50**: Dramatic, foreshadowing, ominous undertones (dramatic irony returns)

## STANZA 46: Arrival at the Mansion

### Narrative Function
Roman, Merkury, and Benedikt arrive at the Capulet mansion exterior. Grandeur, lights, music spilling out into winter night. Final approach, crossing threshold from street to entrance. Anticipation building.

### Content Outline (14 lines)

**Lines 1-4 (Quatrain 1, aBaB)**: Mansion exterior revealed
- Line 1 (a, fem): Mansion stands before them, imposing and grand
- Line 2 (B, masc): Every window ablaze with golden light
- Line 3 (a, fem): Music flows out into winter night
- Line 4 (B, masc): Carriages arrive, guests in brilliant array

**Lines 5-8 (Quatrain 2, ccDD)**: Approaching entrance
- Line 5 (c, fem): Three friends pause moment at gate
- Line 6 (c, fem): Merkury comments, eager to enter
- Line 7 (D, masc): They join the stream of guests
- Line 8 (D, masc): **MANDATORY STRESS SYLLABLE 8** - And step into the warmth and light

**Lines 9-14 (Sestet, eFFeGG)**: Entry vestibule
- Line 9 (e, fem): Servants take cloaks, masks adjusted
- Line 10 (F, masc): Warm air, music louder now
- Line 11 (F, masc): Laughter and voices from ballroom ahead
- Line 12 (e, fem): Roman's heart beats faster (involuntary)
- Line 13 (G, masc): Benedikt asks if he's ready
- Line 14 (G, masc): They move toward the music's call

### Atmospheric Details
- **Setting**: Mansion exterior → entrance → vestibule (transition spaces)
- **Sensory**: Blaze of light from windows, music flowing out, warmth vs. cold
- **Mood**: Anticipatory, excitement building, tension
- **Temperature**: Cold exterior → warm interior (contrast heightened)

### Character Dynamics
- **Merkury**: Peak excitement, ready to enter
- **Roman**: Involuntary physical reactions (heart racing), still resigned
