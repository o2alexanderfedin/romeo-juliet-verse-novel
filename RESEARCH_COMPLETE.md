## Critical Rules Documented

### Russian Poetry Composition

1. **NEVER guess Russian stress** - always verify (unpredictable)
2. **NO verb rhymes** - infinitives and conjugations forbidden
3. **NO grammatical rhymes** - too easy, considered banal
4. **Final stress MANDATORY** - syllable 8 in iambic tetrameter
5. **Odd positions unstressed** - positions 1, 3, 5, 7 must be unstressed
6. **Prefer rich rhymes** - consonants before stress should match
7. **Mix parts of speech** - avoid rhyming same grammatical forms
8. **Read aloud** - best test of natural sound within meter

### Quality Standards

1. **Precision and brevity** - Pushkin's paramount principle
2. **Every word earns its place** - no filler
3. **Show through sensory detail** - not abstract telling
4. **Natural within strict form** - achieved through revision
5. **Voice consistency** - narrator and characters
6. **Cultural appropriateness** - verify all transpositions
