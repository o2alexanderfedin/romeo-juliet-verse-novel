### 3.1 Composition vs. Framework Development

**What We've Done (38 Sprints)**:

- **Framework**: "Here's what to say, here are word options, here's the rhyme scheme"
- **Composition**: "Друзья́, в Москве́ произошла́ драма... судьба́..." (actual verses)

### 3.2 Composition Approaches (Three Options)

#### Option A: Linear Composition (Chapter by Chapter)

**Method**: Compose Chapter 1 (St.1-55) → Chapter 2 (St.1-50) → ... → Chapter 8 (St.1-40)

**Advantages**:
- Maintains narrative flow and continuity naturally
- Voice evolution happens organically
- Reader experience matches composition experience
- Easier to maintain dramatic tension buildup

**Disadvantages**:
- Must complete early chapters before seeing how ending works
- Risk of needing to revise earlier stanzas based on later discoveries
- Momentum might flag in middle chapters

**Best For**: Maintaining narrative coherence and natural voice evolution

**Timeline**: 8-12 weeks (55 stanzas Ch1 → 50 stanzas Ch2 → ... → 40 stanzas Ch8)

#### Option B: Peak-First Composition (Critical Scenes First)

**Method**: Compose 10 peak moments first → Fill in connective tissue

**Peak Moments** (30-40 stanzas):
1. Opening stanza (Ch 1, St.1) - Sets tone for entire novel
2. First sight (Ch 2, St.49-50) - Inciting incident
3. First meeting (Ch 3, St.31-37) - Love begins
4. Balcony scene (Ch 4, St.1-10) - Most famous scene
5. Merkury's death (Ch 5, St.25-30) - Shocking turn
6. Roman kills Tikhon (Ch 5, St.36-38) - Tragic mistake
7. Lark & nightingale (Ch 5, St.54) - Heartrending parting
8. Potion scene (Ch 6, St.46-48) - Yulia's courage
9. Both deaths (Ch 7, St.22-42) - Climax (21 stanzas)
10. Novel ending (Ch 8, St.37-40) - Final impression

**Advantages**:
- Ensures peaks are PERFECT (they carry the novel)
- Builds confidence (hardest parts done first)
- Connective stanzas easier after peaks are set
- Can test if peaks work before investing in full novel

**Disadvantages**:
- Risk of inconsistent voice across scattered composition
- May need heavy revision to connect peak moments smoothly
- Harder to maintain narrator's evolving tone

**Best For**: Ensuring emotional high points are unforgettable

**Timeline**: 3-4 weeks for peaks → 6-8 weeks for connective tissue = 9-12 weeks total

#### Option C: Hybrid Approach (Recommended)

**Method**: Compose in chapter blocks, but polish peaks within each chapter before moving on

**Process**:
1. **Chapter 1**: Compose all 55 stanzas linearly, extra polish on St.1 (opening)
2. **Chapter 2**: Compose St.1-48, then perfect St.49-50 (first sight), then St.51-52
3. **Chapter 3**: Compose with attention to St.31-37 (first meeting) quality
4. **Chapter 4**: Balcony scene (St.1-10) gets EXTRA time, then rest of chapter
5. **Chapter 5**: Merkury death (St.25-30), Tikhon death (St.36-38), parting (St.54) all perfected
6. **Chapter 6**: Potion scene (St.46-48) perfected within chapter flow
7. **Chapter 7**: Both deaths (St.22-42) get MAXIMUM attention - may take 50% of chapter time
8. **Chapter 8**: Ending (St.37-40) perfected, then rest polished

**Advantages**:
- ✅ Maintains narrative flow (like Option A)
- ✅ Ensures peaks are perfect (like Option B)
- ✅ Balanced approach - neither extreme
- ✅ Natural voice evolution preserved
- ✅ Peak moments get deserved attention

**Disadvantages**:
- Requires discipline to spend extra time on peaks (resist urge to rush)

**Best For**: Balanced composition maintaining both coherence and peak quality

**Timeline**: 8-12 weeks (2-3 hours avg per stanza, more for peaks)

**RECOMMENDATION**: Use Option C (Hybrid Approach)

### 3.3 Composition Workflow (Per Stanza)

#### Step 1: Framework Review (10-15 minutes)

**Read**:
- Content requirements for this stanza
- Previous stanza (continuity)
- Next stanza (foreshadowing)
- Voice guidance (narrator tone, character voice if dialogue)

**Verify**:
- Rhyme scheme (aBaBccDDeFFeGG)
- Rhyme word options (3-4 per position)
- Vocabulary bank available
- Special requirements (Line 8 mandatory stress, dialogue, etc.)

#### Step 2: First Draft (45-60 minutes)

**Process**:
1. Draft Lines 1-4 (aBaB - alternating quatrain)
   - Choose rhyme words from verified options
   - Ensure iambic tetrameter (stress on syllables 2,4,6,8)
   - Convey required content
   - Verify stress using dictionaries (NEVER guess!)

2. Draft Lines 5-8 (ccDD - couplets)
   - Line 8 CRITICAL: Must have stress on syllable 8 (mandatory)
   - Verify both couplets rhyme correctly
   - Continue narrative/voice

3. Draft Lines 9-12 (eFfe - alternating quatrain)
   - Return to alternating rhyme pattern
   - Build toward conclusion

4. Draft Lines 13-14 (GG - final couplet)
   - Concluding thought or transition to next stanza
   - Verify masculine rhyme (8 syllables, stress on 8th)

**Key Rules**:
- ❌ NEVER use verb infinitives as rhymes (-ть, -ти endings)
- ❌ NEVER use verb conjugations as easy rhymes
- ❌ NEVER use grammatical ending rhymes (too banal)
- ✅ Prefer rich rhymes (consonants before stressed vowel match)
- ✅ Mix parts of speech (noun with adjective, not two nouns)
- ✅ ALWAYS verify stress with dictionaries

#### Step 3: Technical Verification (15-20 minutes)

**Checklist**:
- [ ] Exactly 14 lines
- [ ] Rhyme scheme aBaBccDDeFFeGG correct
- [ ] All stresses verified with dictionaries
- [ ] Line 8 has mandatory stress on syllable 8
- [ ] Iambic tetrameter throughout (even syllables stressed)
- [ ] Zero forbidden rhymes (no verb infinitives, conjugations)
- [ ] All rhymes at least "exact" (preferably "rich")
- [ ] Natural word order (not forced by meter)

#### Step 4: Voice Verification (10-15 minutes)

**Checklist**:
- [ ] Narrator tone matches chapter requirements
  - Ch 1-2: 60% ironic, 30% philosophical, 10% sympathetic
  - Ch 3-4: 45% ironic, 35% sympathetic, 15% philosophical, 5% playful
  - Ch 5: 20% ironic, 50% sympathetic, 30% philosophical
  - Ch 6-7: 30% ironic, 45% sympathetic, 25% philosophical
  - Ch 8: 25% ironic, 30% sympathetic, 40% philosophical, 5% elegiac

- [ ] Character voice consistent (if dialogue)
  - Roman: Elevated, poetic, passionate
  - Yulia: Gentle, modest, philosophical
  - Merkury: Witty, mocking, loyal
  - Matryona: Pragmatic, warm (then conflicted)

- [ ] Reader address appropriate
  - Formal early (Ch 1-2)
  - Intimate mid-novel (Ch 3-4)
  - Shared grief (Ch 5-7)
  - Deep farewell (Ch 8)

#### Step 5: Read-Aloud Test (10-15 minutes)

**Process**:
1. Read entire stanza aloud in Russian
2. Listen for:
   - Natural flow (not forced)
   - Meter feels effortless (like Pushkin)
   - Rhymes not jarring or obvious
   - Meaning clear
   - Voice consistent

3. Mark any awkward phrases
4. Revise weak spots
5. Read aloud again until stanza sounds natural

#### Step 6: Content Verification (5-10 minutes)

**Checklist**:
- [ ] All required content included
- [ ] Continuity from previous stanza
- [ ] Setup for next stanza (if needed)
- [ ] Dramatic irony exploited (if applicable)
- [ ] Foreshadowing included (if required)
- [ ] Pacing appropriate (fast/moderate/slow)

#### Step 7: Revision (30-45 minutes)

**Focus Areas**:
1. **Weak rhymes**: Replace adequate rhymes with rich rhymes where possible
2. **Forced language**: Smooth any unnatural word order
3. **Meter polish**: Ensure stress pattern feels invisible
4. **Precision**: Every word earns its place (Pushkin's principle)
5. **Vocabulary elevation**: Replace common words with more expressive choices

**Revision Passes**:
- Pass 1: Technical perfection (meter, rhyme, stress)
- Pass 2: Voice consistency (narrator/character)
- Pass 3: Content completeness
- Pass 4: Natural sound (read aloud)
- Pass 5: Word-level polish (precision, elevation)

#### Step 8: Final Approval (5-10 minutes)

**Quality Gates**:
- ✅ Technical perfection (zero errors)
- ✅ Voice consistent
- ✅ Sounds natural when read aloud
- ✅ Content requirements met
- ✅ Publication-ready quality

**If ANY gate fails**: Return to revision

**If ALL gates pass**: Mark stanza COMPLETE, move to next

## Part 7: Risks & Mitigation

### 7.1 Composition Risks

#### Risk 1: Composition Slower Than Estimated

**Probability**: HIGH (creative work often takes longer)

**Impact**: Timeline extends from 5 months to 6-8 months

**Mitigation**:
- Build in 2-4 week buffer in timeline
- Track daily output (if consistently below target, adjust expectations)
- Don't sacrifice quality for speed
- Accept that peak moments SHOULD take longer

#### Risk 2: Voice Inconsistency Across Novel

**Probability**: MEDIUM (composing over 5 months risks drift)

**Impact**: Major revision needed for voice consistency

**Mitigation**:
- Review narrator voice guidelines at start of each chapter
- Do chapter-level QA (read full chapter, check tone distribution)
- Keep voice evolution chart visible
- Reread previous chapter before starting next

#### Risk 3: Technical Errors Slip Through

**Probability**: MEDIUM (400 stanzas, easy to miss errors)

**Impact**: Forbidden rhymes or meter errors in published novel

**Mitigation**:
- NEVER guess stress - always verify
- Use forbidden rhyme checklist for every stanza
- Random sample technical audits during composition (10% per chapter)
- Full technical audit before publication

#### Risk 4: Peak Moments Don't Land

**Probability**: LOW (frameworks are strong)

**Impact**: Novel feels flat, lacks emotional power

**Mitigation**:
- Spend extra time on 10 peak moments (don't rush)
- Test peak moments on others if possible
- Compare to Shakespeare originals (are we matching power?)
- Revise until YOU are moved by the moment

#### Risk 5: Creative Block or Burnout

**Probability**: MEDIUM (5 months is long)

**Impact**: Composition stalls, timeline extends

**Mitigation**:
- Build in rest days (1 day per week minimum)
- Allow non-linear composition (skip ahead if stuck)
- Return to research materials for inspiration
- Remember: frameworks remove most creative burden
- Celebrate milestones (each chapter completion)

### 7.2 Post-Composition Risks

#### Risk 6: Major Revision Needed After Read-Aloud

**Probability**: MEDIUM (some issues only appear in full context)

**Impact**: Additional 2-4 weeks of revision

**Mitigation**:
- Do chapter-level read-aloud tests (catch issues early)
- Don't wait until 400 stanzas to test flow
- Budget 1-2 weeks for final revision in timeline

#### Risk 7: External Feedback Requires Changes

**Probability**: LOW-MEDIUM (if external review happens)

**Impact**: Revision of specific stanzas or passages

**Mitigation**:
- Choose reviewers carefully (Russian speakers, poetry knowledge)
- Weight feedback appropriately (technical issues = must fix, taste = optional)
- Build in time for feedback integration
