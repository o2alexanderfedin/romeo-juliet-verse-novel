# Character Name Mapping: Romeo and Juliet → Russian Verse Novel

## Setting

**City:** Verona → **Казань (Kazan)**
- Historical significance: Ancient city with both Russian and Tatar heritage
- Perfect for representing feuding noble families
- Rich cultural crossroads
- Center of trade and power in 16th-17th century Russia

**Time Period:** Early 17th century (Time of Troubles era)
- Provides context for noble family rivalries
- Authority structure: Tsar's appointed Governor (Воевода)

## MAIN CHARACTERS

### Romeo → Роман Владимирович Монтягин
**Full Name:** Роман Владимирович Монтягин (Roman Vladimirovich Montyagin)

**Name Breakdown:**
- **Имя (First name):** Роман (Roman) - "Roman, novel, romance"
- **Отчество (Patronymic):** Владимирович (Vladimirovich) - "son of Vladimir" (meaning "ruler of the world")
- **Фамилия (Surname):** Монтягин (Montyagin) - Russified from Montague

**Forms of Address:**
- **Formal:** Роман Владимирович
- **Informal (friends):** Роман, Рома (Roma)
- **Diminutive (intimate):** Ромочка (Romochka), Ромка (Romka)
- **By family:** Сын (son), Рома

**Social Rank:** Boyar's son (сын боярский)

**Symbolism:** "Roman" connects to Romance, romantic ideals, and the Roman origins of the story. Vladimir (peaceful ruler) as father suggests noble lineage.

**Pronunciation:** roh-MAHN vlah-DEE-mee-roh-veech mohn-TYAH-geen

**Character Voice:** Passionate, poetic, educated in Western literature, uses Church Slavonic when being dramatic

---

### Juliet → Юлия Григорьевна Капулетова
**Full Name:** Юлия Григорьевна Капулетова (Yulia Grigoryevna Kapuletova)

**Name Breakdown:**
- **Имя:** Юлия (Yulia) - "youthful, downy"
- **Отчество:** Григорьевна (Grigoryevna) - "daughter of Grigory" (meaning "watchful")
- **Фамилия:** Капулетова (Kapuletova) - Russified feminine form of Capulet

**Forms of Address:**
- **Formal:** Юлия Григорьевна
- **Informal:** Юлия, Юля (Yulya)
- **Diminutive (intimate):** Юленька (Yulen'ka), Юлечка (Yulechka)
- **By Nurse:** Голубушка (little dove), Душенька (little soul)
- **By parents:** Дочь (daughter), Юлия

**Social Rank:** Boyar's daughter (боярская дочь)

**Symbolism:** Yulia maintains the "Jul-" sound, connects to youth and innocence. Grigory (watchful) ironically contrasts with parents' blindness.

**Pronunciation:** YOO-lee-yah gree-GOR-yev-nah kah-poo-LEH-toh-vah

**Character Voice:** Articulate, clever, switches between formal speech (with parents) and intimate language (with Romeo, Nurse)

---

### Mercutio → Меркурий Александрович Вольский
**Full Name:** Меркурий Александрович Вольский (Merkury Alexandrovich Volsky)

**Name Breakdown:**
- **Имя:** Меркурий (Merkury) - Direct reference to Mercury (messenger god, quicksilver)
- **Отчество:** Александрович (Alexandrovich) - "son of Alexander" (defender of men)
- **Фамилия:** Вольский (Volsky) - From "воля" (will, freedom)

**Forms of Address:**
- **Formal:** Меркурий Александрович
- **Informal:** Меркурий, Меркуша (Merkusha)
- **By Romeo:** Меркутио (Merkutio) - Western-style nickname
- **By friends:** Вольный (Volny - "the free one")

**Social Rank:** Minor nobility, relative of the Prince/Governor

**Symbolism:** Mercury = quick wit, messenger, volatile. Volsky = free spirit, independence. Perfect for the witty, mercurial character.

**Pronunciation:** mehr-KOO-ree-y ah-lehk-SAHN-droh-veech VOHL-skee

**Character Voice:** Rapid speech, puns, wordplay, mixes high and low language, theatrical

---

### Tybalt → Тихон Иванович Капулетов
**Full Name:** Тихон Иванович Капулетов (Tikhon Ivanovich Kapuletov)

**Name Breakdown:**
- **Имя:** Тихон (Tikhon) - "quiet, peaceful" (ironic for hot-tempered character)
- **Отчество:** Иванович (Ivanovich) - "son of Ivan" (common, traditional)
- **Фамилия:** Капулетов (Kapuletov) - Russified Capulet

**Forms of Address:**
- **Formal:** Тихон Иванович
- **Informal:** Тихон, Тиша (Tisha)
- **By family:** Племянник (nephew), Тиша
- **By enemies:** Капулетов (just surname, dismissive)

**Social Rank:** Boyar's nephew

**Symbolism:** The irony of "Tikhon" (peaceful) for a violent character creates dramatic tension. Traditional patronymic grounds him in old feudal values.

**Pronunciation:** tee-KHOHN ee-VAH-noh-veech kah-poo-LEH-tohf

**Character Voice:** Formal, archaic speech patterns, uses lots of feudal honor terminology

### Benvolio → Бенедикт Сергеевич Монтягин
**Full Name:** Бенедикт Сергеевич Монтягин (Benedikt Sergeyevich Montyagin)

**Name Breakdown:**
- **Имя:** Бенедикт (Benedikt) - "blessed, well-spoken"
- **Отчество:** Сергеевич (Sergeyevich) - "son of Sergey" (servant, protector)
- **Фамилия:** Монтягин (Montyagin) - Montague family

**Forms of Address:**
- **Formal:** Бенедикт Сергеевич
- **Informal:** Бенедикт, Бéня (Benya)
- **By Romeo:** Брат (brother - metaphorical), Беня
- **By family:** Кузен (cousin)

**Social Rank:** Boyar's nephew, Romeo's cousin

**Symbolism:** Benedikt = blessed, well-wishing (matches Benvolio = "good will"). Sergey = protector fits his peacekeeping role.

**Pronunciation:** beh-neh-DEEKT sehr-GEH-yeh-veech mohn-TYAH-geen

**Character Voice:** Calm, measured, uses diplomatic language, voice of reason

### Paris → Павел Петрович Парский
**Full Name:** Павел Петрович Парский (Pavel Petrovich Parsky)

**Name Breakdown:**
- **Имя:** Павел (Pavel) - "small, humble" (ironic for noble suitor)
- **Отчество:** Петрович (Petrovich) - "son of Peter" (rock, foundation)
- **Фамилия:** Парский (Parsky) - Hints at Paris, but sounds authentically Russian

**Forms of Address:**
- **Formal:** Павел Петрович
- **Informal:** Павел, Паша (Pasha)
- **By Capulets:** Павел Петрович (always formal - respect)
- **By Governor:** Паша (familiar)

**Social Rank:** Noble, connected to Governor's administration

**Symbolism:** Pavel (humble) ironically contrasts with his privileged position. Solid patronymic suggests stability, establishment choice.

**Pronunciation:** PAH-vehl peh-TROH-veech PAHR-skee

**Character Voice:** Proper, courtly, formal speech patterns, educated

### Friar Lawrence → Отец Лаврентий
**Full Name:** Отец Лаврентий (Otets Lavrentiy)
**Monastic Name:** Лаврентий (Lavrentiy - from "laurel")
**Birth Name:** Леонид Григорьевич (Leonid Grigoryevich - not commonly used)

**Forms of Address:**
- **Formal:** Отец Лаврентий (Father Lavrentiy)
- **Informal/affectionate:** Батюшка (little father)
- **By Romeo:** Отец (Father), Батюшка
- **Official church:** Иеромонах Лаврентий (Hieromonk Lavrentiy)

**Role:** Orthodox monk, herbalist, confessor

**Rank:** Hieromonk (иеромонах) - monk who is also a priest

**Symbolism:** Lavrentiy = laurel (victory, wisdom). Traditional Orthodox monk who serves as spiritual guide and intermediary.

**Pronunciation:** ah-TYETS lahv-RYEN-tee-y

**Character Voice:** Uses Church Slavonic in prayers, mixing religious terminology with practical wisdom, educated speech

## RUSSIAN NOBILITY HIERARCHY (Context)

1. **Царь (Tsar)** - Supreme ruler (Moscow, not in Kazan)
2. **Князь (Knyaz/Prince)** - Prince Vyazemsky's rank, governor of region
3. **Боярин (Boyar)** - High nobility (Capulets, Montagues)
4. **Дворянин (Dvoryanin)** - Lesser nobility (Volsky, Parsky)
5. **Служилые люди (Service people)** - Various ranks
6. **Посадские люди (Townspeople)** - Merchants, craftsmen
7. **Крестьяне (Peasants)** - Servants, workers

## PRONUNCIATION GUIDE

### Key Sound Patterns

**Russian vowels:**
- А (ah) - as in "father"
- Е (yeh) - as in "yes"
- Ё (yoh) - as in "yore"
- И (ee) - as in "meet"
- О (oh) - as in "more"
- У (oo) - as in "boot"
- Ы (ih) - between "i" and "u"
- Э (eh) - as in "bet"
- Ю (yoo) - as in "you"
- Я (yah) - as in "yard"

**Stress is marked in CAPITALS** in pronunciation guides

**Softening:** Soft sign (ь) softens preceding consonant

## CHARACTER VOICE CONSIDERATIONS

### Speech Register Hierarchy

1. **Church Slavonic** - Religious contexts, dramatic speeches, oaths
2. **High Literary Russian** - Educated nobility (Romeo, Juliet in soliloquies)
3. **Formal Noble Speech** - Official interactions, parents, Prince
4. **Informal Noble Speech** - Friends, peers (Mercutio, Benvolio)
5. **Merchant/Townspeople** - More colloquial
6. **Servant/Peasant** - Folk language, sayings, earthy (Nurse, servants)

### Code-Switching

Characters switch registers based on context:
- **Romeo:** High poetry (alone/with Juliet) → Informal (with friends) → Formal (with parents)
- **Juliet:** Formal obedience (with parents) → Passionate eloquence (with Romeo)
- **Mercutio:** Playful mixing of high and low language for comic effect
- **Nurse:** Almost entirely folk/peasant register, occasional formal when addressing nobles

### Diminutives and Affection

Russian uses diminutives to show affection/intimacy:
- **Formal stage:** Юлия Григорьевна
- **Friendship:** Юля
- **Deep intimacy:** Юленька, Юлечка
- **Romeo can progress through these stages** as relationship deepens

### Formal "You" vs Informal "You"

- **Вы (Vy)** - Formal "you" (plural or polite singular)
  - Used with parents, authorities, strangers, formal situations
- **Ты (Ty)** - Informal "you" (singular)
  - Used with friends, lovers, children, servants (by masters)
  - Romeo and Juliet switch from Вы to Ты at balcony scene

## USAGE IN VERSE NOVEL

### Meter Considerations

Russian names have specific stress patterns that affect verse:
- **Роман** (ro-MAN) - 2 syllables, iambic
- **Юлия** (YU-li-ya) - 3 syllables, trochaic
- **Меркурий** (mer-KU-ri-y) - 4 syllables, anapestic
- **Тихон** (ti-KHON) - 2 syllables, iambic

Plan verse lines around natural name stresses.

### Rhyme Potential

- **-ин endings:** Монтягин, Капулетов (less rhyme-friendly)
- **Names for rhyme:** Роман/обман (Roman/deception), Юля/пуля (Yulya/bullet)
- **Patronymics:** -ович/-евич provide masculine rhyme options
- **Diminutives:** Рома/дома (Roma/home), Юля/буря (Yulya/storm)
