## Revision Process

### Technical Audit
- All 50 stanzas: form, meter, rhyme quality
- Stress verification
- No forbidden rhymes
- Rich/exact rhymes preferred
- Natural language despite constraints

### Voice Consistency
- Narrator voice consistent with Chapter 1 and within Chapter 2
- Roman's voice: melancholic but eloquent
- Merkury's voice: witty, vital, bawdy
- Benedikt's voice: gentle, peacemaking
- Yulia's voice: gentle, sheltered (if she speaks)
- Matryona's voice: warm, caring

### Character Development
- Roman: melancholy → transformation (first sight)
- Merkury: established as vital, witty (critical for his death Ch 5)
- Benedikt: gentle friend
- Yulia: introduced, sheltered, dutiful
- Matryona: surrogate mother, caring

### Narrative Flow
- Parallel tracks (Roman/Yulia) clear
- Persuasion sequence natural
- Journey and arrival atmospheric
- Chapter 2 ending strong cliffhanger
- Smooth transitions between scenes and digressions

### Digression Balance
- 65% digressive ratio maintained
- Digressions enhance rather than interrupt
- Philosophical depth evident
- Narrator's personality strengthens
