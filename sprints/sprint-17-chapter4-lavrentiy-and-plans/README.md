# Sprint 17: Chapter 4 - Otets Lavrentiy and Plans

**Chapter**: 4 - "The Secret Joy"
**Stanzas**: 16-30 (15 stanzas)
**Estimated Time**: 2-3 weeks (30-40 hours)

## Overview

Morning after balcony scene. Roman seeks Otets Lavrentiy (Friar Lawrence equivalent), explains situation. Lavrentiy surprised (different woman yesterday!) but agrees, hopes to end feud through marriage. Parallel: Yulia and Matryona - Yulia confesses love, Matryona shocked but sympathetic, agrees to carry messages. Digressions on Lavrentiy's character, Matryona's role, youth's impetuosity.

## Stanza Breakdown

**Stanzas 16-22**: Roman and Otets Lavrentiy
- Morning: Roman seeks priest
- Finds Lavrentiy in cell/house
- Explains situation (love at first sight, she's Kapuletova)

- But agrees: hopes marriage will end feud
- Plans for secret betrothal

**Stanzas 23-27**: Yulia and Matryona parallel
- Yulia confesses love to nurse
- Matryona shocked: "A Montyagin!"

**Stanzas 28-30**: Digressions
- Otets Lavrentiy's character (Orthodox priest, educated, herbs, hope to end feud)
- Matryona's role (surrogate mother, pragmatic romanticism)
- Youth's impetuosity (met yesterday, marrying today!)
