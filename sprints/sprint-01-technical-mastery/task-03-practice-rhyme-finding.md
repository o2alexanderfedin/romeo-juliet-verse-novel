## Steps

### 1. Review Forbidden Rhymes (15 minutes)
Read and memorize these critical restrictions:

**NEVER rhyme**:
- ❌ Verb infinitives: любить / говорить
- ❌ Verb conjugations: говорит / звонит
- ❌ Verb past tense: сказал / показал
- ❌ Grammatical endings: дома / города
- ❌ Trite pairs: любовь/кровь, розы/морозы

**Reference**: INDEX_TECHNICAL_QUICK_REFERENCE.md lines 65-82

### 2. Learn Good Rhyme Characteristics (15 minutes)

**Rich Rhymes (BEST)**:
- ✓ Consonants BEFORE stressed vowel match
- ✓ Example: **к**óшка / **к**рóшка

**Deep Rhymes (EXCELLENT)**:
- ✓ Entire syllable before stress matches
- ✓ Example: **ми**лéние / **ми**шéние

**Exact Rhymes (STANDARD)**:
- ✓ Match from stressed vowel onward
- ✓ Example: лýна / однá

**Mix Parts of Speech** (PREFERRED):
- ✓ Noun with adjective
- ✓ Noun with verb (NOT infinitive)
- ✓ Unexpected combinations

**Reference**: INDEX_TECHNICAL_QUICK_REFERENCE.md lines 85-107

### 3. Rifmovnik.ru Interface Practice (20 minutes)

Visit https://rifmovnik.ru/ and practice:

1. **Basic Search**:
   - Enter: зима (winter)
   - Review results
   - Note stress patterns marked

2. **Filter by Quality**:
   - Look for "точные" (exact) rhymes
   - Look for "богатые" (rich) rhymes
   - Avoid "бедные" (poor) rhymes

3. **Filter by Part of Speech**:
   - Find only noun rhymes
   - Find only adjective rhymes
   - Understand categories

4. **Stress Pattern Search**:
   - Search for masculine rhymes (-á, -ó)
   - Search for feminine rhymes (-ája, -ája)
   - Practice identifying stress positions

## Common Mistakes to Watch For

### Mistake 1: Accepting First Result
❌ Don't take the first rhyme Rifmovnik shows
✓ Browse through, find best quality rhyme

### Mistake 2: Verb Rhymes
❌ Easy to accidentally pick verb infinitive
✓ Always check part of speech before accepting

### Mistake 3: Trite Pairs
❌ любовь/кровь feels natural but it's overused
✓ Force yourself to find fresh rhymes

### Mistake 4: Grammatical Rhymes
❌ Two nouns in same case ending (дома/города)
✓ Mix different grammatical forms

### Mistake 5: Settling for Poor Rhymes
❌ "Close enough" approximate rhyme
✓ Keep searching for rich rhyme with matching consonants

## Pro Tips

1. **Build Personal Rhyme Bank**: Keep list of good rhymes for words you'll use often
2. **Study Pushkin's Rhymes**: See what he rhymed in Eugene Onegin
3. **Think Semantically**: Best rhymes often have interesting semantic contrast
4. **Consider Multiple Forms**: Try genitive, dative cases for more options
5. **Don't Force It**: If rhyme isn't working, restructure line instead
