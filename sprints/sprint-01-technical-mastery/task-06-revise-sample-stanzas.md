## Revision Strategy

### Step 1: Stanza 2 Complete Rewrite (60-90 minutes)

**Current Concept**: Moscow setting, winter, nobility, social season

**Keep**:
- Winter atmosphere
- Moscow as setting
- Nobility context
- Social season beginning

**Change**:
- ALL verb-ending rhymes must be eliminated
- Find noun/adjective/adverb rhymes instead
- May need to restructure entire stanza

**Process**:
1. List key concepts to convey
2. Choose NEW rhyme words (verify: no verbs!)
3. Build lines around these rhyme words
4. Verify stress for every word
5. Check scansion
6. Read aloud for naturalness
7. Revise until publication quality

### Step 2: Stanza 3 Revision (45-60 minutes)

**Focus**: Lines 5/7 (случилась / пролилась)

**Options**:
1. Replace both line-end words with nouns/adjectives
2. Restructure to say same thing without verbs at line-end
3. Consider different grammatical approach

**Process**:
1. Identify what lines 5/7 are expressing
2. Find alternative ways to express same content
3. Choose non-verb rhyme pair
4. Rewrite lines
5. Verify no other verb rhymes in stanza
6. Strengthen other weak rhymes

### Step 3: Stanza 1 Polish (30-45 minutes)

**Less Critical** (no forbidden rhymes, just weak ones)

**Focus**:
- Strengthen lines 1/9 rhyme
- Strengthen lines 3/5 rhyme
- Strengthen lines 7/13 rhyme
- Fix lines 11/14 stress pattern issue

**Process**:
1. For each weak rhyme pair, find richer alternative
2. Restructure lines as needed
3. Maintain meaning and tone
4. Verify all changes don't break other lines

## Detailed Revision Process

### For Each Line Being Revised:

1. **Identify Content Goal**: What must this line express?
2. **Choose Rhyme Word**: Select line-end word (verify: NOT verb!)
3. **Verify Stress**: Look up stress for chosen word
4. **Count Back from End**: Syllable 8 must be stressed
5. **Build Line**: Write words leading up to rhyme word
6. **Verify Each Word Stress**: All words in line
7. **Count Syllables**: 8 (masculine) or 9 (feminine)
8. **Check Scansion**: u — u — u — u — pattern
9. **Assess Natural Sound**: Read aloud
10. **Revise**: Adjust until natural and correct

### For Each Rhyme Pair:

1. **Verify Not Forbidden**:
   - Not verb infinitives
   - Not verb conjugations
   - Not verb past tense
   - Not same grammatical ending (if possible)

2. **Assess Quality**:
   - Exact rhyme (minimum)?
   - Rich rhyme (consonants before stress match)?
   - Deep rhyme (entire syllable before stress matches)?

3. **Check Part of Speech**:
   - Are they different parts of speech? (preferred)
   - If same, is it avoiding grammatical rhyme? (required)

## Common Revision Challenges

### Challenge 1: "I Can't Find Non-Verb Rhyme"
**Solution**:
- Try different line-end word entirely
- Restructure line order in stanza
- Consider different grammatical approach to content
- Use Rifmovnik.ru filters aggressively

### Challenge 2: "New Version Lost Original Meaning"
**Solution**:
- Meaning must be preserved
- Keep trying different approaches
- Sometimes takes 5-10 attempts
- Consult Eugene Onegin for how Pushkin solved similar problems

### Challenge 3: "New Version Sounds Forced"
**Solution**:
- Keep revising
- Natural sound comes with iteration
- Try completely different approach
- Sleep on it, return with fresh perspective

### Challenge 4: "This Is Taking Forever"
**Solution**:
- Quality takes time
- These are opening stanzas - critical to get right
- Better to spend time now than publish poor quality
- This practice makes later composition faster
