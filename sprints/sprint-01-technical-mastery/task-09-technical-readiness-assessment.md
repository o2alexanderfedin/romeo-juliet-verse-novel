## Assessment Framework

This assessment uses **three criteria** for readiness:
1. **Technical Proficiency** (can you execute the form correctly?)
2. **Efficiency** (can you work at reasonable speed?)
3. **Quality** (do your stanzas meet publication standards?)

**Decision Rule**:
- ✅ **READY**: Meet standards in all three areas
- ⚠️ **MARGINAL**: Meet 2 of 3, can proceed with caution
- ❌ **NOT READY**: Meet 0-1 of 3, need more practice

---

## Assessment Part 1: Technical Proficiency (Pass/Fail)

### Criterion 1.1: Onegin Stanza Form Execution

**Test**: Review your 5 practice stanzas and 3 revised sample stanzas (8 total)

Count how many have:
- [ ] Exactly 14 lines
- [ ] Perfect rhyme scheme (aBaBccDDeFFeGG)
- [ ] Correct feminine/masculine rhyme positions
- [ ] Iambic tetrameter (8 syllables, 9 for feminine)
- [ ] Final stress on syllable 8 (all 14 lines)

**Scoring**:
- 8/8 stanzas perfect = ✅ PASS
- 6-7/8 stanzas perfect = ⚠️ MARGINAL
- <6/8 stanzas perfect = ❌ FAIL

**Your Score**: ___ / 8

### Criterion 1.2: Forbidden Rhyme Avoidance

**Test**: Review all 8 stanzas for forbidden rhymes

Count how many contain:
- ❌ Verb infinitive rhymes
- ❌ Verb conjugation rhymes
- ❌ Verb past tense rhymes
- ❌ Grammatical ending rhymes (excessive)
- ❌ Trite pairs from Nabokov's catalog

**Scoring**:
- 0 forbidden rhymes = ✅ PASS
- 1-2 forbidden rhymes = ⚠️ MARGINAL (if in early practice stanzas only)
- 3+ forbidden rhymes = ❌ FAIL

**Your Count**: ___ forbidden rhymes found

### Criterion 1.3: Stress Verification Accuracy

**Test**: Pick 20 random words from your stanzas

For each word:
1. Mark what stress you USED in stanza
2. Look up CORRECT stress in dictionary
3. Compare

**Scoring**:
- 20/20 correct = ✅ PASS
- 18-19/20 correct = ⚠️ MARGINAL
- <18/20 correct = ❌ FAIL

**Your Score**: ___ / 20

## Assessment Part 3: Quality (Subjective)

### Criterion 3.1: Natural-Sounding Russian

**Test**: Read your best 3 stanzas aloud

Rate each on naturalness (1-5 scale):
- 5 = Sounds effortless, like Pushkin
- 4 = Mostly natural, few awkward moments
- 3 = Understandable but somewhat forced
- 2 = Often awkward, clearly struggling with meter
- 1 = Unnatural, word salad

**Scoring**:
- Average ≥ 4.0 = ✅ HIGH QUALITY
- Average 3.0-3.9 = ⚠️ ACCEPTABLE
- Average < 3.0 = ❌ NEEDS WORK

**Your Ratings**: ___ , ___ , ___ (Average: ___ )

### Criterion 3.2: Rhyme Quality

**Test**: Analyze all 56 rhyme pairs across 8 stanzas (7 pairs × 8 stanzas)

Classify each rhyme:
- **Rich**: Consonants before stress match
- **Exact**: From stressed vowel onward matches
- **Approximate**: Close but not exact
- **Weak**: Poor quality

**Scoring**:
- 70%+ rich or exact = ✅ HIGH QUALITY
- 50-69% rich or exact = ⚠️ ACCEPTABLE
- <50% rich or exact = ❌ NEEDS WORK

**Your Distribution**:
- Rich: ___ (___%)
- Exact: ___ (___%)
- Approximate: ___ (___%)
- Weak: ___ (___%)

### Criterion 3.3: Voice Consistency

**Test**: Review narrator voice in your stanzas

**Questions**:
1. Can you identify narrator's tone in each stanza? (Yes/No)
2. Is tone appropriate for content? (Yes/No)
3. Does voice feel consistent across stanzas? (Yes/No)
4. Is there successful reader address? (Yes/No)
5. Does it sound like a persona, not just you? (Yes/No)

**Scoring**:
- 5/5 Yes = ✅ HIGH QUALITY
- 3-4/5 Yes = ⚠️ ACCEPTABLE
- <3/5 Yes = ❌ NEEDS WORK

**Your Score**: ___ / 5

## Decision Matrix

### ✅ READY FOR NOVEL COMPOSITION
**Criteria**: All three parts score ✅, or two ✅ and one ⚠️

**Action**: Proceed to Sprint 02 (Chapter 1 Composition)

**Recommendations**:
- Begin Chapter 1, Stanzas 1-5
- Use your workflow checklist religiously
- Continue to verify everything
- Quality over speed initially
- Expect improvement over first 20-30 stanzas

### ⚠️ MARGINAL READINESS
**Criteria**: Two parts ⚠️ and one ✅, or one ✅ and two ⚠️

**Action**: Proceed with caution OR do targeted practice

**If proceeding**:
- Start slowly, expect revision
- Extra vigilance on weak areas
- May need to revise heavily later
- Consider this "learning on the job"

**If practicing more**:
- Compose 5 more practice stanzas
- Focus on weakest criteria
- Reassess after practice
- Then proceed when ready

### ❌ NOT READY FOR NOVEL COMPOSITION
**Criteria**: Two or more parts score ❌

**Action**: Complete additional practice (REQUIRED)

**Practice Plan**:
1. **If Technical Proficiency ❌**:
   - Review Russian Prosody Complete Guide again
   - Study more Onegin examples
   - Compose 10 more practice stanzas with focus on form
   - Reassess

2. **If Efficiency ❌**:
   - Practice rhyme finding (30 min daily for week)
   - Practice stress lookup (30 min daily for week)
   - Compose 5 more stanzas, tracking time improvement
   - Reassess

3. **If Quality ❌**:
   - Study Eugene Onegin more deeply (natural sound)
   - Focus on voice consistency
   - Read practice stanzas aloud repeatedly
   - Revise practice stanzas 2-3 times each
   - Compose 5 new stanzas with quality focus
   - Reassess
