## Key Content Requirements

### Tone Shift
- Chapter 5 is 60% narrative (most plot-driven)
- Sudden violence after Chapter 4's joy
- Fate's machinery in motion
- Narrator shocked, increasingly dark

### Characters
- Roman: pacifist because of secret marriage, misunderstood as cowardice
- Tikhon: hot-blooded, proud, family honor paramount
- Merkury: loyal friend, cannot bear insult to Roman
- Benedikt: peacemaker, trying to prevent violence

### Honor Culture
- Dueling inevitable in this society
- Insult demands response
- Roman's refusal incomprehensible (they don't know about marriage)
- Merkury steps in for friend's honor
