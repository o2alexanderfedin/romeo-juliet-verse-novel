## Overview

Day 4 morning after Roman's exile. Capulet household in mourning for Tikhon. Grigory's rage at Montyaginy. Yulia grieving (publicly for cousin, privately for husband). Grigory decides to hasten Yulia's marriage to Pavel - sooner the better (Thursday, just two days!). Tells Yulia. Yulia protests. Grigory angry, insists (might strike her). Mother ineffectual. Digressions on arranged marriage, parents' power, daughters' powerlessness.
