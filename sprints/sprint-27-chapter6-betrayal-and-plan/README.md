Digressions on Matryona's pragmatism, Pavel's character, Lavrentiy's desperate plan.

## Key Content Requirements

### Matryona's Betrayal
- Not malicious, but pragmatic
- Class perspective (survival over love)
- Yulia feels betrayed (trusted surrogate mother)
- Narrator's complex view (understands Matryona, but sides with Yulia)

### Pavel's Character
- Important digression: not villain
- Genuinely wishes to marry Yulia
- Decent man, tragedy he'll suffer too

- Desperate (threatens suicide)
- Grasps at plan (only hope)
- Courage building (will take potion)
