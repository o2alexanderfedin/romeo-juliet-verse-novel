## Revision Focus Areas

### Technical Perfection
- All 60 stanzas (longest chapter)
- Action scenes vivid within verse constraints
- Two duel scenes clear and distinct
- Death scenes moving

### Emotional Impact
- Merkury's death: shocking, mourned
- "Plague on both houses": powerful, memorable
- Roman's transformation: pacifist to killer
- Tikhon's death: consequence, not triumph
- Yulia's conflict: cousin vs. husband
- Final parting: reluctant, sorrowful
- Chapter as turning point: joy to tragedy

### Character Consistency
- Roman: pacifist → anguished → enraged → horrified → loving → exiled
- Merkury: witty to the end, death meaningful
- Tikhon: hot-blooded pride, dies as he lived
- Yulia: chooses love over family (transformation)
- Benedikt: loyal friend, peacemaker failed
- Narrator: shocked, mourning, increasingly dark

### Pacing and Action
- 60% narrative (most plot-driven chapter)
- Fast pacing for action
- Slow for emotional moments (deaths, parting)
- Digressions enhance rather than interrupt
- Two duels distinct (first formal/Merkury, second immediate/Roman)

### Thematic Depth
- Violence begets violence
- Honor codes' destructiveness
- Friendship and loyalty
- Fate's inexorability
- Love vs. family duty
- Consequences (immediate and severe)
