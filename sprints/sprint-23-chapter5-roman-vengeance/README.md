## Key Content Requirements

### Roman's Transformation
- From pacifist (wouldn't fight Tikhon) to avenger
- Anguish over Merkury's death
- Rage overcomes reason
- Love vs. honor vs. friendship conflict
- After killing: horror at what he's done

### Second Duel
- Immediate, hot-blooded (unlike first duel's formality)
- Roman's skill or luck
- Tikhon dies
- No satisfaction, only consequences

### Justice Scene
- Governor must judge (law and order)
- Exile rather than execution (mercy: Tikhon provoked, Merkury killed first)
- Roman's guilt and innocence both acknowledged
- Law vs. passion

### Yulia's Conflict
- Cousin dead by husband's hand
- Love and family loyalty in conflict
- Her choice: chooses Roman (love over family)
- Matryona may be present, react
- Yulia's transformation: from girl to woman making hard choice
