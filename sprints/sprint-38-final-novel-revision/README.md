## Revision Areas

### 1. Technical Perfection (All 400 Stanzas)
- Form: Every stanza aBaBccDDeFFeGG, 14 lines
- Meter: Iambic tetrameter throughout
- Rhyme: Zero forbidden rhymes (no verb rhymes, no grammatical endings)
- Stress: Every syllable stress verified

- Line 8: Mandatory stress syllable 8
- Natural language despite strict constraints

**Method**: Systematic chapter-by-chapter audit, create issues list, fix all issues

### 2. Voice Consistency (Narrator and Characters)

**Narrator Voice Evolution**:
- Chapter 1-2: Detached, ironic, scene-setting
- Chapter 3-4: More engaged, sympathetic to lovers
- Chapter 5: Shocked, mourning (especially Merkury)
- Chapter 6-7: Increasingly anguished, knowing tragedy approaches
- Chapter 8: Elegiac, philosophical, final wisdom

Ensure smooth transitions between tones.

**Character Voices**:
- **Roman**: Melancholic (Ch 1-2) → passionate (Ch 3-4) → violent (Ch 5) → despairing (Ch 7)
- **Yulia**: Dutiful (Ch 2-3) → awakened (Ch 3-4) → courageous (Ch 6-7)
- **Merkury**: Witty, vital (Ch 2) → dying with dignity (Ch 5)
- **Matryona**: Warm (Ch 2-4) → pragmatic betrayer (Ch 6)
- **Lavrentiy**: Well-meaning throughout, flawed judgment
- **Grigory**: Patriarchal authority (Ch 1, 3, 6)
- **Tikhon**: Hot-blooded pride (Ch 3, 5)

All voices distinct and consistent within their arcs.
