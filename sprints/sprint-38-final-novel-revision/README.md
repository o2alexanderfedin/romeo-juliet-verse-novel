## Revision Areas

### 1. Technical Perfection (All 400 Stanzas)
- Form: Every stanza aBaBccDDeFFeGG, 14 lines
- Meter: Iambic tetrameter throughout
- Rhyme: Zero forbidden rhymes (no verb rhymes, no grammatical endings)
- Stress: Every syllable stress verified
- Quality: Rich > exact > approximate rhymes
- Line 8: Mandatory stress syllable 8
- Natural language despite strict constraints

**Method**: Systematic chapter-by-chapter audit, create issues list, fix all issues

### 2. Voice Consistency (Narrator and Characters)

**Narrator Voice Evolution**:
- Chapter 1-2: Detached, ironic, scene-setting
- Chapter 3-4: More engaged, sympathetic to lovers
- Chapter 5: Shocked, mourning (especially Merkury)
- Chapter 6-7: Increasingly anguished, knowing tragedy approaches
- Chapter 8: Elegiac, philosophical, final wisdom

Ensure smooth transitions between tones.

**Character Voices**:
- **Roman**: Melancholic (Ch 1-2) → passionate (Ch 3-4) → violent (Ch 5) → despairing (Ch 7)
- **Yulia**: Dutiful (Ch 2-3) → awakened (Ch 3-4) → courageous (Ch 6-7)
- **Merkury**: Witty, vital (Ch 2) → dying with dignity (Ch 5)
- **Matryona**: Warm (Ch 2-4) → pragmatic betrayer (Ch 6)
- **Lavrentiy**: Well-meaning throughout, flawed judgment
- **Grigory**: Patriarchal authority (Ch 1, 3, 6)
- **Tikhon**: Hot-blooded pride (Ch 3, 5)

All voices distinct and consistent within their arcs.

### 3. Narrative Coherence (Cross-Chapter)

**Plot Continuity**:
- Timeline: 5 days + prologue + aftermath (verify consistency)
- Causality: Each event follows logically from previous
- Foreshadowing: Chapter 1 predictions fulfilled Chapter 7
- Setup/payoff: All setups have payoffs

**Character Arcs**:
- Roman: Complete arc from melancholy youth to tragic death
- Yulia: Complete arc from dutiful daughter to tragic heroine
- Supporting characters: Consistent development
- Relationships: Evolution tracked (Roman/Yulia, Roman/Merkury, Yulia/Matryona)

**Parallel Structures**:
- Chapter 2: Roman and Yulia parallel tracks
- Chapters 1-4 vs. 5-8: Joy to tragedy structure
- Digressions complement narrative throughout

### 4. Thematic Unity

**Core Themes** (present throughout, culminate in ending):
- Fate vs. free will (both, inseparable)
- Ancient grudges and their irrationality
- Youth vs. age, parents vs. children
- Romantic love vs. arranged marriage
- Public vs. private, society's codes
- Violence begets violence
- Communication's fragility
- Love and death intertwined
- Memory and monument

Ensure all themes introduced, developed, resolved.

### 5. Digression Balance

**Target Ratios**:
- Chapter 1: 30% narrative, 70% digressive ✓
- Chapter 2: 35% narrative, 65% digressive ✓
- Chapter 3: 44% narrative, 56% digressive ✓
- Chapter 4: 40% narrative, 60% digressive ✓
- Chapter 5: 60% narrative, 40% digressive ✓
- Chapter 6: 48% narrative, 52% digressive ✓
- Chapter 7: 58% narrative, 42% digressive ✓
- Chapter 8: 35% narrative, 65% digressive ✓
- **Overall**: ~44% narrative, 56% digressive

Verify ratios, adjust if needed. Ensure digressions enhance rather than interrupt.

### 6. Pacing and Momentum

**Arc Structure**:
- Act 1 (Ch 1-2): Slow build, scene-setting
- Act 2 (Ch 3-4): Love blooms, intensity rises
- Act 3 (Ch 5): Crisis, violence, peak intensity
- Act 4 (Ch 6-7): Desperation and deaths
- Act 5 (Ch 8): Resolution, elegiac

Verify pacing appropriate to each section.

### 7. Russian Language Excellence

- Era-appropriate vocabulary and syntax
- Russian prosody honored
- Stress patterns authentic
- No awkward inversions for rhyme
- Dialogue believable within verse
