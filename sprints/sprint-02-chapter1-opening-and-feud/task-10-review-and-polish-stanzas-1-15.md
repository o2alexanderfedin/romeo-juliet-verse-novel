# Task 10: Review and Polish Stanzas 1-15

**Sprint**: 02 - Chapter 1: Opening & Feud
**Stanza Numbers**: 1-15 of 400 (Review)
**Estimated Time**: 4-6 hours
**Priority**: CRITICAL

## Objective

Complete review of all 15 opening stanzas for voice consistency, technical accuracy, and publication readiness.

## Review Process

### Phase 1: Voice Consistency (90 min)
Read all 15 stanzas in sequence. Check:
- [ ] Narrator voice consistent across all stanzas
- [ ] Tone appropriate for Chapter 1 opening stage
- [ ] Reader address natural and building intimacy
- [ ] Ironic/sympathetic/philosophical balance correct

### Phase 2: Technical Audit (90 min)
For each stanza, verify:
- [ ] Perfect Onegin form (aBaBccDDeFFeGG)
- [ ] All stress patterns correct
- [ ] Zero forbidden rhymes
- [ ] Rhyme quality rich/exact
- [ ] Meter perfect

### Phase 3: Narrative Flow (60 min)
- [ ] Stanzas 1-5 establish opening effectively
- [ ] Stanzas 6-15 tell feud story clearly
- [ ] Transitions smooth between all stanzas
- [ ] Pacing appropriate (70% digressive, 30% narrative)

### Phase 4: Read Aloud Test (30 min)
- Read entire 15-stanza sequence aloud
- Mark any awkward moments
- Note voice breaks

### Phase 5: Polish (60-90 min)
- Fix all identified issues
- Strengthen weak rhymes
- Improve any forced language
- Final verification
