**3. Irrationality of Feuds**:
- St.9: "Ancient grudge" - origins forgotten
- St.20: Feuds irrational, destructive, accomplish nothing
- Social critique strong (Pushkin-like)
- Universal human folly examined

**4. Parents vs. Children**:
- Parents' decisions destroyed children
- Grigory forced marriage
- Vladimir perpetuated feud
- Children's love vs. parents' hatred
- Resolution: Parents realize they failed children

**5. Tragedy's Aftermath**:
- How quickly violence ends when tragedy strikes
- Grief supersedes hatred (temporarily)
- Everyone human before death
- But: damage done, irreversible

**6. Guilt and Responsibility**:
- Personal guilt (Grigory, Vladimir)
- Collective guilt (both families)
- Lavrentiy's guilt (plan failed)
- Everyone shares responsibility
- Foundation for reconciliation
