## Content Guidelines

**Stanza 1**: Chapter 2 opening, Roman waking or already awake
- Transition from Chapter 1 (feast is tonight)
- Moscow morning atmosphere (winter light, sounds)
- Roman's melancholy state
- Perhaps digression on morning or youth

**Stanza 2**: Roman's thoughts or wandering
- Physical movement (wandering estate/streets) OR interior contemplation
- Thoughts of unrequited love (woman from Chapter 1, not named)
- Visual details of Moscow winter morning
- Narrator may comment on his state

**Stanza 3**: Continuation or shift
- Deepen Roman's melancholy
- OR Benedikt's arrival (friend coming to cheer him)
- Could be digressive (narrator on youth/love)
- OR continue narrative (Roman's solitude)

## Key Requirements

### Voice
- **Roman** (if speaking): Melancholic, poetic, genuine feeling
- **Narrator**: Sympathetic but gently ironic, philosophical
- Establish Chapter 2 tone (more playful than Chapter 1 ending)

### Atmosphere
- Moscow morning
- Winter setting (daylight, cold)
- Domestic or public space
- Time markers (day of the feast)

### Narrative Function
- Open Chapter 2 smoothly
- Establish Roman's continued melancholy
- Show time has passed since Chapter 1
- Set up friends' arrival and persuasion

### Technical Standards
- Perfect Onegin stanza form
- Natural Russian despite constraints
- Rich/exact rhymes preferred
- No forbidden rhymes
- Stress verification for every word
