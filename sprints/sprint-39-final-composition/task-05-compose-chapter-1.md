## Composition Workflow

**For Each Stanza** (follow 8-step process from COMPOSITION_PLAN.md Part 3, Section 3.3):

1. **Framework Review** (30 seconds)
   - Read content requirements
   - Review rhyme options (3-4 per position)
   - Check vocabulary bank
   - Note voice guidance

2. **First Draft** (1-2 minutes)
   - Lines 1-4 (aBaB): Choose rhymes, compose in iambic tetrameter
   - Lines 5-8 (ccDD): Line 8 MANDATORY stress on syllable 8
   - Lines 9-12 (eFfe): Continue narrative
   - Lines 13-14 (GG): Concluding couplet

3. **Technical Verification** (30 seconds)
   - 14 lines? Rhyme scheme correct?
   - All stresses verified? (NEVER guess!)
   - Line 8 stress on syllable 8?
   - Zero forbidden rhymes?

4. **Voice Verification** (15 seconds)
   - Narrator tone: 60% ironic, 30% phil, 10% sym?
   - Reader address formal?
   - Digressive style natural?

5. **Read-Aloud Test** (30 seconds)
   - Does it sound natural?
   - Meter invisible (like Pushkin)?
   - Meaning clear?

6. **Content Verification** (15 seconds)
   - Required content included?
   - Continuity maintained?

7. **Revision** (30-60 seconds)
   - Polish weak spots
   - Strengthen rhymes
   - Perfect precision

8. **Final Approval** (10 seconds)
   - All quality gates passed?
   - Publication-ready?
   - Mark COMPLETE, move to next stanza

**Average Time Per Stanza**: 2-3 minutes = 110-165 minutes for 55 stanzas
