## Audit Scope

### Segment 1: Roman's Vault Monologue (St.22-30) - 9 stanzas

**Audit Questions**:
1. **Vocabulary Depth**: Does each stanza have 300-500+ stress-marked words?
2. **Rhyme Resources**: Does each stanza have 150-250+ verified rhyme pairs?
3. **Emotional Arc Design**:
   - St.22-24: Entering vault, finding Yulia "dead"
   - St.25-27: Despair, grief, speaking to her body
   - St.28-30: Decision to die, poison preparation, final words
4. **Dramatic Irony**: Is reader's knowledge (Yulia alive) exploited for maximum grief?
5. **Monologue Quality**: Does Roman's speech equal balcony scene eloquence?

**Quality Target**: Each stanza A+ (gold standard)

### Segment 2: Roman's Poisoning (St.31-34) - 4 stanzas

**Audit Questions**:
1. **Pacing**: Swift and devastating, not drawn out?
2. **Physical Detail**: Poison drinking, last kiss, death described?
3. **Emotional Impact**: Reader devastated (even knowing he's wrong)?
4. **Dramatic Irony Peak**: Roman dies believing Yulia dead, reader knows truth

**Quality Target**: A+ (devastating)

### Segment 3: Yulia's Awakening (St.35-40) - 6 stanzas

**Audit Questions**:
1. **Awakening**: Confusion, remembering potion, hope Roman is there?
2. **Discovery**: Finding Roman dead, understanding what happened?
3. **Emotional Crescendo**: Hope → confusion → horror → despair?
4. **Ultimate Tragedy**: She wakes moments too late, all for nothing?

**Quality Target**: A+ (unbearable tragedy)

### Segment 4: Yulia's Death (St.41-42) - 2 stanzas

**Audit Questions**:
1. **Dagger Scene**: Does she find his dagger? Physical act described?
2. **Final Words**: What does she say? To Roman? About fate?
3. **Tragic Finality**: Does death feel like release or additional horror?

**Quality Target**: A+ (cathartic despite tragedy)

## Audit Process

### Step 1: Read Sequentially (30-45 minutes)

Read all 21 stanzas in order across 4 framework files:
1. `stanzas_22-26_frameworks.md` - Read St.22-26
2. `stanzas_27-30_frameworks.md` - Read St.27-30
3. `stanzas_31-37_frameworks.md` - Read St.31-37 (focus on 31-34 for audit)
4. `stanzas_38-45_frameworks.md` - Read St.38-42 (focus on 38-42 for audit)

**Note-taking**: For each stanza, assess:
- Vocabulary depth (adequate/strong/gold-standard?)
- Rhyme resources (adequate/strong/gold-standard?)
- Emotional beat design (present/detailed/powerful?)
- Overall quality grade (B/A-/A/A+)

### Step 2: Identify Gaps (15-30 minutes)

**Create Gap List**:
- Which stanzas lack vocabulary depth?
- Which stanzas lack rhyme resources?
- Which stanzas lack emotional beat design?
- Which stanzas are below gold standard (anything less than A)?

**Prioritize**: Critical gaps vs. nice-to-have enhancements

### Step 3: Enhance Inadequate Frameworks (30-60 minutes)

**For Each Gap**:
1. Expand vocabulary bank (to 300-500+ words)
2. Generate additional rhyme pairs (to 150-250+ pairs)
3. Detail emotional beats (line-by-line guidance)
4. Add technical support (Line 8 verification, forbidden rhyme warnings)

**Enhancement Standard**: Match Merkury's death quality (1,460+ lines total for 6 stanzas = ~240 lines per stanza)

### Step 4: Verify Emotional Arc (15-30 minutes)

**Read Enhanced Sequence**:
- Does St.22-30 build to Roman's death?
- Does St.31-34 devastate?
- Does St.35-40 create unbearable tension?
- Does St.41-42 provide cathartic release?

**Test**: Would you cry reading this sequence? If not, enhance more.
