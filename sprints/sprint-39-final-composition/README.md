## Technical Requirements (ALL STANZAS)

From **[INDEX_TECHNICAL_QUICK_REFERENCE.md](../../INDEX_TECHNICAL_QUICK_REFERENCE.md)**:

### Onegin Stanza Form
- **14 lines** exactly
- **Rhyme scheme**: aBaBccDDeFFeGG
- **Meter**: Iambic tetrameter (stress on syllables 2, 4, 6, 8)
- **Line 8**: MANDATORY stress on syllable 8 (inviolable)
- **Feminine rhymes** (lines 1,3,5,6,9,12): 9 syllables
- **Masculine rhymes** (lines 2,4,7,8,10,11,13,14): 8 syllables

### Forbidden Rhymes (ZERO TOLERANCE)
- ❌ Verb infinitives (-ть, -ти endings)
- ❌ Verb conjugations (говорит/звонит)
- ❌ Verb past tense (-л/-ла/-ло/-ли)
- ❌ Grammatical endings (too easy/banal)
- ❌ Trite pairs (любовь/кровь, розы/морозы)

### Required Rhyme Quality
- ✅ 50%+ rich rhymes (consonants before stressed vowel match)
- ✅ Mix parts of speech (noun with adjective, not two nouns)
- ✅ Exact rhymes minimum (stressed vowel + all following sounds match)

### Russian Stress Rules
- ✅ NEVER guess stress - unpredictable in Russian
- ✅ ALWAYS verify with dictionaries (Wiktionary, Rifmovnik.ru)
- ✅ Mark stresses: á, é, í, ó, ú, ы́, э́, ю́, я́
- ✅ ё (yo) is ALWAYS stressed (most reliable indicator)

## Composition Workflow (Per Stanza)

From **[COMPOSITION_PLAN.md](../../COMPOSITION_PLAN.md)** Part 3, Section 3.3:

**8-Step Process** (2-3 minutes per stanza with AI):
1. Framework review (content, rhyme options, voice guidance)
2. First draft (14 lines in iambic tetrameter)
3. Technical verification (meter, rhyme, stress)
4. Voice verification (narrator/character consistency)
5. Read-aloud test (natural sound)
6. Content verification (requirements met)
7. Revision (polish weak spots)
8. Final approval (all quality gates passed)

**Use frameworks religiously** - they contain:
- Pre-planned rhyme schemes with verified options
- Stress-marked vocabulary banks (150-350+ words per stanza)
- Rhyme pair collections (75-200+ pairs per stanza)
- Voice guidance and tone specifications
- Content requirements and continuity notes
