**Format**: word [stress] - translation (part of speech)

### 2. Rhyme Pairs Collection (150-250+ pairs)

**Detail each beat**:
- Physical state description

- Internal voice/thought (what character thinks)
- Line-by-line guidance (which lines convey this beat)
- Vocabulary focus
- Connection to dramatic arc

### 4. Technical Support
- Pre-planned rhyme scheme options
- Line 8 mandatory stress verification examples

- Voice guidance (Roman: eloquent despair; Yulia: awakening horror)

### 5. Dramatic Guidance
