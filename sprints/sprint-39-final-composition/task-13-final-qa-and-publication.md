## Final QA Process

### Phase 3A: Complete Read-Aloud Test (4-5 hours)

**Objective**: Read entire 400-stanza novel aloud, note all issues

**Process**:
1. **Setup**:
   - Clear 4-5 hour block (no interruptions)
   - Prepare note-taking system
   - Have Russian dictionaries ready

2. **Read Aloud Chapter by Chapter**:
   - Chapter 1 (55 stanzas): ~35-40 minutes
   - Chapter 2 (50 stanzas): ~30-35 minutes
   - Chapter 3 (45 stanzas): ~30 minutes
   - Chapter 4 (55 stanzas): ~35-40 minutes
   - Chapter 5 (60 stanzas): ~40-45 minutes
   - Chapter 6 (50 stanzas): ~30-35 minutes
   - Chapter 7 (45 stanzas): ~30 minutes
   - Chapter 8 (40 stanzas): ~25-30 minutes
   - **Total**: ~4-5 hours

3. **Note Every Issue**:
   - Awkward phrasing (doesn't sound natural)
   - Meter stumbles (forced stress patterns)
   - Voice inconsistencies
   - Continuity gaps between stanzas
   - Unclear meaning
   - Weak rhymes
   - Any technical errors discovered

4. **Mark Peak Moments**:
   - Did 10 peak moments work as intended?
   - Did you cry at deaths?
   - Did ending provide catharsis?
   - Were you moved?

**Deliverable**: `FULL_NOVEL_READ_ALOUD_REPORT.md`
- Complete issue list
- Peak moments assessment
- Overall flow evaluation
- Emotional impact notes

---

### Phase 3B: Technical Perfection Audit (1-2 hours)

**Objective**: Verify 100% technical perfection (zero errors tolerated)

**Process**:
1. **Random Sample Technical Audit** (10% = 40 stanzas):
   - Select random stanzas from each chapter
   - Verify: 14 lines, correct rhyme scheme, all stresses verified
   - Check Line 8 stress on syllable 8
   - Check for forbidden rhymes
   - Extrapolate error rate

2. **If ANY errors found in sample**:
   - Audit 100% of stanzas (all 400)
   - Fix every error
   - No compromises

3. **Critical Stanza Verification** (100% = all peak moments):
   - Verify all 10 peak moments technically perfect
   - Opening (Ch 1, St.1)
   - First sight (Ch 2, St.49-50)
   - First meeting (Ch 3, St.31-37)
   - Balcony (Ch 4, St.1-10)
   - Merkury's death (Ch 5, St.25-30)
   - Tikhon's death (Ch 5, St.36-38)
   - Lark/nightingale (Ch 5, St.54)
   - Potion (Ch 6, St.46-48)
   - Both deaths (Ch 7, St.22-42)
   - Ending (Ch 8, St.37-40)

**Deliverable**: `TECHNICAL_PERFECTION_AUDIT_REPORT.md`
- Sample audit results
- Error list (if any)
- Peak moments verification
- Technical perfection certification (or failure notification)

4. **Re-test Revised Stanzas**:
