### Process Reminders

**For Each Stanza**:
1. Read framework KEY MOMENT description
2. Review Structure Guidance (line-by-line)
3. Consult Vocabulary Bank for word choices
4. Select rhyme pairs from suggestions
5. Draft all 14 lines following emotional beats
6. Verify meter (syllables, stress pattern)
7. Verify rhyme scheme (aBaBccDDeFFeGG)
8. Verify Line 8 mandatory stress (syllable 8)
9. Check character voice consistency
10. Read aloud for natural sound
11. Polish and finalize

**Critical Warnings**:
- ❌ NEVER guess Russian stress - verify with dictionary
- ❌ NEVER use verb infinitive rhymes (-ть/-ти)
- ❌ NEVER use verb conjugation rhymes (говорит/звонит)
- ❌ NEVER use trite pairs (любовь/кровь, розы/морозы)
- ✅ ALWAYS verify Line 8 stress on syllable 8
- ✅ ALWAYS read aloud for naturalness
