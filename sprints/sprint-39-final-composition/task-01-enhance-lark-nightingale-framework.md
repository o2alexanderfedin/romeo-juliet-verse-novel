## Requirements

### 1. Vocabulary Bank (150-200+ stress-marked words)

**Categories to Build**:
- **Dawn imagery**: light, frost, church bells, cold, morning
- **Bird vocabulary**: lark (жáворонок), nightingale (соловéй), song, call
- **Denial/reluctance**: "not yet", "stay", "it's still night", "darkness remains"
- **Acceptance/parting**: "morning", "must go", "farewell", "day breaks"
- **Moscow winter context**: frost, bells, pale light, breath misting, cold
- **Emotional words**: reluctance, longing, parting, sorrow, love

**Format**: Russian word [stress-marked] - English translation (part of speech)

**Example Entry**:
```
жáворонок [ZHÁ-vo-ro-nok] - lark (m, nom)
соловéй [so-lo-VÉY] - nightingale (m, nom)
рассвéт [ras-SVÉT] - dawn (m, nom)
```

### 2. Rhyme Pairs Collection (75-100+ verified pairs)

**Thematic Groups**:
- Dawn/night opposition (light/dark, morning/evening)
- Bird-related rhymes (song, call, flight)
- Love/parting emotions (sorrow/tomorrow, stay/away)
- Dialogue-friendly rhymes (natural speech patterns)

**Verify ALL stresses** with Rifmovnik.ru or Wiktionary

**Format**: word1 / word2 - [rhyme type: rich/exact/adequate]

### 3. Dialogue Framework

**Structure**:
- Lines 1-4 (aBaB): Roman speaks OR alternating
- Lines 5-8 (ccDD): Yulia speaks OR alternating
- Lines 9-12 (eFfe): Continued exchange
- Lines 13-14 (GG): Resolution (both acknowledge dawn)

**Voice Requirements**:
- **Roman**: Elevated, insisting it's morning (protective, wants Yulia safe)
- **Yulia**: Gentle but denying, wants to delay (desperate for more time)

**Specify**: Who speaks which lines, or if narrator mediates

### 4. Emotional Beat Design (7-8 beats)

**Progression**:
1. **Reluctance**: Neither wants to acknowledge dawn
2. **Denial begins**: Yulia insists it's nightingale, not lark
3. **Reality intrudes**: Light increasing, church bells begin
4. **Roman's insistence**: Must go or death awaits in Moscow
5. **Yulia's resistance**: Stay, light is not day yet
6. **Forced acceptance**: Both know truth, dawn has come
7. **Heartrending parting**: Final embrace, kiss, farewell
8. **Roman departs**: Yulia watches him go, sun rises

**Detail each beat**: What's said, what's felt, what's seen

### 5. Cultural Adaptation (Moscow Winter, Not Verona Summer)

**Adaptations**:
- Not just lark's song → Church bells + lark + pale light
- Not warm summer → Frost, cold breath, winter dawn
- Not balcony → Window, cold air, possibly snow
- Russian bird symbolism if different from English

### 6. Technical Support

**Provide**:
- Pre-planned rhyme scheme for stanza with dialogue
- Line 8 mandatory stress verification
- Forbidden rhyme warnings for dialogue (avoid verb infinitives)
- Natural speech pattern guidance (iambic tetrameter in dialogue is HARD)
