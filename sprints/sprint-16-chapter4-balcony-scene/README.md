## Key Content Requirements

### Balcony Scene Excellence
- Most famous scene in Romeo & Juliet
- Must be powerful in Russian verse
- "Wherefore" = "Why" (not "Where")
- "What's in a name?" theme central
- Vows believable and moving
- Parting reluctant but necessary (dawn)

### Russian Adaptation
- Garden setting appropriate to Moscow estate
- Window or balcony era-appropriate
- Cold night (winter) affects staging
- Russian poetry tradition honored
- Names: "Montyagin" and "Kapuletov" in verse

### Digressions
- Balcony scene poetry theme
- Night as concealment and intimacy
- Garden as threshold space
- Identity vs. name philosophy
- Narrator's lyrical response
