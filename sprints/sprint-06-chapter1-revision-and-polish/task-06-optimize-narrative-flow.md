# Task 06: Optimize Narrative Flow

**Sprint**: 06 - Chapter 1 Revision
**Scope**: Transitions, pacing, balance across 55 stanzas
**Time**: 4-5 hours
**Priority**: MEDIUM

## Objective

Optimize narrative flow: smooth transitions, appropriate pacing, narrative/digression balance.

## Audit Process

### Check Transitions:
- Between sections (1→2, 2→3, etc.)
- Between stanzas within sections
- Between narrative and digression
- Are they smooth or jarring?

### Check Pacing:
- Does action move appropriately?
- Are digressions paced well?
- Any sections drag or rush?

### Check Balance:
**Target**: ~30% narrative, ~70% digressive
- Count narrative vs digressive stanzas
- Verify balance appropriate

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] All transitions reviewed
- [ ] Jarring transitions fixed
- [ ] Pacing optimized
- [ ] Balance verified (~70% digressive)
- [ ] Chapter flows smoothly
