# Task 01: Complete Technical Audit (All 55 Stanzas)

**Sprint**: 06 - Chapter 1 Revision
**Scope**: All 55 stanzas
**Time**: 6-8 hours
**Priority**: CRITICAL

## Objective

Verify technical perfection of every stanza: form, meter, rhyme quality, stress patterns.

## Audit Process

### For Each Stanza (1-55):
1. Verify 14 lines, aBaBccDDeFFeGG rhyme scheme
2. Count syllables (8 or 9 appropriate to rhyme type)
3. Mark ALL stress patterns
4. Verify stress on syllable 8 (every line)
5. Check no odd positions stressed
6. Verify NO forbidden rhymes
7. Assess rhyme quality (rich > exact > approximate)

### Tools:
- INDEX_TECHNICAL_QUICK_REFERENCE.md verification checklist
- Wiktionary for stress verification
- Spreadsheet to track issues

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] All 55 stanzas audited
- [ ] Technical issues documented
- [ ] Priority fixes identified
- [ ] Zero forbidden rhymes confirmed
- [ ] Stress patterns 100% verified

## Output

File: `composition/chapter_01/technical_audit_results.md`

Include:
- Issues found per stanza
- Priority ranking (critical/high/medium)
- Estimated fix time
- Overall technical quality score
