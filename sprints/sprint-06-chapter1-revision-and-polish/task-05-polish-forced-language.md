# Task 05: Polish Forced Language

**Sprint**: 06 - Chapter 1 Revision
**Scope**: Any unnatural-sounding lines in 55 stanzas
**Time**: 6-8 hours
**Priority**: MEDIUM

## Objective

Identify and revise lines that sound forced or unnatural, making language flow smoothly within strict form.

## Process

### Phase 1: Read-Aloud Test (2 hours)
Read all 55 stanzas aloud in Russian.
Mark every line that sounds:
- Forced or awkward
- Unnatural word order
- Archaic without purpose
- Unclear meaning

### Phase 2: Revise Forced Lines (4-6 hours)
For each marked line:
1. Identify why it sounds forced
2. Try alternative word order
3. Try different vocabulary
4. Simplify if complex
5. Re-verify meter and rhyme
6. Read aloud again

**Goal**: Every line sounds natural despite strict form

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] All 55 stanzas read aloud
- [ ] Forced lines identified
- [ ] Revisions completed
- [ ] All lines now sound natural
- [ ] Meter and rhyme maintained
