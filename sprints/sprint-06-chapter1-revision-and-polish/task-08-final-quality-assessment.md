Make final quality assessment: Is Chapter 1 publication-ready? Is quality standard set for remaining chapters?

## Assessment Process

### Technical Excellence:
- [ ] All 55 stanzas perfect Onegin form
- [ ] Zero forbidden rhymes
- [ ] All stress patterns verified
- [ ] 70%+ rich or exact rhymes
- [ ] All meter correct

### Artistic Quality:
- [ ] Voice consistent throughout
- [ ] All language natural-sounding
- [ ] Characters clear and distinct
- [ ] Narrative compelling
- [ ] Opening hooks reader
- [ ] Ending creates anticipation

### Pushkin Standard:
- [ ] Would Pushkin recognize this as akin to his work?
- [ ] Technical mastery demonstrated?
- [ ] Deceptive simplicity achieved?
- [ ] Irony and sympathy balanced?

### Publication Readiness:
- [ ] Would you publish this?
- [ ] Proud of this work?
- [ ] Quality standard for 345 more stanzas?

## Decision Matrix

**READY FOR CHAPTER 2**: All criteria met, confident in quality
**NEEDS MORE REVISION**: Some criteria not met, additional revision sprint needed
**RESTART NEEDED**: Fundamental problems, consider rewriting sections
