# Task 04: Strengthen Weak Rhymes

**Sprint**: 06 - Chapter 1 Revision
**Scope**: All weak/approximate rhymes in 55 stanzas
**Time**: 8-10 hours
**Priority**: HIGH

## Objective

Replace weak and approximate rhymes with rich or exact rhymes.

## Process

### Phase 1: Identify Weak Rhymes (2 hours)
Audit all 385 rhyme pairs (55 stanzas × 7 pairs):
- Classify: Rich / Exact / Approximate / Weak
- Target: All approximate and weak rhymes for replacement

### Phase 2: Replace Weak Rhymes (6-8 hours)
For each weak rhyme:
1. Try to strengthen without restructuring line
2. If not possible, restructure line
3. Verify new rhyme is rich or exact
4. Re-verify meter after change

**Goal**: 70%+ rich or exact rhymes

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] All rhymes classified
- [ ] Weak rhymes identified
- [ ] Replacements made
- [ ] New rhymes verified (not forbidden)
- [ ] Meter maintained
- [ ] 70%+ rich/exact achieved
