# Task 02: Voice Consistency Audit

**Sprint**: 06 - Chapter 1 Revision
**Scope**: Narrator voice across 55 stanzas
**Time**: 4-5 hours
**Priority**: HIGH

## Objective

Verify narrator voice consistency throughout Chapter 1.

## Audit Process

### Check Against Narrator Persona Profile:
[Narrator Persona Profile](../../planning/narrator/narrator_persona.md)

**Chapter 1 Target**: 60% ironic, 30% sympathetic, 10% philosophical

### For Each Narrator Stanza:
1. Identify dominant tone
2. Check register (high/middle/low) appropriate
3. Verify reader address natural
4. Check against development arc (detached → engaged)

### Track:
- Tone distribution across chapter
- Any voice breaks or inconsistencies
- Reader address frequency and quality

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Voice analyzed for all narrator stanzas
- [ ] Tone balance appropriate
- [ ] Inconsistencies identified
- [ ] Corrections planned
